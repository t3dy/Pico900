## edelheit2022:072 (Amos Edelheit, assertive)

**Restatement:** Edelheit says Pico's thesis 31, that essence and existence in any created being are in fact distinguished, concerns a distinction discussed in Thomas's De ente et essentia, where in God essence and existence are identical while in created intellectual substances existence differs from essence and is limited by the receiving nature.

## edelheit2022:149 (Amos Edelheit, assertive)

**Restatement:** Edelheit says Torni presents what is called the Merton theorem of uniform acceleration to show that two bodies can be in one place, and since no contradiction is found, that God could move a stone from place a to place b while at the same time it is in place b.

> What we have here is in fact a formulation of what is called ‘the Merton theorem of uniform acceleration’. Torni presents this theorem in order to show yet again that two bodies can (and in fact must) be in one place. Since no contradiction can be found in this thesis it is in God’s power to move a stone from place a. to place b. while at the same time the stone a. is in place b.
> (edelheit2022:17663)
