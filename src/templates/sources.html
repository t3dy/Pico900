Pico's Intellectual Sources

The complete library of works, authors, and traditions that shaped Giovanni Pico della Mirandola's philosophical writings. Explore 87 major sources across eight traditions: Scholastic, Platonic, Aristotelian, Islamic, Kabbalistic, Patristic, Hermetic, and Scientific.

About These Sources

Organization: Sources are organized by philosophical tradition, not chronologically. This reflects how Pico synthesized different intellectual lineages.

Priority Levels: CORE sources are foundational to Pico's thought; MAJOR sources significantly influenced his work; SECONDARY sources provided supplementary ideas or represent later developments in traditions Pico engaged.

Conclusions Linked: The number of 900 Conclusions that reference or engage with each source.

Scholarship: Each source entry cites the modern scholars (Copenhaver, Wirszubski, Howlett, etc.) who have written on Pico's engagement with that source.
