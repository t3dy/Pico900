What the scholars say: verified claims as cards

1886 claims from 15 works on Pico's angelology and the One. Every card is a claim whose quotation was re-found in its source by a script; restatements are model paraphrases, sampled by a second reader. Scholarly quotations are shown, cut to 40 words, only for the 290 claims cited in the essays; every claim carries its work and line so it can be checked in the book.

Scores are triage instruments with published weights (docs/CLAIMS_MODEL.md), not measures of truth.
