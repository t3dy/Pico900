Pseudo-Dionysius the Areopagite

Celestial Hierarchy, Divine Names, Mystical Theology

c. 500 CE (authorship disputed)

Conclusions Linked

Priority
CORE
Related Scholars

Pseudo-Dionysius's account of nine orders of angels and their hierarchical procession from God shaped medieval angelology profoundly. Pico adapted the Dionysian hierarchy into his metaphysical system and integrated it with Kabbalah and Plotinian emanation. Essential for all angelology conclusions.

Relevance to Pico's Thought

  • Angelic hierarchy
  • divine names
  • mystical ascent
  • light
  • procession
