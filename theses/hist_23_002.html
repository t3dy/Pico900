Doctrine

Farmer's note glosses 'intellectual hierarchy' as one of the henadic orders in Proclus's system, and records a scholarly disagreement over the origin of these Procline henads: Saffrey and Westerink trace them to Proclus's teacher Syrianus, while Dodds and Dillon trace the concept earlier, Dillon finding evidence for the henads already in Iamblichus, the authority Pico cites here (farmer1998:15720).
