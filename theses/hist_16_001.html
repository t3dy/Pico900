Latin

Diffinitio de anima data ab Aristotele in qua dicitur: Anima est actus corporis, cum de rationali, de qua principaliter datur, intelligitur, accipienda est causaliter non formaliter.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14474

English

Aristotle's definition of the soul — that the soul is the actuality of the body — when it is understood of the rational soul, for which it is chiefly given, must be taken causally, not formally. unverified draft
