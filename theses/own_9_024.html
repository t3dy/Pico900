Thesis 9>24

Latin

Ex secretioris philosophiae principiis necesse est confiteri plus posse caracteres et figuras in opere Magico, quam possit quaecunque qualitas materialis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25470

English

From the principles of the more secret philosophy it is necessary to confess that characters and figures can do more in magical work than any material quality whatever can. unverified draft

'caracteres' and 'figurae' (characters and figures) mean, per Farmer, words and numbers, not figures inscribed on astrological talismans, as Frances Yates argued (farmer1998:25497).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-swarm-batch1, from Latin at farmer1998:25470

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (farmer1998:25470). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

Farmer cites Pico's own gloss in the Apology (Opera, 172, 175) that 'characters' and 'figures' refer to words and numbers, against Yates's reading of them as figures on astrological talismans (farmer1998:25497). The 'more secret philosophy' refers to Pythagorean mathematics, as also suggested in the neighboring thesis, and Pico's association of Pythagorean 'formal numbers' with the creative powers of the intellectual nature, superior to the 'material qualities' of Aristotelian physics, is further suggested at 3>25-26 (farmer1998:25497). Farmer notes that Thomas Aquinas and the authors of the Malleus Maleficarum, published shortly before Pico's text, explicitly associated magic using characters and figures with demonic magic, of which Pico was surely aware (farmer1998:25497).

Context

Farmer cross-references the thesis to 3>25, on the impossibility of understanding numbered ideas without destroying intellectual nature (farmer1998:20643).

Historiography

Akopyan quotes the thesis in Latin alongside its neighbor 9>25 while discussing formal number in Pico's magical theses (akopyan2018:616; akopyan2021:1345). Busi's edition and Italian commentary situate the thesis, number 24 among the magical conclusions, as the immediate predecessor of the thesis on characters and Cabalistic numbers (busi2014:1618; busi2014:9635).
