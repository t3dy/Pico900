Latin

Illius naturae quae est orizon temporis et aeternitatis propria est magia, inde est petenda per modos debitos, notos sapientibus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25358

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 per modos debitos notus

1487 reads 'notus' for 'notos' (farmer1998:25385).

English

That nature which forms the horizon of time and eternity has magic as its own; it is to be sought from it by the fitting means, known to the wise. unverified draft
