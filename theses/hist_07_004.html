Averroes · thesis 7.4

Thesis 7.4

Latin

Possibile est tenendo unitatem intellectus, animam meam, ita particulariter meam ut non sit mihi communis cum omnibus, remanere post mortem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12947

English

Granting the intellect's unity, my soul could nonetheless survive death — mine so exclusively that no other shares it with me.
