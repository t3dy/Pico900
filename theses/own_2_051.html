Latin

Necessarium est dicere secundum Auenroem quod substantia est de intrinseca quiditate accidentis, et est opinio et Aristoteli et philosophiae maxime consona.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 19670

English

It is necessary to say, following Averroes, that substance belongs to the intrinsic quiddity of an accident, and this is an opinion in the fullest harmony with both Aristotle and philosophy. unverified draft

'quiditas' kept as the technical term quiddity.
