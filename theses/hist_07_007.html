Latin

Deus primum mobile non solum ut finis, sed ut uerum efliciens et proprius motor mouet.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13033

Farmer's transcription reads 'efliciens' for 'efficiens' (farmer1998:13033).

English

God moves the first mobile heaven not only as its final cause, but as its true efficient cause and proper mover. unverified draft
