Latin

Ex praecedenti conclusione intelligi potest cur dixerit Paulus / datum esse Iesu nomen quod est super omne nomen, et cur in nomine lesu dictum sit: Omne genu flecti caelestium, terrestrium, et infernorum, quod etiam est maxime cabalisticum; et potest ex se intelligere qui est profundus in cabala.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 26500; 1486 print folio <31r/31v>

English

From the preceding conclusion one can understand why Paul said that the name given to Jesus is above every name, and why it is said in the name of Jesus that every knee should bow, of those in heaven, on earth, and under the earth — which is also supremely Kabbalistic; and whoever is versed in the Cabala can understand this for himself. unverified draft

'ex se intelligere' is rendered 'understand this for himself', that is, without need of further exposition, for one already competent in the Cabala (farmer1998:26500).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch18, from Latin at farmer1998:26500

Attribution: Invokes Paul on the name of Jesus (farmer1998:26500). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

The thesis holds that the preceding Kabbalistic conclusion explains Paul's statement that Jesus was given 'the name above every name' and the further scriptural statement that every knee should bow to that name, and asserts that this is itself 'supremely Kabbalistic' (farmer1998:26500).

unverified draft

Context

The thesis belongs to the seventy-two own-opinion Cabalistic conclusions 'confirming the Christian religion' (farmer1998:26227).

Reception

The study of Pico's Kabbalistic paradigm quotes the thesis's Latin text in full, alongside its neighboring conclusion, in a discussion of the name of Jesus interpreted 'according to the mode and principles of the Cabala' (kabbalah_paradigma:1942).
