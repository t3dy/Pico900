Latin

Si ponamus deum cognoscere creaturas ut obiectum secunda/rium suae intuitionis, ut communiter ponitur, dico quod pater prius producit uerbum quam creaturas cognoscat.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21549; 1486 print folio <21r/21v>

The slash in Farmer's transcription marks a line break inside the word 'secundarium'; reproduced verbatim (farmer1998:21549).

English

If we suppose that God knows creatures as the secondary object of his intuition, as is commonly held, I say that the Father produces the Word before he knows creatures. unverified draft
