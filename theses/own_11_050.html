Cabalistic, confirming the Christian religion · thesis 11>50

Thesis 11>50

Latin

Cum dicunt cabalistae a septima et octaua petendos filios, ita dicas in merchiaua inferiori accipi, ut ab una petatur ut det, ab altera ne prohibeat. Et quae det et quae prohibeat potest intelligere ex praecedentibus conclusionibus qui fuerit intelligens in astrologia et cabala.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27439

English

When the Cabalists say that children are to be sought from the seventh and the eighth, take this, within the lower merkavah, to mean: one is petitioned to grant them, the other only asked not to stand in the way. Which one grants and which withholds, anyone versed in both astrology and the Cabala can work out from the conclusions already given.

'merchiaua' transliterates merkavah, the Divine Chariot; Farmer's note holds the 'inferior chariot' names the realm of sensible natures rather than the seventh and eighth sefirot directly (farmer1998:27453).
