Latin

Ideo dixit Socrates in Cratylo se somniare circa ideas, quia ideis non utimur in hoc statu, sed earum imaginibus proximis uel secundariis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23097

English

Socrates said in the Cratylus that he was dreaming about the Ideas for this reason: that in this state we do not make use of the Ideas themselves, but of their proximate or secondary images. unverified draft
