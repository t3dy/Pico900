Plato · thesis 5>54

Thesis 5>54

Latin

Ideo dixit Socrates in Cratylo se somniare circa ideas, quia ideis non utimur in hoc statu, sed earum imaginibus proximis uel secundariis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23097

English

Socrates says in the Cratylus that he only dreams about the ideas, and the reason is this: in our present state we work not with the ideas themselves but with their nearest, or secondary, images.
