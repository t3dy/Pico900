Thesis 10>13

Latin

Idem est Typhon apud Orpheum, et Zamael in Cabala.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25884

English

What Orpheus names Typhon and the Cabala names Samael are one.

Sense checked against farmer1998:25943; no divergence. 'Zamael' given as the standard English 'Samael' per Farmer's note identifying the figure with Satan (farmer1998:25919).
