Plotinus · thesis 20.4

Thesis 20.4

Latin

Anima quae peccauit uel in terreno uel in aereo corpore post mortem bruti uitam uiuit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15094

English

The soul that has sinned, whether in an earthly or an aerial body, lives the life of a beast after death.
