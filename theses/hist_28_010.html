Latin

Rectius dicitur quod paradisus sit totum aedificium quam quod sit decima, et in medio eius est collocatus magnus Adam, qui est Tipheret.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17728

English

It is more correctly said that paradise is the whole edifice than that it is the tenth [sefirah], and in its midst is placed the great Adam, who is Tiferet. unverified draft

'decima' refers to the tenth of the ten sefirot; 'Tipheret' (Tiferet, 'Beauty'/'Glory') is the sixth sefirah, glossed by Wirszubski: "Adam means homo, and Tiferet (Glory or Beauty) is the sixth sefirah" (wirszubski1989:9702).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-swarm-batch1, from Latin at farmer1998:17728

Attribution: Reports Hebrew Cabalists (farmer1998:17728). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer glosses 'paradise'/'the whole edifice' as referring to the entire sefirotic system, and 'Tiferet' as the sixth sefirah, described in one of several medieval schemes as the 'middle' sefirah (farmer1998:17752). Wirszubski's summary gloss states plainly that "Adam means homo, and Tiferet (Glory or Beauty) is the sixth sefirah" (wirszubski1989:9702).

unverified draft

Context

The thesis is the tenth of Pico's conclusions reporting the secret doctrine of the wise Hebrew Cabalists, and Wirszubski cross-references it to Menahem Recanati's commentary, folios 17va-18ra (wirszubski1989:1720).

unverified draft

Historiography

Busi's edition transcribes the thesis among the conclusions grouped under 'Paradisus, Empyreum' (busi2014:24874). Wirszubski identifies the thesis as the tenth conclusio of the Hebrew Cabalists' series and supplies Recanati as its parallel source (wirszubski1989:1717; wirszubski1989:1720).
