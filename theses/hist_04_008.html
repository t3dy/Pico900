Latin

Ens de suis passionibus et ultimis differentiis quiditatiue non praedicatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12243

English

Being is not predicated quidditatively of its own attributes and ultimate differences. unverified draft

Reflects the transcendental, wholly undetermined character Scotus assigned to the concept of being (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12275). Sense checked against farmer1998:12304.
