Scotus · thesis 4.6

Thesis 4.6

Latin

Vnumquodque indiuiduum est indiuiduum per propriam differentiam indiuidualem, quae dicitur haecceitas.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12236

OCR 'hecheitas' corrected to 'haecceitas'.

English

Every individual is individuated by its own individuating difference, termed its haecceity.

'haecceity' = the 'thisness' of a thing, a formal distinction differentiating each created individual from all others (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12269). Sense checked against farmer1998:12296.
