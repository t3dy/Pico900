Scotus · thesis 4.19

Thesis 4.19

Latin

Actus intelligendi ab obiecto et intellectu tanquam duobus agentibus partialibus, ut quod causatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12371

The closing clause 'ut quod causatur' is elliptical in the source as transmitted; no fuller reading is supplied by the apparatus consulted.

English

The act of understanding, as an effect, is caused by the object and the intellect together, each as a partial cause.

Sense checked against farmer1998:12435; the elliptical closing clause is rendered by supplying 'as an effect' for the sense, per Farmer's own reading at the same locator.
