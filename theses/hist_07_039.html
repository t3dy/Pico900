Averroes · thesis 7.39

Thesis 7.39

Latin

Essentia cuiuslibet intelligentiae est substantialiter ad aliquid.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13498

English

The essence of any intelligence stands, substantially, in relation to something else.

'ad aliquid' is the Aristotelian category of relation; cf. farmer1998:13516 note on 'relations'.
