Latin

Essentia cuiuslibet intelligentiae est substantialiter ad aliquid.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13498

English

The essence of each intelligence is substantially relative, that is, constituted toward something. unverified draft

'Ad aliquid' is the Aristotelian category of relation; the thesis holds an intelligence's essence to consist in a relation, not an absolute nature (farmer1998:13498).
