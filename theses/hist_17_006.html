Thesis 17.6

Latin

Sciri potest ex praecedentibus conclusionibus quare intellectus agens quandoque arti, quandoque habitui, quandoque lumini, assimilatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14566

English

From the preceding conclusions one can understand why the agent intellect is likened, at times, to an art, at times to a habit, and at times to light.
