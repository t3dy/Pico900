Latin

Ad complementum praecedentis conclusionis, quam non solum Adelandus, sed omnes Mauri dicunt, dico ego illas species actu et substantialiter esse in parte quae non cadit, et recipi de nouo et accidentaliter in parte quae cadit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15287

English

To complete the preceding conclusion, which not only Adelandus but all the Moors affirm, I say that those forms exist actually and substantially in the part [of the soul] that does not fall, and are received anew and accidentally in the part that falls. unverified draft

'Adelandus' (farmer1998:15287) is left as a proper name; the source Pico had in mind is not identified here.
