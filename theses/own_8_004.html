Latin

Ibidem per radices terrae nihil aliud intelligere possunt quam uitam uegetalem, conuenienter ad dicta Empedoclis, qui ponit transanimationem etiam in plantas.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24832

English

In the same place, by 'the roots of the earth' they can understand nothing other than vegetal life, in agreement with the sayings of Empedocles, who posits transanimation even into plants. unverified draft

'transanimationem' is rendered 'transanimation' rather than 'transmigration', preserving Pico's own term for soul-transfer into plants, at farmer1998:24832.
