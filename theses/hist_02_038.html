Thomas Aquinas · thesis 2.38

Thesis 2.38

Latin

Grauia potius seipsis, quam a seipsis mouentur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11784

English

Heavy bodies are moved rather in virtue of themselves than strictly by themselves.

The Latin distinguishes 'seipsis' (ablative, 'in virtue of/with themselves') from 'a seipsis' (agent, 'by themselves'). Sense checked against farmer1998:11845.
