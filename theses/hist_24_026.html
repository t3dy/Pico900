Proclus · thesis 24.26

Thesis 24.26

Latin

Iupiter, Neptunnus, et Pluto, Saturni regnum partientes, a Saturno Regnum non accipiunt nisi per medium conditoris Iouis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16371

English

Jupiter, Neptune and Pluto, in dividing up the realm of Saturn, take that realm from Saturn only by way of Jove the founder, who stands between.

'Regnum' capitalised mid-sentence in the print (farmer1998:16372); rendered without capital. 'conditoris' rendered 'founder' as in 24.23. Sense checked against farmer1998:16423.
