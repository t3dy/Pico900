Historiography

Wirszubski transcribes the thesis in full as his 'Conclusio XXXV' and identifies its direct source as Recanati, fol 50ra (wirszubski1989:2665, 2668). Busi and Ebgi likewise quote the thesis in Latin with an Italian translation ('Nessuna realta spirituale, che scende verso il basso, opera senza indumento'), citing Corazzol's edition of Recanati's Commentary on the Daily Prayers as the source Menahem Recanati passage (busi2014:15714); this quotation could not be independently re-located in the copy of Busi and Ebgi consulted for this entry, which appears to carry only the volume's introductory essay and indices.
