Latin

Ista propositio non est concedenda: essentia est pater fihi.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12492

Apparatus (Farmer): colon retained from 1486 edition

Farmer retains the colon after 'concedenda' from the 1486 edition (farmer1998:12515); 'fihi' is Farmer's transcription, evidently for 'filii' ('of the son').

English

This proposition is not to be granted: the essence is the father of the son. unverified draft

Edelheit paraphrases the thesis's target proposition as 'the Father is the essence of the Son', inverting the grammatical subject and predicate of the Latin 'essentia est pater filii' as transcribed at farmer1998:12492 (edelheit2022:15435).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch7, from Latin at farmer1998:12492

Attribution: Reports Henry of Ghent (farmer1998:12492; edelheit2022:15435). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Edelheit glosses the thesis's target proposition as denying that 'the Father is the essence of the Son', and states that this requires first considering the nature of the relations among the Persons in the Trinity (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15435). He reports that Henry maintains, in article 51, question 1, of his Summa, that there are absolutely no differences between the Persons in the respect under discussion, though the excerpt consulted breaks off at this point (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15435).
