Thesis 7a>42

Latin

Vtrum in angelo sit aliud quam intelligens, intellectio, et intellectum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24270

English

Whether there is anything in the angel beyond the one understanding, the act of understanding, and the thing understood.

Sense checked against Farmer's English at farmer1998:24326; the three terms are unpacked as 'the one understanding'/'the act of understanding'/'the thing understood' rather than Farmer's 'intellect, intellection, and that intellected'.
