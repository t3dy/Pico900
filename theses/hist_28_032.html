Latin

Ideo circumcisio fit octaua die, quia est superior quam sponsa uniuersalizata.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18179

English

Circumcision therefore takes place on the eighth day, because [that day] is superior to the universalized bride. unverified draft

'octaua die' ('eighth day'), following Wirszubski's (1989: 45) reading, refers to the ninth sefirah; 'sponsa uniuersalizata' ('universalized bride') symbolizes the tenth sefirah (farmer1998:18213).

Attribution: Reports Hebrew Cabalists (farmer1998:18179). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer glosses 'eighth day,' following Wirszubski's (1989: 45) reading, as the ninth sefirah, and 'universalized bride' as a symbol of the tenth sefirah (farmer1998:18213).

unverified draft

Context

Farmer's cross-reference network links the thesis to 11>35, on how nothing is understood to proceed from God considered in himself, as infinite and one, but only separation from things and his enclosure within himself (farmer1998:27129); 26.5, on the intelligible order being above every intellectual hierarchy, as Amosis the Egyptian said (farmer1998:17143); 28.47, on the sefirot's influx expressed through AMEN (farmer1998:18410); 2>80, on Hebrew as the first language (farmer1998:20213); 3>55, on the order of the Hebrew language (farmer1998:21095); and 9>22, on the magical efficacy of Hebrew names alone (farmer1998:25378).

Historiography

Wirszubski transcribes the thesis in full as his 'Conclusio XXXII' and gives Recanati, fol 47vb, as its source (wirszubski1989:2573, 2576).
