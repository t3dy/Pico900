Per sex dies geneseos habemus intelligere sex extremitates aedificii procedentes a Bresith, sicut procedunt cedri a Libano.

By the six days of Genesis we are to understand the six extremities of the edifice proceeding from Bereshit, just as cedars proceed from Lebanon. unverified draft

Doctrine

The thesis reads the six days of creation as the six lower sefirot, called the 'edifice', proceeding from 'Bereshit', a term Pico elsewhere treats as a symbol for the second sefirah, Hokhmah, itself read in his second, Christianizing set of Cabalistic conclusions as a figure of Christ, the Wisdom of the Father (farmer1998:17712). Farmer glosses the 'cedars of Lebanon' as the sefirot from Hesed to Yesod, and cross-references Pico's own numbering of the sefirot at thesis 11>48 (farmer1998:17760, 17766).

Historiography

Busi and Ebgi print the Latin and gloss the cedars of Lebanon as the six sefirot from Hesed to Yesod, citing Recanati's commentary on Genesis (busi2014:15767). Wirszubski quotes the same Latin as the ninth conclusion of the Cabalistic set and compares it to Recanati (wirszubski1989:1675, 1678).
