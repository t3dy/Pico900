Hebrew Cabalists · thesis 28.9

Thesis 28.9

Latin

Per sex dies geneseos habemus intelligere sex extremitates aedificii procedentes a Bresith, sicut procedunt cedri a Libano.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17649

English

The six days of Genesis stand for the six extremities of the edifice that issue from Bereshit, the way cedars issue from Lebanon.

'Bresith' left transliterated as in Pico's Latin (Hebrew bereshit, 'in the beginning'); reworded to avoid near-identity with the published English; sense checked against farmer1998:17699.
