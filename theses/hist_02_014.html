Doctrine

The thesis distinguishes two modes of Christ's bodily presence: local presence in heaven and sacramental presence on the altar (edelheit2022:10872). Edelheit locates the underlying question in Thomas's Summa theologiae 3, question 75, article 1, on the mode of Christ's presence in the sacrament (edelheit2022:10872).

Context

Farmer's note pairs 2.13-14 as sacrament theses and records that 2.14 is discussed at length in Pico's Apology, in the Opera edition at 183ff (farmer1998:11507).

Reception

Farmer records that the thesis received extended discussion in Pico's own Apology (Opera, 183ff), though the content of that discussion is not given in the sources consulted here (farmer1998:11507).

Historiography

Edelheit is the only scholar found discussing this thesis in the sources consulted; he ties Pico's formulation directly to the question of Christ's sacramental presence as Thomas frames it in Summa theologiae 3, question 75, article 1 (edelheit2022:10872).
