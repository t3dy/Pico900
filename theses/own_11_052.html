Thesis 11>52

Latin

Ex praecedenti conclusione intelligi potest cur euangelista Matheus, in quatuordecim illis generationibus ante Christum, quasdam dimiserit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27520

English

The previous conclusion explains why Matthew the evangelist left certain persons out of the fourteen generations before Christ that he lists.

Matt. 1:1-17 divides the lineage from Abraham to Christ into three sets of fourteen generations; the persons omitted from the last set are, on Farmer's note, those with a 'diminished correspondence' to Christ like Zedekiah's in 11>51 (farmer1998:27550).
