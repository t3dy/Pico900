Latin

Aeuum est subiectiue in angelo beatiori.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11534

English

Aevum exists subjectively in the more blessed angel. unverified draft

'Aevum' names a durational mode intermediate between time and eternity; Farmer's note glosses 'exists subjectively' as 'exists in a metaphysical substance' and states the concept had syncretic origins (farmer1998:11569).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch3, from Latin at farmer1998:11534

Attribution: Reports Thomas Aquinas (farmer1998:11534). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

The thesis holds that aevum, as a mode of duration, exists subjectively (i.e., inheres in a substance) more fully in the more blessed angel (edelheit2022:11147; farmer1998:11569). Edelheit locates the relevant discussion in Thomas's commentary on the Sentences, book 2, distinction 2, question 1, article 2, on whether aevum is only one, and judges Pico's thesis 'accurate but perhaps not explicit enough' (edelheit2022:11147).

unverified draft

Context

Farmer's note observes that the concept of aevum was sometimes expanded into a larger hierarchical series of durational modes, without further detail given in the excerpt consulted (farmer1998:11569).

unverified draft

Historiography

Edelheit is the only scholar found discussing this thesis in the sources consulted; he treats Pico's formulation as substantively correct relative to Thomas's own discussion of aevum, while noting it compresses Thomas's argument (edelheit2022:11147).
