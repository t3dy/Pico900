Latin

De materia ut ex ea per se fiunt res tractare methaphysicum, physicum autem ut ex ea res fiunt per accidens, habemus dicere secundum doctrinam Aristotelis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 19878

English

We must say, following Aristotle's teaching, that it belongs to the metaphysician to treat matter as that from which things come to be in themselves, and to the physicist to treat it as that from which things come to be accidentally. unverified draft
