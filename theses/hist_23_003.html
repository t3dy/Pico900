Latin

Corporalis natura est in intellectu immobiliter, in animo ex se primo mobiliter, in animali ex se mobiliter participatiue, in coelo aliunde ordinate mobiliter, infra lunam aliunde mobiliter inordinate.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15646

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 aliunde ordinate: | Emendationes errorum, corrige: aliunde ordinate mobiliter |

English

Corporeal nature exists in the intellect immovably; in the first soul, movably through itself; in the animal soul, movably through itself and by participation; in the heavens, movably through another and in an ordered way; below the moon, movably through another and in a disordered way. unverified draft
