Latin

Christus in ultimo iudicio iudicabit non solum in natura humana, sed etiam secundum naturam humanam.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11525

English

In the Last Judgment, Christ will judge not only in his human nature, but also according to his human nature. unverified draft

The Latin as transmitted repeats 'natura humana' / 'naturam humanam' in both disjuncts, where a contrast (e.g. with the divine nature) is expected; Farmer's own English preserves the same repetition without comment (farmer1998:11558), and no apparatus variant is recorded here. Sense checked against farmer1998:11558.
