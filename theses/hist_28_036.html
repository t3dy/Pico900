Latin

Peccatum Soddomae_fuit per truncationem ultimae plantae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18198

Farmer's transcription shows an underscore, 'Soddomae_fuit'; read as two words, 'Soddomae fuit' (farmer1998:18198). Wirszubski's quotation of the same conclusio reads 'Peccatum Soddomae fuit per truncationem ultimae plantae' without the underscore (wirszubski1989:2690).

English

The sin of Sodom occurred through the truncation of the last plant. unverified draft

'truncationem ultimae plantae' is left literal, 'the truncation of the last plant' (farmer1998:18198); the packet consulted does not gloss which sefirah 'the last plant' names, so no symbolic equivalence is supplied here.

Attribution: Reports Hebrew Cabalists (farmer1998:18198). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer's note ties the thesis to Genesis 18:20 and following, the narrative of Sodom's condemnation, and directs the reader to thesis 28.4 for 'the symbolism of the "severing of the shoots"' (paraphrase, farmer1998:18273). Thesis 28.4 itself states, in Pico's Latin, that 'the sin of Adam was a truncation of the kingdom from the other plants' (farmer1998:17544), so Farmer's cross-reference sets Sodom's sin and Adam's sin as parallel instances of a 'truncation' involving 'plants' in Pico's Kabbalistic vocabulary, without his note here specifying what the plants or the truncation represent (farmer1998:18273).
