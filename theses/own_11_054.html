Cabalistic, confirming the Christian religion · thesis 11>54

Thesis 11>54

Latin

Quod dicunt Cabalistae, beatificandos nos in speculo lucente reposito sanctis in futuro saeculo, idem est praecise, sequendo fundamenta eorum, cum eo quod nos dicimus, beatificandos sanctos in filio.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27529

English

What the Cabalists claim, that in the world to come we shall be made blessed in the shining mirror restored to the saints, comes, on their own premises, to exactly what we ourselves say: that the saints will be made blessed in the Son.

The 'shining mirror' is the sixth sefirah, 'the great Adam', one of Pico's regular symbols for Christ, carried over from 11>53 (farmer1998:27560).
