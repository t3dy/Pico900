Thesis 11>54

Latin

Quod dicunt Cabalistae, beatificandos nos in speculo lucente reposito sanctis in futuro saeculo, idem est praecise, sequendo fundamenta eorum, cum eo quod nos dicimus, beatificandos sanctos in filio.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27529

English

What the Cabalists say -- that we are to be beatified in the shining mirror laid up for the saints in the age to come -- is, following their own foundations, precisely the same as what we say: that the saints are to be beatified in the Son. unverified draft

'speculo lucente' (the shining mirror) is glossed as the sixth sefirah, which Pico regularly calls 'the great Adam' and treats as one of his standard symbols for Christ (paraphrase; farmer1998:27560).

Doctrine

Farmer's note explains that the Cabalists' 'shining mirror' and the Christian claim that the saints are beatified in the Son come to the same thing, following the Cabalists' own foundations, because the 'shining mirror' is the sixth sefirah, which Pico regularly identifies with 'the great Adam,' one of his standard symbols for Christ (paraphrase; farmer1998:27560). The thesis is thus an instance of Pico's programme of showing Kabbalah and Christian doctrine to coincide (farmer1998:27529).
