Latin

Quaecunque uel deorum uel naturae operationes duplices sunt, immanentes et transeuntes; per immanentes seipsum unumquodque continetet quae in ipso sunt rationes; per transeuntes ad exteriora se conuertit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16176

English

Whatever operations, whether of the gods or of nature, are twofold: immanent and transient. Through the immanent operation each thing contains itself and the principles that are within it; through the transient operation it turns itself toward external things. unverified draft
