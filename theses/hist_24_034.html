Latin

Duodecim dei de quibus in Phedro dei sunt medii inter supermundanos et mundanos, uinculum illorum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16483

Latin as in the packet with run-together or artefact spellings normalised: 'Phedro_dei' to 'Phedro dei'.

English

The twelve gods of whom the Phaedrus speaks are the intermediate gods between the supermundane and the mundane, the bond of them. unverified draft

Print reads 'Phedro_dei' (farmer1998:16483); underscore treated as an OCR artefact of a space or punctuation mark, so 'dei' after 'Phedro' is read as an emphatic repetition and rendered once. Not established whether the original has a comma or another word here. 'illorum' taken as the two ranks, supermundane and mundane. Sense checked against farmer1998:16542.
