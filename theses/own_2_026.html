Practicum et speculatiuum sunt differentiae accidentales habitus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 19216

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 differentie accidentalis habitus | Emendationes errorum, corrige: differentiae accidentales habitus | 1487 text not emended

1486 reads 'differentie accidentalis habitus' (singular, ungrammatical); the Emendationes errorum corrects to 'differentiae accidentales habitus' (the plural reading given here), which the 1487 reprint left unemended.
