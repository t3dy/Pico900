Latin

Prima substantia prior est qualibet substantia habente habitudinem ad operationem transeuntem, quaecunque sit illa, alicuius causae siue formalis, siue materialis, siue efficientis, siue finalis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13664

English

The first substance is prior to any substance having a relation to transient operation, of whatever kind that relation may be, whether to a formal, material, efficient, or final cause. unverified draft
