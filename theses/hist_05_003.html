Latin

Processiones distinguuntur in diuinis penes intellectum et uoluntatem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12489

English

The processions in the Godhead are distinguished by way of intellect and will. unverified draft

'Penes' is rendered in its scholastic sense 'by way of, in respect of', not its classical sense 'in the possession of' (farmer1998:12489).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch7, from Latin at farmer1998:12489

Attribution: Reports Henry of Ghent (farmer1998:12489; edelheit2022:15338). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Edelheit judges that, beyond changing Henry's 'ratio generandi' to 'principium generandi', the thesis is in accordance with Henry's opinion (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15338). He states that the thesis holds that the processions in the Trinity are conducted by the divine intellect and the divine will, and that traces of this are found in article 54, questions 9 and 10, of Henry's Summa (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15338).
