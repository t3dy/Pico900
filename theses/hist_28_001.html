Doctrine

The thesis sets a priestly analogy between man, the 'lower priest', who offers the souls of irrational animals to God, and the archangel Michael, the 'higher priest', who offers the souls of rational animals (farmer1998:17532). Farmer records that Pico himself points to a mystical reading of this conclusion in thesis 11>11, and that the two texts Wirszubski proposes as its Kabbalistic sources concern the symbolism of the sefirot without addressing that mystical point (farmer1998:17563).

unverified draft

Historiography

A study of Pico's Kabbalistic epistemology quotes the thesis under its full heading, 'Conclusio cabalistica secundum secretam doctrinam sapientium Hebraeorum cabalistarum', and connects it to Abraham Abulafia's De secretis legis and to Archangelus's Interpretationes in dogmata (kabbalah_paradigma:1459). Wirszubski transcribes the same Latin as the first conclusion of Pico's Cabalistic set (wirszubski1989:1246, 1249).
