Doctrine

The thesis locates freedom essentially in reason rather than in will, a claim Edelheit calls 'not an obvious statement' since freedom is usually associated with the will rather than with reason or intellect (edelheit2022:11936).

unverified draft

Context

Edelheit reads the thesis as returning, after the intervening thesis on Aristotelian physics, to the intellect-will debate already raised at thesis 2.19 (edelheit2022:11936).

unverified draft
