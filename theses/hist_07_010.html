Latin

Tres modi per se sunt ad demonstrationem utiles: primus, secundus et quartus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13047

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 utiles.Pnmus.Secundus

English

Three of the per se modes are useful for demonstration: the first, the second, and the fourth. unverified draft

Refers to Aristotle's modes of per se predication in the Posterior Analytics, of which the third is standardly held useless for demonstration (farmer1998:13047).
