Averroes · thesis 7.10

Thesis 7.10

Latin

Tres modi per se sunt ad demonstrationem utiles: primus, secundus et quartus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13047

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 utiles.Pnmus.Secundus

The 1486 apparatus punctuates this as separate sentences, 'utiles. Primus. Secundus' (farmer1998:13060); the sheet's continuous reading is followed here.

English

In demonstration, three of the per se modes have utility: the first, the second, and the fourth.
