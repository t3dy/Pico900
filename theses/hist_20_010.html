Plotinus · thesis 20.10

Thesis 20.10

Latin

In ratione similitudines rerum sunt et species, in intellectu uere ipsa entia.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15116

English

In reason there are the likenesses and images of things, but in the intellect there are the things themselves, in their true being.
