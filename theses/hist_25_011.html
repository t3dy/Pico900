Latin

Iudicium sensus in musica non est adhibendum, sed solius intellectus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17016

English

In music, the judgment of the senses is not to be applied, but that of the intellect alone. unverified draft

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch9, from Latin at farmer1998:17016

Attribution: Reports Pythagorean mathematics (farmer1998:17016). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer's note reads the thesis as reflecting the view that the study of musical proportion prepares the soul for higher things, comparing Plato's Republic 7 (farmer1998:17048).

Historiography

Busi and Ebgi (2014) quote the thesis in their annotated edition and gloss it in their own Italian translation as 'In musica non va usato il giudizio dei sensi ma solo quello dell'intelletto', noting its proximity to a further thesis (10>2) on the efficacy of Orphic hymns in natural magic (busi2014:21386).
