Thesis 28.40

Latin

Quotienscunque ignoramus proprietatem a qua est influxus super petitione quam petimus, ad dominum naris recurrendum est.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18298

English

When we cannot identify the property from which the influence over a petition flows, we must have recourse to the lord of the nose.
