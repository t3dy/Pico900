Latin

Diffinitio naturae coelestia comprehendit, et ly et copulatiue tenetur et non disiunctiue.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20104

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 & ly : & copulatiue tenetur | 1487 et li. et copulative tenetur

1486/1487 apparatus: 1486 & ly : & copulatiue tenetur | 1487 et li. et copulative tenetur (farmer1998:20120).

English

The definition of nature comprehends celestial things as well, and the 'and' there is taken conjunctively, not disjunctively. unverified draft

'ly'/'li' (the sheet's apparatus preserves both readings, farmer1998:20120) is the scholastic marker for a term taken materially, naming the word itself; left implicit in the translation.
