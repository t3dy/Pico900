Thesis 5>29

Latin

Non est credendum in doctrina Platonis animam quicquam intelligere per inspectionem ad ideas, nisi cum peruenit ad illum statum qui est supremus gradus contemplatiuae perfectionis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22660

English

It must not be believed that, on Plato's teaching, the soul understands anything by looking directly to the ideas, except when it has arrived at that state which is the highest grade of contemplative perfection.

A corollary follows this thesis in the source, beginning 'Errant qui credunt...' (farmer1998:22665), that is not given in the sheet's Latin for this entry and is left untranslated here. Main clause sense checked against farmer1998:22716.
