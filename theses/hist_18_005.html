Alexander of Aphrodisias · thesis 18.5

Thesis 18.5

Latin

Numerus abstractorum de quo agit Aristoteles in duodecimo Methaphysicae, non est numerus motorum, sed numerus intelligentiarum, quae sunt fines motus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14731

English

Aristotle's twelfth book of the Metaphysics counts a certain number of separate substances; that count, for Pico, gives not the number of celestial movers but the number of intelligences, the ends toward which motion tends.
