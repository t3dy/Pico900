Latin

Numerus abstractorum de quo agit Aristoteles in duodecimo Methaphysicae, non est numerus motorum, sed numerus intelligentiarum, quae sunt fines motus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14731

English

The number of abstract things treated by Aristotle in the twelfth book of the Metaphysics is not the number of movers, but the number of intelligences, which are the ends of motion. unverified draft
