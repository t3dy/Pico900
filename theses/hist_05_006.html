Doctrine

Farmer's note states that Pico discusses this issue in the Apologia (paraphrase, farmer1998:12578). Edelheit treats theses 6 and 7 together as angelology, reporting that thesis 6 holds that operations of angels are measured by definite time, with traces of the doctrine at Henry's Quodlibet II, question 9, a text he notes Pico knew well from the discussion of the Apology elsewhere in his book (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15499).
