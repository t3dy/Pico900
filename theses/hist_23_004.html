Iamblichus · thesis 23.4

Thesis 23.4

Latin

Elementa in octo coeli corporibus coelesti modo bis inueniuntur, quae quis inueniet si retrogrado ordine in illa bina numeratione processerit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15651

English

The elements are found twice over, in a celestial mode, in the eight heavenly bodies — as anyone will discover who proceeds in reverse order through that twofold enumeration.

Sense checked against farmer1998:15712; Farmer's note at farmer1998:15731 identifies 'illa numeratione' as a reference to Binah, the kabbalistic sefirah, not present in Iamblichus's own text.
