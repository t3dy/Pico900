Plato · thesis 5>3

Thesis 5>3

Latin

Qui nouerit modum illuminationis superiorum super media intelliget idem significare et Platonicos per congregationem animarum in Monte Ida, et hebreos per animarum congregationem in Monte Synao in auditione legis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22219

English

Whoever has grasped the manner in which higher things shed light on middle things will understand that the Platonists mean the same thing by the gathering of souls on Mount Ida as the Hebrews mean by the gathering of souls on Mount Sinai to hear the Law.
