Latin

Ideo Ioseph ossibus tantum sepultus est et non corpore, quia eius ossa erant uirtutes et militie arboris superioris uocati Sadich influentis ad terram superiorem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18305

English

Joseph's bones alone, not his body, were buried, because those bones were the powers and hosts of the higher tree named Tzaddik, whose influence flows into the higher earth. unverified draft
