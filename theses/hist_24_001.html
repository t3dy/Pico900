Thesis 24.1

Latin

Quod est in intelligibilibus terminus et infinitum, est in intellectualibus mas et femina, in supermundanis idemtitas et alteritas, similitudo et dissimilitudo. In anima circulatio eiusdem et circulatio alterius.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15843

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 terminis

English

What is limit and the unlimited among the intelligibles is male and female among the intellectual beings, and identity and otherness, likeness and unlikeness, among the supermundane beings. In the soul, [these correspond to] the circuit of the same and the circuit of the other. unverified draft
