Latin

Qui crediderit intentionem dici de omni esse aliam ab ea quam dixit praecedens conclusio defendere non potest Aristotelem a Theophrasto, quod ex maiori necessaria et minori inuenta sequatur conclusio necessaria.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13876

English

Whoever believes that the sense of 'predicated of all' is other than what the preceding conclusion stated cannot defend Aristotle against Theophrastus, that a necessary conclusion follows from a necessary major premise and a minor premise that is merely found true. unverified draft

Refers to the ancient dispute, reported for Theophrastus, over whether a necessary conclusion can follow a syllogism with an assertoric (non-modal) minor premise (farmer1998:13876).
