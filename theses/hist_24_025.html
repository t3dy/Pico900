Latin

Quadruplex est fabrica: prima uniuersalia uniuersalium ex toto perornat; secunda uniuersa quidem sed particulariter; tertia secundum diuisa secundum uniuersalitatem; quarta partes particulariter contexit uniuersalibus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16292

Apparatus (Farmer): colon retained from 1486 edition

English

Fabrication is fourfold: the first adorns, as a whole, the universals of universals; the second adorns universal things too, but in a particular way; the third adorns divided things, but according to universality; the fourth weaves particular parts together with universals in a particular way. unverified draft
