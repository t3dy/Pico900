Latin

Opinio Cratyli de nominibus ita est intelligenda: non quod talia sint nomina, sed quod talia esse debent si sint recta.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23093

Apparatus (Farmer): colon retained from 1486 edition

English

Cratylus's opinion about names is to be understood in this way: not that names actually are such, but that they ought to be such, if they are to be correct. unverified draft

Cratylus's claim is read as normative rather than descriptive, per the Cratylus's dispute on the correctness of names, at farmer1998:23093.
