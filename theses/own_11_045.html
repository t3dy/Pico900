Latin

Scitur in cabala apertissime cur dei filius cum aqua baptismi uenerit, et spiritus sanctus cum igne.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27335

English

It is known most clearly in the Kabbalah why the Son of God came with the water of baptism, and the Holy Spirit with fire. unverified draft

'aqua'/'igne' (water/fire) are, per the note shared with the companion thesis 11>44, standard kabbalistic associations of water with the fourth sefirah and fire with the fifth (paraphrase; farmer1998:27363).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch20, from Latin at farmer1998:27335

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (section 11>, Cabalistic conclusions confirming the Christian religion by his own opinion) (farmer1998:27335). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

Farmer's note, covering theses 11>44-45 together, glosses 'water' as associated with the fourth sefirah, God's love or piety, and 'fire' with the fifth, God's judgment or power (paraphrase; farmer1998:27363). Thesis 11>45 argues that this pairing explains, from within the Kabbalah, why the Son of God came with the water of baptism and the Holy Spirit with fire (farmer1998:27335).
