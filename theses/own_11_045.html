Thesis 11>45

Latin

Scitur in cabala apertissime cur dei filius cum aqua baptismi uenerit, et spiritus sanctus cum igne.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27335

English

The Cabala makes it perfectly plain why the Son of God arrived with the waters of baptism, and the Holy Spirit with fire.

Continues the water/fourth-sefirah, fire/fifth-sefirah pairing Farmer notes at 11>44 (farmer1998:27363).
