Doctrine

The thesis states that the effect of the preceding Orphic hymns depends on the work of the Cabala, whose special province is to operate upon every kind of quantity, both continuous and discrete, as Wirszubski's complete quotation of the sentence confirms (wirszubski1989:7196). Wirszubski cites the thesis, calling it 'the twenty-first Orphic thesis', as evidence for 'the proper or specific function of Kabbala' among Pico's Orphic-Kabbalistic conclusions (wirszubski1989:7196).
