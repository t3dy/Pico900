Latin

Vtrum recte multitudo scibilium ad decem praedicamentorum / numerum sit reducta.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24144; 1486 print folio <26v/27r>

English

Whether the multitude of knowable things has rightly been reduced to the number of the ten categories. unverified draft

'praedicamenta' = the Aristotelian categories, or predicaments (farmer1998:24144); the source text breaks the word across a line ('praedicamentorum / numerum', farmer1998:24144), reproduced here as given.
