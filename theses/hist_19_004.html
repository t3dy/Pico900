Latin

Praeter duas species demonstrationis quia quas ponit Aristoteles, tertia alia ponenda est, et est cum una proprietas per coeuam proprietatem demonstratur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14893

English

Besides the two species of demonstration 'that' [quia] which Aristotle posits, a third must be posited, namely when one property is demonstrated through a coeval property. unverified draft

'demonstratio quia' (demonstration of the fact, as opposed to 'propter quid', demonstration of the reasoned fact) is Aristotle's term from Posterior Analytics 1.13 (farmer1998:14893).
