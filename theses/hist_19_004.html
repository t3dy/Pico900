Latin

Praeter duas species demonstrationis quia quas ponit Aristoteles, tertia alia ponenda est, et est cum una proprietas per coeuam proprietatem demonstratur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14893

English

Besides the two kinds of demonstration from effect that Aristotle posits, a third must be added, which occurs when one property is demonstrated through a coeval property. unverified draft

Sense checked against farmer1998:14948; 'coeuam proprietatem' rendered 'coeval property', which Farmer's note (farmer1998:14966) glosses as a 'convertible property'.
