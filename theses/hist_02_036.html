Thomas Aquinas · thesis 2.36

Thesis 2.36

Latin

Duo accidentia solo numero differentia non sunt in eodem subiecto.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11777

OCR 'accıdentia' corrected to 'accidentia'.

English

No single subject can bear two accidents that differ from each other only numerically.
