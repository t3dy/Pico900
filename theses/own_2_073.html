Latin

Tenendo opinionem de anima intellectiua quam tenet Commentator, uidetur mihi rationabiliter tenendum illam animam nullius accidentis esse subiectum; et positionem hanc tanquam ueram defendam, quanquam utrum hoc ille tenuerit, ego non definio.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20093

English

Holding the opinion on the intellective soul that the Commentator holds, it seems to me reasonable to maintain that this soul is the subject of no accident; and I will defend this position as true, although I do not determine whether he himself held it. unverified draft
