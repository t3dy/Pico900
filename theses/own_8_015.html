Zoroaster and Chaldeans · thesis 8>15

Thesis 8>15

Latin

Quid sit intelligendum per capras apud Zoroastrem intelliget qui legerit in libro Bair quae sit affinitas capris et quae agnis cum spiritibus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25046

'Bair' is Pico's spelling of the Bahir; kept as printed.

English

Whoever reads in the book Bahir what kinship the she-goats have, and what the lambs have, with spirits will grasp what the she-goats mean in Zoroaster.
