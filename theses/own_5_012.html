Thesis 5>12

Latin

Predictis uitis cooperantur Sol et Luna ut causae uniuersales et per appropriationem: Luna Mercurio et Saturno, Sol Veneri et Marti, simul uterque Ioui.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22349

The sheet's OCR transcription used underscores in place of spaces ('uniuersales_et', 'Luna_Mercurio_et'); silently normalised here. Cf. the 1486 print's punctuation with colons, 'Luna:Mercuno:&Saturno.Sol:Venen & Marti' (farmer1998:22365).

English

To the aforesaid lives, the Sun and Moon lend their cooperation as universal causes, and also by appropriation: the Moon to Mercury and Saturn, the Sun to Venus and Mars, and both of them together to Jove.
