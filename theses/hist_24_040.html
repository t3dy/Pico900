Latin

In quantum opifex ad exemplar formae unius in per se uiuente respicit, primam partem corporis mundani facit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16591; 1486 print folio <10v/11r>

English

Insofar as the demiurge looks to the exemplar of the form of the 'one' in the 'living being in itself', he makes the first part of the body of the world. unverified draft
