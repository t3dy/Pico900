Book of Causes · thesis 6>3

Thesis 6>3

Latin

Cum dixit Abucaten Omnem causam primariam plus influere, per plus intelligas eminentiam modi causandi et intimitatem eius quod in re producitur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23443

English

Abucaten's statement that every primary cause exerts a greater influence should be taken to mean the superior manner of its causing and the depth to which what it produces penetrates the thing.
