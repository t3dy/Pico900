Latin

Cum dicit Aristoteles nono Methaphysicae separata et diuina aut totaliter sciri a nobis, aut totaliter ignorari, intelligendum est de ea cognitione quae his contingit qui iam ad summam intellectus actuationem peruenerunt.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14736; 1486 print folio <7r/7v>

English

When Aristotle says in the ninth book of the Metaphysics that separate and divine things are either wholly known by us or wholly unknown, this is to be understood of that knowledge which befalls those who have already arrived at the highest actuation of the intellect. unverified draft
