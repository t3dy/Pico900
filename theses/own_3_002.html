Thesis 3>2

Latin

Non possumus dicere quod de re precisissime sumpta aliquid praedicetur praedicatione proprie dicta.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20321

English

Of a thing taken in its most precise sense, we cannot say that anything at all is predicated of it by predication properly so called.
