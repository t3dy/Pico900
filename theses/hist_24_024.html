Latin

Ducalis trinitatis Iupiter est substantificatiuus, Neptunnus uiuificatiuus, Pluto conuersiuus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16288

English

Of the trinity of leaders, Jupiter makes substance, Neptune gives life, Pluto turns things back. unverified draft

The three adjectives 'substantificatiuus', 'uiuificatiuus', 'conuersiuus' are scholastic coinages; rendered by paraphrase ('makes substance', 'gives life', 'turns back'). 'Neptunnus' is Pico's printed spelling. Sense checked against farmer1998:16343.
