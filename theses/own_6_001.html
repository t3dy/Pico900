Latin

Cum dixit Abucaten Auenan animam esse supra tempus, intelligendum est de anima absoluta secundum substantiam, praescindendo ab omni operatione quae competit ei inquantum est anima.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23354

English

When Abucaten Auenan said that the soul is above time, this is to be understood of the soul considered absolutely according to its substance, prescinding from every operation that belongs to it insofar as it is a soul. unverified draft

'Abucaten Auenan' is kept untransliterated as Pico's Latin name for the authority he is citing on the Book of Causes material, with no identification attempted here, at farmer1998:23354.
