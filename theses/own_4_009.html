Thesis 4>9

Latin

Licet ita mihi uideatur probabile, non est tamen pertinaciter asserendum quod anima Christi per alium modum nobis ignotum non potuerit in infernum descendere.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21661

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 ignotum potuerit | Emendationes errorum, corrige: non potuerit | 1487 text emended sic.

1486 reads '...ignotum potuerit...'; the Emendationes errorum corrects this to 'non potuerit', which 1487 follows. The negation is essential to the sense and is kept.

English

Though it strikes me as probable, one should not stubbornly insist that the soul of Christ could not have descended into hell by some other way unknown to us.

The Latin marks this as Pico's own probable view, explicitly withheld from firm assertion; pico_stance recorded as unstated on that basis.
