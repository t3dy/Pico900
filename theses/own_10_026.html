Doctrine

Farmer's note glosses the 'first form' of the thesis as 'the total intellect', the highest level of reality attained by the mystic before the soul's Sabbath, that is, union with God, and compares the phrasing to thesis 7.3 (from Averroes) and thesis 20.7 (from Plotinus) (farmer1998:26148).

unverified draft

Context

The thesis belongs to the Orphic Hymns own-opinion series (farmer1998:25560). Farmer cross-references thesis 5>52, on Socrates's prayer to Pan in the Phaedrus as giving 'the complete opinion of Plato on felicity' (farmer1998:23089), and thesis 20.7, from Plotinus, on the ultimate felicity of man as the conjunction of the particular intellect with the total, first intellect (farmer1998:15105).
