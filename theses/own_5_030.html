Thesis 5>30

Latin

Modus cognoscendi per ideas est ille cuius dixit Plato in Timeo paucos homines esse participes, sed bene deos omnes.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22669

English

Plato states in the Timaeus that only a few human beings share in this way of knowing through the ideas, though every god does fully; it is this mode of knowledge that is meant here.
