Thesis 8>8

Latin

Magi in .xvii. amphorismo nihil aliud intelligunt per triplex indumentum ex lino, panno, et pellibus, quam triplex animae habitaculum, caeleste, spiritale, et terrenum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24851

English

In the seventeenth aphorism the magi understand by the threefold garment of linen, cloth, and skins nothing other than the threefold dwelling-place of the soul: celestial, spiritual, and terrestrial.
