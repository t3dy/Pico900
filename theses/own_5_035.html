Latin

Per necessitatis regnum in Symposio Platonis, nihil aliud intelligere debemus quam superabundantiam naturae alterius supra naturam eiusdem, et infiniti supra terminum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22767

English

By the 'kingdom of necessity' in Plato's Symposium we should understand nothing other than the superabundance of the nature of the Other over the nature of the Same, and of the Infinite over the Limit. unverified draft

'necessitatis regnum' is kept literal, 'kingdom of necessity', glossing the Symposium myth of Poros and Penia, at farmer1998:22767.
