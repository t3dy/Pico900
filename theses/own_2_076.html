Latin

Sicut quilibet philosophus habet dicere quod uirtutes sensitiue sunt in corde, ita quilibet medicus habet dicere quod sint in cerebro.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20108; 1486 print folio <18r/18v>

The sheet's line-break slash in 'sensitiue / sunt' is repaired to a space (farmer1998:20108).

English

Just as every philosopher must say that the sensitive powers reside in the heart, so every physician must say that they reside in the brain. unverified draft

Farmer's note reads this as a secular double-truth harmonizing Aristotle and Galen (farmer1998:20189); both claims form Pico's own reconciling formula rather than a report of a single ascribed view, so pico_stance is given as endorses.
