Thesis 26.1

Latin

Ordo separatorum principialis non est primus, ut putant aegyptii, sed super eum est ordo fontalis, unialiter superexaltatus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17125

The sheet's OCR gives 'aegyptü' for 'aegyptii'; corrected here.

English

It is not the first-ranked order of separated things that stands highest, as the Egyptians hold, but a fontal order above it, exalted above everything in its own unity.
