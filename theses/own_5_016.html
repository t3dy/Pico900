Latin

Per ipsum tale, uere tale, semper tale, in Platonis doctrina, debemus intelligere proprietatem intellectus, animae, et primorum corporum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22442; 1486 print folio <23r/23v>

English

By 'the such itself,' 'the truly such,' and 'the always such,' in Plato's doctrine, we ought to understand the property of intellect, of soul, and of the first bodies. unverified draft
