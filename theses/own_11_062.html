Thesis 11>62

Latin

Qui profunde considerauerit nouenarium beatitudinum numerum de quo apud Matheum in euangelio, uidebit illas mirabiliter conuenire nouenario nouem numerationum quae sunt infra primam, quae est inaccessibilis diuinitatis abyssus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27741

English

Whoever weighs closely the nine beatitudes set out in Matthew's Gospel will see them correspond, remarkably, to the group of nine numerations that lie beneath the first — the first being the unreachable abyss of the godhead.

'numeration' renders 'numeratio', Pico's own word for a sefirah. Matt. 5:1-12 gives the nine beatitudes; Farmer's note takes the 'inaccessible abyss of divinity' to be Keter ('crown'), the first sefirah, rather than Ein-Sof, God himself above the whole system (farmer1998:27768).
