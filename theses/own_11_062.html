Doctrine

The thesis correlates the nine beatitudes of Matthew's Gospel (farmer1998:27768) with a ninefold numerological scheme; Farmer's note, reporting the sense of the thesis's remainder, glosses the 'inaccessible abyss of divinity' as not Ein Sof or God himself (paraphrase; farmer1998:27768, 27787).

Historiography

Busi and Ebgi's edition quotes the thesis's Latin in full (in a text extending beyond the point at which Farmer's copy-text breaks off) as an instance of the 'abyss of divinity' image recurring in Pico's Kabbalistic theses stated in his own opinion (busi2014:15610); the surrounding Italian argument, including a comparison to Lewy's Chaldaean Oracles, is not established here. Wirszubski's Pico della Mirandola's Encounter with Jewish Mysticism likewise quotes the thesis's Latin in full (wirszubski1989:7404, 7406).
