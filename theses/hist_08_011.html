Organum odoratus sunt caruncule mamillares in anteriori cerebri parte constitutae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13680

English

The organ of smell is the mammillary caruncles situated in the anterior part of the brain. unverified draft

'Caruncule mamillares' (mammillary caruncles/processes) is the medieval anatomical term, following Avicenna, for what later anatomy identifies with the olfactory bulbs (farmer1998:13680).
