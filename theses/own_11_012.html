Latin

Non potest operari per puram Cabalam, qui non est rationaliter intellectualis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 26604

English

No one who is not rationally intellectual can act by way of the pure Cabala. unverified draft

'rationaliter intellectualis' is kept literal rather than smoothed to 'rational and intellectual'; Farmer's note ties the pure Cabala to the intellectual part of the soul, as against natural magic in the rational part (farmer1998:26679).
