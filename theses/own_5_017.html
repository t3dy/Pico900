Latin

Si Syriani doctrinam sequamur, conueniens est post unitatem totalis intellectionis, quae et trifariam diuiditur in substantialem, potentialem, et operatiuam, ponere trinarium intellectionis, partialis scilicet, participatae, et imaginariae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22446

English

If we follow the doctrine of Syrianus, it is fitting, after the unity of total intellection—which is itself divided three ways into substantial, potential, and operative—to posit a further triad of intellection: namely partial, participated, and imaginal. unverified draft
