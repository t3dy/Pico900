Latin

Si Syriani doctrinam sequamur, conueniens est post unitatem totalis intellectionis, quae et trifariam diuiditur in substantialem, potentialem, et operatiuam, ponere trinarium intellectionis, partialis scilicet, participatae, et imaginariae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22446

English

If we follow the doctrine of Syrianus, it is fitting, after the unity of total intellection -- itself divided three ways into substantial, potential, and operative -- to posit a threefold intellection: namely partial, participated, and imaginary. unverified draft
