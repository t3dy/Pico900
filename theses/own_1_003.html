Latin

De ponenda sexta notione, quae est inspirabilitas, non debent discordare Thomistae et Scotistae, si recte suorum doctorum fundamenta introspiciunt.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18582; 1486 print folio <14v/15r>

The sheet's 'funda/menta' (farmer1998:18582) repairs a line-break hyphenation to 'fundamenta'.

English

On positing the sixth notion — spirability — Thomists and Scotists should not disagree, if they examine correctly the foundations of their own masters. unverified draft

'inspirabilitas' rendered 'spirability' (the Holy Spirit's mark of procession, farmer1998:18623) rather than a literal 'inspirability'.
