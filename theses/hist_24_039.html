Proclus · thesis 24.39

Thesis 24.39

Latin

Opifex, ad quaternitatem respiciens per se animalis, quatuor fabricat partes principales mundi.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16587

English

The maker, looking to the fourfold of the animal per se, fashions the four chief parts of the world.

'opifex' is the demiurge ('maker'); Farmer's note identifies the demiurge here with the third of the seven chief members of the intellectual hebdomad (farmer1998:16622-16625). 'per se animal' is kept in Latin form because it is a fixed term for the intelligible living being.
