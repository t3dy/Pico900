Latin

Virtutes morales et cardinales remanebunt in patria post resurrectionem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11415; 1486 print folio <1v/2r>

English

The moral and cardinal virtues will remain in the homeland after the resurrection. unverified draft

'in patria' ('in the homeland') is the scholastic term for the state of the blessed after the resurrection, as against life 'in via' on earth, the state of this thesis (farmer1998:11415).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch2, from Latin at farmer1998:11415

Attribution: Reports Thomas Aquinas (farmer1998:11415). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

The thesis holds that the moral and cardinal virtues persist in the blessed after the resurrection (farmer1998:11415). Edelheit reports that moral and cardinal virtues are at the centre of thesis 11, which states that they remain in patria after the resurrection, and finds this discussed by Thomas in his commentary on the Sentences, 3, distinction 34, question 1, article 3, where Thomas argues, aiming to support a contrary position, in the response to argument 6 (edelheit2022:10624). Farmer places the thesis within a diffuse set of theses on the virtues (farmer1998:11454).

unverified draft

Historiography

Edelheit (2022) judges that Thomas's discussion is much more elaborate than Pico's single-sentence thesis, while finding the latter's formulation adequate to and reflective of familiarity with Thomas's treatment (edelheit2022:10624).
