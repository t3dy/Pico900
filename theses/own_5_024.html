Latin

Amor de quo Plotinus loquitur non est coelestis amor de quo Plato in Symposio, sed illius uera et proxima imago.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22561

English

The love of which Plotinus speaks is not the heavenly love that Plato speaks of in the Symposium, but its true and closest image. unverified draft

'coelestis amor' is Plato's Uranian love from the Symposium myth of the two Aphrodites, of which Pico here makes Plotinus's love only an image, at farmer1998:22561.
