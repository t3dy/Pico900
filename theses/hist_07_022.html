Thesis 7.22

Latin

Demonstratio septimi Physicorum, quod omne quod mouetur, mouetur ab alio, est demonstratio signi et nullo modo causae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13266

Sheet's underscore in 'mouetur_mouetur' normalized to a comma, restoring the standard Aristotelian formula 'omne quod movetur ab alio movetur'.

English

That every moved thing is moved by another — the proof given in the seventh book of the Physics — argues from a sign, and not at all from the cause.
