Scotus · thesis 4.16

Thesis 4.16

Latin

Relatio creaturae ad deum est idem realiter fundamento, distincta formaliter et ex natura rei.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12361

English

A creature's relation to God is really identical with its ground, yet is formally distinct from it, and distinct from the nature of the thing itself.

Opens a series on relations, resolving the tension between a creature's dependence on God and God's transcendence via a 'formal distinction' (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12399). Sense checked against farmer1998:12422.
