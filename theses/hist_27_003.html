Latin

Anima in corpore, mens in anima, in mente uerbum, tum horum pater deus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17248

English

Soul is in body, mind is in soul, in mind is the word, and God is the father of these three. unverified draft

Rendered directly from the Latin at farmer1998:17248, taking 'tum horum pater deus' as naming God the father of the three preceding terms, body, soul and mind, rather than of the word alone (farmer1998:17248).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch10, from Latin at farmer1998:17248

Attribution: Mercury Trismegistus · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

The thesis states a graded procession from body through soul and mind to the word, naming God as the father of the series (farmer1998:17248). The thesis is headed, in the edition consulted, 'Conclusio secundum priscam doctrinam Mercurii Trismegisti Aegyptii', which frames it as a report of Hermetic doctrine rather than Pico's own claim (kabbalah_paradigma:1397). Farmer pairs it with the following thesis, 27.4, and directs comparison to thesis 22.12 of Porphyry (farmer1998:17338).
