Cabalistic, confirming the Christian religion · thesis 11>31

Thesis 11>31

Latin

Cum audis Cabalistas ponere in Thesua informitatem, intellige informitatem per antecedentiam ad formalitatem, non per priuationem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27021

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 per accidentiam ad formalitatem

'Thesua' is kept as printed; Farmer judges Pico's transliteration here possibly corrupt for Teshuvah, an alternate name of the third sefirah (farmer1998:27093).

English

When you hear the Cabalists place formlessness in Thesua, understand that formlessness as coming before form, not as a privation of it.

'per antecedentiam ad formalitatem' is kept as 'coming before form' to preserve the contrast Pico draws with mere privation; Farmer connects the sefirah in question with the intellectual nature, the source of worldly forms (farmer1998:27093).
