Thesis 3>71

Latin

Empedocles per litem et amicitiam in anima nihil aliud intelligit quam potentiam sursum ductiuam et deorsum ductiuam in ea, quas ego credo proportionari in scientia sephirot aeternitati et decori.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21360

English

By strife and friendship in the soul, Empedocles points to nothing more than the upward-drawing and downward-drawing powers within it, which I take to correspond, in the science of the sefirot, to Eternity and Beauty.

'aeternitati et decori' name the seventh and eighth sefirot (Netzach and Hod) in Pico's kabbalistic correspondences.
