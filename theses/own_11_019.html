Latin

Si dictum illud Prophetae: Vendiderunt iustum argento, cabalistice exponamus, nihil aliud nobis significat quam hoc, scilicet, Deus ut redemptor uenditus fuit argento.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 26801

English

If we explain that saying of the Prophet, They sold the just man for silver, in the Cabalistic manner, it signifies to us nothing other than this: that God, as redeemer, was sold for silver. unverified draft

The verse is Amos 2:6, read through Matt. 26:14-16 (farmer1998:26833); 'iustus' and 'redemptor' are, on Farmer's note, standing names for the ninth sefirah, the same pairing taken up again in 11>21 (farmer1998:26833).
