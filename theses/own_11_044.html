Thesis 11>44

Latin

Scitur ex cabala per mysterium partis septentrionalis cur iudicabit deus saeculum per ignem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27331

English

It is known from the Kabbalah, through the mystery of the northern part, why God will judge the world through fire. unverified draft

'partis septentrionalis' (the northern part) is glossed by Farmer's note, shared with the companion thesis 11>45, as the standard kabbalistic association of 'north' and 'fire' with the fifth sefirah, God's judgment or power (paraphrase; farmer1998:27363).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch20, from Latin at farmer1998:27331

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (section 11>, Cabalistic conclusions confirming the Christian religion by his own opinion) (farmer1998:27331). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

Farmer's note, covering theses 11>44-45 together, explains that in medieval Kabbalism 'north' and 'fire' were most often associated with the fifth sefirah, God's judgment or power, and 'water' with the fourth, God's love or piety (paraphrase; farmer1998:27363). Thesis 11>44 uses this association to argue that kabbalistic symbolism accounts for the Christian teaching that God will judge the world by fire (farmer1998:27331).
