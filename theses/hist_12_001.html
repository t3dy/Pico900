Demonstratio octaui Physicorum, a Aristotele ad probandum primum motorem adducta, aliquid probat speciale primo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14139

English

The demonstration in the eighth book of the Physics, brought forward by Aristotle to prove a first mover, proves something particular about a first mover, not an absolutely first one. unverified draft

'aliquid probat speciale primo' is ambiguous; rendered per the standard reading that the argument proves only a limited, not an absolutely universal, first mover (farmer1998:14139).
