Thomas Aquinas · thesis 2.35

Thesis 2.35

Latin

Haec propositio: Homo est risibilis, non est in secundo modo dicendi per se.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11773; 1486 print folio <2r/2v>

Sheet's mid-word line-break slash removed; text otherwise as printed.

English

The proposition 'man is risible' does not belong to the second mode of per se predication.

The 'second mode' predicates a propria passio or proper accident (Farmer's note on 2.24, farmer1998:11682). Sense checked against farmer1998:11834.
