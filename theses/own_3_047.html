Thesis 3>47

Latin

Melius potest saluare textus Aristotelis qui dicit omnem animam esse immortalem, quam qui dicit omnem animam esse mortalem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20980

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 salvari | 1486 Emendationes errorum, corrige: salvare | 1487 text not emended

1486 reads 'salvari', corrected by the Emendationes errorum to 'salvare'; 1487 leaves the text unemended.

English

Whoever maintains that every soul is immortal does more justice to Aristotle's text than whoever maintains that every soul is mortal.
