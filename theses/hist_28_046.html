Hebrew Cabalists · thesis 28.46

Thesis 28.46

Latin

Non punitur Rex terrae in terra, quin prius humilietur militia coelestis in coelo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18406

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 ponitur

1487 reads 'ponitur' for 'punitur' (farmer1998:18416).

English

Earth's king cannot be punished on earth until the heavenly host has first been brought low in heaven.

Sense checked against farmer1998:18452; reworded to avoid near-identity with the published English at that line.
