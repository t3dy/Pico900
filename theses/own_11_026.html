Latin

Quilibet Cabalista habet concedere ex dictis doctorum huius scientiae hoc manifeste dicentium, quod peccatum originale in aduentu messiae expiabitur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 26921

English

Every Cabalist has to concede, from the sayings of the doctors of this science who state this manifestly, that original sin will be expiated at the Messiah's coming. unverified draft

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch19, from Latin at farmer1998:26921

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (farmer1998:26921). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

Farmer's note groups this thesis with the preceding 11>25 as views 'that could be drawn directly from a wide range of kabbalistic, Midrashic, or Talmudic texts' (farmer1998:26992). The thesis follows the same pattern as 11>25, a claim about the Messiah said to be conceded by Cabalist doctors themselves and read by Pico as confirming Christian doctrine, here applied to original sin rather than to the nature of captivity (farmer1998:26917, 26921).
