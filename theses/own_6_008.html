Latin

Per ultimam propositionem Abucaten possumus intelligere quid sibi uelit illa diuisio quam fecit Plato in principio tractatus Timei, et possumus scire quod sub ea non comprehenditur anima nisi / per uiam extremalis conbinationis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23542; 1486 print folio <25v/26r>

English

By Abucaten's last proposition we can understand what is meant by the division which Plato makes at the beginning of the treatise of the Timaeus, and we can know that the soul is comprehended under it only by way of an extreme combination. unverified draft
