Latin

Cum dicit Salomon in oratione sua in libro Regum: Exaudi o_coelum, per coelum lineam uiridem debemus intelligere quae gyrat uniuersum.

When Solomon says, in his prayer in the Book of Kings, 'Hear, O heaven,' by 'heaven' we are to understand the green line that girds the universe. unverified draft

Doctrine

The thesis reads Solomon's prayer kabbalistically: the vocative 'heaven' is glossed as the 'green line' that girds the universe, a standard symbol for the third sefirah, 'intelligence' (farmer1998:17673). Farmer notes an occult link to thesis 22.12 of Porphyry, drawn by way of thesis 11>29, and reports that the two texts from Mithridates that Wirszubski cites for the phrase 'green line' do not themselves explain Pico's sense here (farmer1998:17673).

Historiography

Wirszubski quotes the thesis as the seventh conclusion of the Cabalistic set and glosses 'the green line that encircles the universe' as Intelligence, the third sefirah, in his discussion of Pico's sources (wirszubski1989:1501, 1504, 9218).
