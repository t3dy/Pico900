Thesis 3>25

Latin

Nisi destruamus naturam intellectualem, non possumus intelligere ideas numerari, nisi per intimationem ideae numeri per reliquas, sicut est cuiuslibet per omnes.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20643; 1486 print folio <19r/19v>

English

Short of destroying the intellectual nature, the only way we can understand the ideas as numbered is through the number-idea being intimated by the rest of the ideas, the way any single idea is intimated through all the others.
