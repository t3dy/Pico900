Latin

Nisi destruamus naturam intellectualem, non possumus intelligere ideas numerari, nisi per intimationem ideae numeri per re/liquas, sicut est cuiuslibet per omnes.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20643; 1486 print folio <19r/19v>

English

Unless we destroy intellectual nature, we cannot understand the ideas to be numbered, except through the intimation of the idea of number by means of the rest, just as it is the case of any one through all. unverified draft
