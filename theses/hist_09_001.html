Latin

Primum quod est necessarium in demonstratione non est quod diffiniuit Aristoteles primo Posteriorum, sed sic debet definiri: Primum est illud quod ita est uniuersalius subiecto, ut tamen de subiecti genere non praedicetur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13772

Apparatus (Farmer): colon retained from 1486 edition

English

The 'first' that is necessary in a demonstration is not what Aristotle defined in the first book of the Posterior Analytics, but should be defined thus: the first is that which is so more universal than the subject that it is nevertheless not predicated of the subject's genus. unverified draft
