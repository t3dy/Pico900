Mohammed of Toledo · thesis 13.5

Thesis 13.5

Latin

De nulla re an existat potest quaerere aliquis artifex specialis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14228

English

No particular science can inquire, of any given thing, whether it exists.

The Latin admits two readings of 'artifex specialis': a particular science or discipline (paralleling 7.14, that no science proves that its own subject exists), or, as Farmer renders it, 'a special creator' (a question about a divine agent) at farmer1998:14273. This translation follows the first reading; the crux is not resolved here.
