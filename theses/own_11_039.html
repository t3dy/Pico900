Latin

Ex hac conclusione et trigesima superius posita sequitur quod quilibet Cabalista habet concedere quod interrogatus Iesus quis esset, rectissime respondit, dicens: Ego sum principium qui loquor uobis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27226

The sheet prints 'lesus' for 'Iesus' (I/long-s confusion), corrected here.

English

It follows from this conclusion, taken with the thirtieth stated earlier, that every Cabalist must concede that when Jesus was asked who he was, his answer was exactly right: I am the beginning, the one speaking to you. unverified draft

The verse is a variant form of John 8:25 as it appears in some manuscripts of the Vulgate; Pico here links Christ to Hokhmah, the second sefirah, as in 28.25 (farmer1998:27301).
