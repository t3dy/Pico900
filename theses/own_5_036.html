Latin

Per demonstrationem Platonis in Phedro de animae immortalitate, nec de nostris animis, ut Proclus, Hermias, et Syrianus credunt, nec de omni anima, ut Plotinus et Numenius, nec de mundi tantum anima, ut Posidonius, sed de coelesti qualibet anima, probatur et concluditur immortalitas.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22772

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 ut Proclus.Hermias. & Syrianus credunt

English

By Plato's demonstration in the Phaedrus concerning the immortality of the soul, immortality is proved and concluded neither of our own souls, as Proclus, Hermias, and Syrianus believe, nor of every soul, as Plotinus and Numenius hold, nor of the soul of the world alone, as Posidonius holds, but of any celestial soul whatever. unverified draft

The chain of authorities, Proclus, Hermias, Syrianus, Plotinus, Numenius, Posidonius, is transliterated as printed by Farmer at farmer1998:22772, with no independent check of the ascriptions.
