Proclus · thesis 24.10

Thesis 24.10

Latin

Intellectuales dii uniones habent ab uno primo; substantias ab intelligibilibus; uitas perfectas et contentiuas, generatiuas diuinorum, ab intelligibilibus et intellectualibus; intellectualem proprietatem a seipsis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16060

English

The intellectual gods hold their unities from the first One and their substances from the intelligibles. Their complete and containing lives, which generate divine things, they hold from the intelligibles and intellectuals together, and their intellectual property from themselves.

Sense checked against farmer1998:16114; no further crux. 'contentiuas' rendered 'containing'; 'ab intelligibilibus et intellectualibus' kept as one source, the gods both intelligible and intellectual.
