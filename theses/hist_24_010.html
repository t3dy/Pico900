Latin

Intellectuales dii uniones habent ab uno primo; substantias ab intelligibilibus; uitas perfectas et contentiuas, generatiuas diuinorum, ab intelligibilibus et intellectualibus; intellectualem proprietatem a seipsis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16060

English

The intellectual gods have their unions from the first one; their substances from the intelligible gods; their perfect and containing lives, generative of divine things, from the intelligible and intellectual gods; and their intellectual property from themselves. unverified draft
