Latin

Actus quo foelicitatur natura angelica et rationalis ultima foelicitate, nec est actus intellectus, nec uoluntatis, sed est unio unitatis quae est in alteritate animae cum unitate quae est sine alteritate.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20885

English

The act that brings the angelic and rational nature to their final beatitude is neither an act of intellect nor of will, but the union of the unity present within the soul's otherness with the unity that exists free of otherness. unverified draft
