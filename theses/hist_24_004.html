Proclus · thesis 24.4

Thesis 24.4

Latin

Contentiua proprietas est medii ordinis secundae trinitatis, qui in Phedro coelestis dicitur circumductus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15954

English

The containing property belongs to the middle order of the second triad, which the Phaedrus calls the heavenly revolution.
