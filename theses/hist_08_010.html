Latin

Propositio dicens quod sensibile supra sensum positum non facit sensationem, non est uera nisi per accidens.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13676

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 dicens que sensibile | Emendationes errorum, corrige: dicens quod sensibile | 1487 text emended sic

English

The proposition stating that a sensible object placed directly upon the sense organ does not produce sensation is true only accidentally, not universally. unverified draft
