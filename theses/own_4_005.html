Thesis 4>5

Latin

Attributales perfectiones nec in deo, nec secundum se quiditatiue sumptae, dicunt diuersas rationes in recto, et principaliter diffinibiles uel descriptibiles.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21554

English

The attributal perfections, whether taken in God or taken quidditatively on their own, do not properly amount to distinct concepts, above all ones open to definition or description.

Parallels the claim about the three transcendentals in 4>7.
