Latin

Attributales perfectiones nec in deo, nec secundum se quiditatiue sumptae, dicunt diuersas rationes in recto, et principaliter diffinibiles uel descriptibiles.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21554

English

The attributal perfections -- neither in God, nor when taken quidditatively in themselves -- express diverse formal characters directly, and are principally definable or describable. unverified draft

A literal rendering of a technical scholastic formulation about the divine attributes; 'rationes...in recto' ('formal characters...directly') is not resolved into a single scholar's terminology here (farmer1998:21554).
