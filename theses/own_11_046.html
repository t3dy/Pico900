Latin

Per eclipsationem solis quae accidit in morte Christi sciri potest secundum fundamenta cabalae quod tunc passus est filius dei et uerus messias.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27339

English

Through the eclipse of the sun that occurred at Christ's death, it can be known, according to the foundations of the Kabbalah, that then the Son of God and the true Messiah suffered. unverified draft

Doctrine

Farmer's note is limited to a cross-reference: 'For the correlation between Christ and the sun, see 11>51' (farmer1998:27406). Thesis 11>51 states, in Farmer's cross-reference text, that as the moon was full in Solomon, so the sun was full in the true Messiah, Jesus, and that a corresponding waning can be conjectured in Zedekiah for one who searches deeply in the Kabbalah (farmer1998:27445). The thesis immediately follows 10>10, on 'the fear of Isaac', and precedes 11>47, on Satan's temptation of Christ via 'the property of Aquilo' in the Kabbalah (farmer1998:25782, 27343).

Context

The 'Erkenntnistheorie und christliche Kabbalah' study groups 11>46 with 8>9 and 11>38 under a heading concerning the veil of the Temple ('Zum Tempelvorhang'), citing Pompeo Faracovi's discussion of 'il tema dell'eclissi' (the theme of the eclipse) for further background (kabbalah_paradigma:2122).

Historiography

Akopyan (2018) quotes the thesis, numbered 46 in his citation of the Conclusiones Cabalisticae, immediately after quoting thesis 51 (his number 51) on the full moon and full sun, and appends a reference to 'Secret, I cabbalisti cristiani, 60, 77-78, 137-39' for further study of the idea (akopyan2018:690). Akopyan (2021) reprints the same pairing of theses 46 and 51 in identical Latin (akopyan2021:1404). Busi and Ebgi print the thesis as their number [93], immediately followed by 11>51 as [94], without further discursive comment in the material consulted (busi2014:25054).
