Latin

Theologice loquendo dico quod in aeuo non est successio formaliter intrinseca continuatiua, sed bene terminatiua; secundum philosophos tamen aliter dicerem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22046

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 Thelogice

English

Speaking theologically, I say that in aevum there is no formally intrinsic continuative succession, but there is indeed a terminative one; according to the philosophers, however, I would say otherwise. unverified draft

'aeuum' ('aevum', aeviternity) is the scholastic middle mode of duration between eternity and time, proper to angels; left transliterated (farmer1998:22046).
