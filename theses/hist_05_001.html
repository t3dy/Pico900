Henry of Ghent · thesis 5.1

Thesis 5.1

Latin

Datur lumen superius lumine fidei, in quo Theologi uident ueritates theologicae scientiae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12482

English

There exists a light higher than the light of faith, by which theologians behold the truths of theological science.
