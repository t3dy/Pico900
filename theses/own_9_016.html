Thesis 9>16

Latin

Illa natura quae est orizon temporis aeternalis est proxima mago, sed infra eum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25354

The sheet's OCR text joined 'aeternalis' and 'est' as 'aeternalis_est'; restored as two words.

English

That nature which is the horizon of eternal time is nearest to the magician, but below him.
