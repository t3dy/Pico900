Latin

Posse animam per uiam purgatoriam, absque alio studio uel investigatione, per solam modicam et facillimam collationem et aduertentiam super iam desuper habita intelligibilia, perfectam omnium scibilium scientiam acquirere, non solum Platonici philosophi, sed etiam inter Peripateticos, hi de quibus minus uidetur, Auenrois sequaces habent concedere.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20868; 1486 print folio <19v/20r>

English

That the soul can, by a purgative path, acquire perfect knowledge of everything knowable through nothing more than a modest and effortless gathering of, and attention to, intelligibles already possessed from above, without any further study or inquiry — this is a concession not only the Platonist philosophers but even, among the Peripatetics, the followers of Averroes (in whom it is least expected) are bound to make. unverified draft
