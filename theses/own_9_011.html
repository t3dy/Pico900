Latin

Mirabilia artis magicae non sunt nisi per unionem et actuationem eorum quae seminaliter et separate sunt in natura.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25272

English

The marvels of the magical art come about only through the union and actuation of those things that exist seminally and separately in nature. unverified draft

'seminaliter et separate' parallels the same pair of terms in 9>5's 'seminaliter et separata', which Farmer's note on 9>5 explicitly links to this thesis (farmer1998:25215).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch17, from Latin at farmer1998:25272

Attribution: Pico's own opinion, section 9> (Magic) (farmer1998:25272). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

Farmer groups this thesis with 9>10-14, explaining that the purified soul of the magician can gather up the rational forces distributed through the celestial and terrestrial worlds and elevate them, so that the magus functions as a cosmic priest, cross-referencing 8>3 and 10>3 (farmer1998:25295).

unverified draft

Historiography

Akopyan (2018) and Akopyan (2021) quote this thesis's Latin verbatim in the same footnote as 9>5, from consecutive pages of Pico's magic conclusions, treating the pair as a matched statement of the magus's power over dispersed seminal virtues (akopyan2018:581; akopyan2021:1339).
