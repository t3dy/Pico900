Latin

Sicut fides quae est credulitas est infra scientiam, ita fides quae est uere fides est supersubstantialiter supra scientiam et intellectum, nos deo immediate coniungens.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16610

English

Just as the faith that is mere credulity is below knowledge, so the faith that is truly faith is, in a supersubstantial way, above knowledge and intellect, joining us immediately to God. unverified draft
