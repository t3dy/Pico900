Book of Causes · thesis 6>2

Thesis 6>2

Latin

Cum dicit Abucaten: Omnis anima nobilis tres habet operationes, diuinam, intellectualem, et animalem, ita est intelligendum, quod primam habeat per imaginem proportionalitatis, secundam per formalitatem participationis, tertiam per proprietatem essentialitatis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23437

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 operationes.diuinam:

1486 print reads 'operationes.diuinam:' with a period and colon for commas (farmer1998:23469); wording unchanged.

English

Abucaten states that every noble soul has three operations—divine, intellectual, and animal; understand this to mean that it holds the first by a likeness of proportion, the second by the formal character of participation, and the third by the property of essentiality itself.
