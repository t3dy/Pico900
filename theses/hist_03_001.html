Latin

Ideo haec est falsa: Essentia generat, quia essentia est ultimate abstracta, et generat formaliter praedicatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11974

The sheet carries a stray trailing character after the sentence, an OCR artifact, omitted here.

English

Hence the statement 'the essence generates' is false, since the essence is abstracted to the utmost degree, while 'generates' is predicated formally. unverified draft

'the essence' = God's abstract nature considered apart from the Persons (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12005). Sense checked against farmer1998:12028.
