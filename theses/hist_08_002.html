Latin

Licet in nullo syllogismo qui sit actu uel potentia cathegoricus ex duabus negatiuis concludi possit, potest tamen hoc fieri in syllogismo compositiuo, ut scilicet ex duabus negatiuis concludatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13570

English

Granted that no syllogism categorical either in act or in potential can draw a conclusion from two negative premises, this can still happen in a composite syllogism, where a conclusion is indeed drawn from two negatives. unverified draft
