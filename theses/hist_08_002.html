Latin

Licet in nullo syllogismo qui sit actu uel potentia cathegoricus ex duabus negatiuis concludi possit, potest tamen hoc fieri in syllogismo compositiuo, ut scilicet ex duabus negatiuis concludatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13570

English

Although in no syllogism that is actually or potentially categorical can a conclusion be drawn from two negative premises, this can nevertheless happen in a compositive syllogism, namely that a conclusion be drawn from two negatives. unverified draft
