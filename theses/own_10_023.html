Orphic Hymns · thesis 10>23

Thesis 10>23

Latin

Qui Apollinem adibit, mediabit opus per Bacchum triethericum, et consumabit per nomen ineffabile.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 26014

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 adibit.mediabit

1486 prints 'adibit.mediabit' without a break (farmer1998:26029).

English

One who draws near to Apollo carries the work forward through the triennial Bacchus and brings it to completion through the ineffable name.
