Thesis 5>42

Latin

Per demonstrationem Platonis in Phedro de immortalite animae, firmius demonstratur aeternitas mundi quam per ullam rationem Aristotelis in .vii. Physicorum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22882

English

Aristotle's arguments in Book Seven of the Physics prove the world's eternity less firmly than Plato's demonstration, in the Phaedrus, of the soul's immortality does.
