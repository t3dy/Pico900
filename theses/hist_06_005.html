Latin

Angeli non fuerunt creati in gratia.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12686

English

Angels were not created in grace. unverified draft

'in gratia' denotes the theological state of sanctifying grace; the thesis denies that angels possessed this state from the moment of their creation (farmer1998:12686).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-swarm-batch6, from Latin at farmer1998:12686

Attribution: Reports Giles of Rome (farmer1998:12686). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer's note pairs 6.5 with the following 6.6 as 'again anti-Thomistic,' citing Thomas, Summa Ia, question 62 article 3, and Ia, question 64 article 2, as the position opposed (paraphrase, farmer1998:12768). Edelheit reports that Giles's commentary on the Sentences, Book 2, distinction 4, question 1, offers two views on the beatitude and misery of good and evil angels, including the view that evil angels can never be blessed (paraphrase, edelheit2022:16363).

unverified draft

Context

Edelheit divides the eleven Giles theses into a first group, theses 1-7, on theological issues including angelology, and a second, theses 8-11, on natural philosophy (edelheit2022:15967). He states that thesis 6.5 'opens the whole discussion of whether angels were created in grace, a complicated issue which cannot be reflected in a short thesis' (edelheit2022:16988). He reports that theses 5, 6 and 7 of this set 'are included in a recent English translation of selections from Pico's 900 Theses,' in Copenhaver's *Magic and the Dignity of Man* (edelheit2022:16386); that translation is in copyright and is not reproduced here.
