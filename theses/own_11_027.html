Doctrine

Farmer's note states that the thesis 'ties theses 28.31 and 11>22 together nicely' (farmer1998:26995), but the truncation of the Latin available here prevents any further account of how the argument runs (farmer1998:26926). Thesis 28.31, among the historical Hebrew-Cabalist theses and quoted in this thesis's cross-reference list, holds that circumcision was given for liberation from unclean powers that 'walk about' (farmer1998:18175), and 11>28, the following thesis, reads a Hebrew particle repeated twice in Genesis 1:1 as pointing to two prior creations, an intellectual nature and an animal nature (farmer1998:27006).
