Thesis 11>27

Latin

Ex principiis cabalistarum euidenter elicitur quod per aduentum messiae tolletur circumcisionis necessitas.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 26926

English

From the Cabalists' own principles it is plainly drawn that the necessity of circumcision will be removed by the Messiah's coming.
