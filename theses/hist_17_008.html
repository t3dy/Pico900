Thesis 17.8

Latin

Sicut lumen colores non facit colores, sed praeexistentes colores potentia uisibiles, facit actu uisibiles, ita intellectus agens non facit species cum non essent prius, sed actu praeexistentes species potentia cognoscibiles, facit actu cognoscibiles.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14649

English

Just as light does not make colors to be colors, but makes colors that pre-exist as potentially visible to be actually visible, so the agent intellect does not make species when they did not exist before, but makes species that actually pre-exist as potentially knowable to be actually knowable. unverified draft
