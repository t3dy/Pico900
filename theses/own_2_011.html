Latin

Cum dicit Auenrois non esse aliud medium ad probandum abstractum praeter aeternitatem motus, non intelligit de quocunque abstracto, sed de eo quod ultimato gradu abstractionis est abstractum a corpore.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18994

English

When Averroes says that there is no other means of proving the abstract except through the eternity of motion, he does not mean this of anything abstract whatever, but of that which, at the furthest degree of abstraction, is abstract from body. unverified draft
