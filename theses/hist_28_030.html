Doctrine

Farmer reads the thesis, like 28.28, as apparently drawing further distinctions within the angelic orders (farmer1998:18160).

Context

Farmer cross-references 28.28, on the winged creature of the fifth day understood as worldly angels (farmer1998:18078) (farmer1998:18160).

unverified draft

Historiography

Wirszubski transcribes the thesis in full as his 'Conclusio XXX' and states that, though its ultimate source is undoubtedly the Zohar (part I, fol 34v), there is no need to look for its immediate source beyond Recanati (wirszubski1989:2523). Elsewhere he places the thesis in a sequence of three theses on angels, noting that the source of the preceding conclusio begins in Recanati at fol 11vb and that of this one at fol 12va (wirszubski1989:3180).
