Thesis 11>71

Latin

Per id quod dicunt Cabalistae de aegypto, et attestata est experientia, habemus credere quod terra aegypti sit in analogia, et subordinatione proprietatis potentiae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27937

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 subordinationis

The 1487 print reads 'subordinationis' (genitive) where the 1486 text has 'subordinatione' (ablative) (farmer1998:27948).

English

From what the Kabbalists say about Egypt, and as experience attests, we are bound to believe that the land of Egypt stands in analogy to, and in subordination under, the property of power. unverified draft

'Proprietas potentiae' (property of power): Farmer's note identifies this with Gevurah, the fifth sefirah (farmer1998:28019).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch21, from Latin at farmer1998:27937

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (farmer1998:27937). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

The thesis holds that the land of Egypt stands, on Kabbalistic testimony and 'attested experience,' in analogy and subordination to a 'property of power.' Farmer's note identifies this power as Gevurah, the fifth sefirah, so that a text discussing Egypt can be read symbolically as referring to God's power and, as in the neighboring theses 10>10 and 28.40, to magic (farmer1998:28019). Farmer locates the thesis's apparent inspiration in commentaries on Exodus 7:1 and following, the chapters narrating the confrontation of Moses and Aaron with Pharaoh (farmer1998:28019).
