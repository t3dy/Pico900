Thesis 11>71

Latin

Per id quod dicunt Cabalistae de aegypto, et attestata est experientia, habemus credere quod terra aegypti sit in analogia, et subordinatione proprietatis potentiae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27937

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 subordinationis

English

From what the Cabalists say about Egypt, borne out by experience, we must hold that the land of Egypt stands in analogy, and in subjection, to the property of power.

'property of power' is Gevurah, the fifth sefirah; Farmer's note takes any text on Egypt to be readable, on this basis, as referring symbolically to God's power and, as in 28.40 and 10>10, to magic as well (farmer1998:28019).
