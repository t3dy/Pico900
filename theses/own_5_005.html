Thesis 5>5

Latin

Empedocles per spheram intelligibilem a Venere contentam, nihil aliud intelligit quam mundum archetipum ab ordine intra se manentis primae prouidentiae contentum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22229

English

By the intelligible sphere held together by Venus, Empedocles means nothing other than the archetypal world, held together by the order of a first providence that remains within itself.
