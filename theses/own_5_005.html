Latin

Empedocles per spheram intelligibilem a Venere contentam, nihil aliud intelligit quam mundum archetipum ab ordine intra se manentis primae prouidentiae contentum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22229

English

By the intelligible sphere contained by Venus, Empedocles understands nothing other than the archetypal world contained by the order of the first providence remaining within itself. unverified draft
