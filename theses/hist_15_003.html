Latin

Ita se habet intellectus agens ad producenda intelligibilia in possibilem intellectum, sicut se habet forma artis ad producendas formas in materiam artis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14374

English

The agent intellect stands to the production of intelligible objects in the possible intellect just as the form belonging to a craft stands to the production of forms in the material worked by that craft. unverified draft
