Thesis 8>9

Latin

Poteris ex praecedenti conclusione aliquid intelligere de pelliceis tunicis quas sibi fecit Adam, et de pellibus quae erant in Tabernaculo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24941

English

From the preceding conclusion you will be able to understand something about the skin tunics that Adam made for himself, and about the skins that were in the Tabernacle.
