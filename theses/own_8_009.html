Doctrine

The conclusion extends the preceding conclusion, on Zoroaster's dog as the irrational soul, to two biblical images, Adam's tunics of skin (Genesis 3:21) and the skins of the Tabernacle (Exodus 36:19), both heavily glossed in medieval exegesis (farmer1998:24972). Farmer identifies the tabernacle skins as red rams' skins forming part of the veil, which Pico's Cabalistic theses treat as a symbol of Christ, and notes the Christian tradition that the veil was torn at Christ's death, making the skins here a figure of Fall and Redemption (farmer1998:24972).

Historiography

A German-language study of Pico's epistemology and Kabbalah connects this conclusion to the Temple veil (Tempelvorhang) and to two Cabalistic conclusions on the death of Christ, 11>38 and 11>46, citing a discussion by Pompeo Faracovi (kabbalah_paradigma:2121).
