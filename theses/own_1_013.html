English

The Commentator's opinion on indeterminate dimensions is not at all opposed to the principles and foundations of Saint Thomas's doctrine. unverified draft

'indeterminate dimensions' (dimensiones interminatae): quantitative extension considered in prime matter without a determinate figure.
