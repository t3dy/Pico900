The temporal procession of the Holy Spirit is measured according to the gifts of sanctifying grace. unverified draft

'dona gratiae gratum facientia', the gifts of grace that make [the recipient] pleasing [to God], is Thomas's own phrase for sanctifying grace, per Edelheit's citation of the parallel Thomistic text (edelheit2022:10301).

Doctrine

The thesis holds that the Holy Spirit's temporal procession is measured by the gifts of sanctifying grace (farmer1998:11312). Edelheit reports that in thesis 2 the temporal procession of the Holy Spirit is extended according to the gifts of grace granted to the one worthy of grace, and finds this in Thomas's Summa theologiae, 1, question 43, article 3 (edelheit2022:10271). Farmer's note places theses 2.2-9 together as concerning various sides of the problem of divine omnipotence and free will (farmer1998:11348).
