Latin

Haec est in quarto modo dicendi per se: homo est homo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 10933

English

This belongs to the fourth mode of per se predication: man is man. unverified draft

The 'fourth mode' names a predicate given as the essential cause of its subject (Farmer's note, farmer1998:11013). Sense checked against farmer1998:10989.
