Doctrine

The thesis classifies the proposition 'man is man' as belonging to the fourth of the modes in which a predicate is ascribed to a subject per se (farmer1998:10933). Farmer's note traces the four modes to the commentary tradition on the Posterior Analytics 1.4 (farmer1998:11013). The same note cross-refers the fourth mode to a thesis on common sensibles and to three of Pico's own theses on the common sense and reason (farmer1998:11130).

Historiography

Edelheit's chapter states that thesis 3 places the proposition 'a human being is a human being' in the fourth mode of speaking per se, and that Albert discusses the four modes in his commentary on the Posterior Analytics, book 1, tract 2, chapter 8 (edelheit2022:9242). Edelheit reports that in this fourth mode the proposition does not depend on anything else (edelheit2022:9267).
