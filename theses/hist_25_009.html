Thesis 25.9

Latin

Irascibilis ad concupiscentiam habet proportionem diapente.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17010

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 a diapente | Emendationes errorum, dele a quia superflue ponitur | 1487 text emended sic

English

The irascible faculty stands to concupiscence in the proportion of the fifth.
