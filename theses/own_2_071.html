Latin

Secundum omnes philosophos dicendum est deum necessario agere quicquid agit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20002

English

According to all the philosophers it must be said that God necessarily does whatever he does. unverified draft

Farmer's note reads the phrase 'according to all philosophers' as signaling an implicit double-truth, resolved elsewhere by Pico's hierarchical modes of necessity (compare 2>14-15; farmer1998:20075); pico_stance is accordingly given as unstated rather than endorses.
