Doctrine

The thesis holds that form comes to be only accidentally, not in its own right, since existence per se belongs to the composite of matter and form and not to form alone (farmer1998:11691; edelheit2022:11742). Edelheit locates Thomas's discussion in his commentary on Aristotle's Metaphysics, book 7, lecture 7, explicating Aristotle's distinction, at Metaphysics Z 1033a24-1033b19, between form or essence, which lacks matter and so is not itself generated, and the composite, which is generated (edelheit2022:11742; edelheit2022:11761).
