Thesis 3>49

Work in progress. This entry is a draft awaiting verification: every claim carries a locator, but no second reader has yet re-found each one.

Latin

Magis improprie dicitur de deo quod sit intellectus uel intelligens, quam de anima rationali quod sit angelus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20990

Edelheit transcribes the same conclusion's title, from the source he consulted, with subject and predicate reversed: 'Magis improprie dicitur de Deo quod sit intelligens vel intellectus quam de angelo quod sit anima rationalis' -- 'than of the angel that it is a rational soul,' rather than Farmer's 'than of the rational soul that it is an angel' (edelheit2022:20783; farmer1998:20990).

English

It is said more improperly of God that he is intellect or understanding, than of the rational soul that it is an angel. unverified draft

The thesis is comparative.

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-swarm-batch1, from Latin at farmer1998:20990

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (farmer1998:20990). · Pico's stance: endorses

Condemnation

Q12 in the order of the Apology: Whether God understands. Printed 7 December 1486; censured by the papal commission in February-March 1487; the work as a whole condemned by the bull of 4 August 1487.

"Wrong and can be twisted to a heretical meaning" (dougherty2008:730), Dougherty's translation of the commission's censure of the thesis as Q12; a rendering of this censure in Farmer's own English was not located in the sources consulted (see not_established).

Doctrine

Farmer cross-references the thesis to two of the numerical theses, 7a>5 ('Whether he has intellect') and 7a>6 ('In what way God understands') (farmer1998:12956; farmer1998:12957); this uploaded copy of Farmer 1998 includes the thesis's own bare Latin and English text but not the interpretive note that would explain the cross-reference or record Pico's formal reply to the commission (see not_established).

unverified draft

Context

Farmer cross-references the thesis to two theses among the numerical theses: 7a>5, Vtrum sit intelligens ('Whether he has intellect'), and 7a>6, Quomodo deus intelligat ('In what way God understands') (farmer1998:12956; farmer1998:12957).

unverified draft

Reception

The papal commission's censure of the thesis (its Q12 in the Apology's order) is recorded by Dougherty as "Wrong and can be twisted to a heretical meaning" (dougherty2008:730); a rendering of this censure in Farmer's own English was not located in the sources consulted (this uploaded copy of Farmer 1998 lacks the interpretive/trial-history notes apparatus for this thesis; see not_established). Copenhaver lists the Apology's order of the thirteen questions, giving this thesis as Q12, between Q8 (Belief) and Q13 (Soul) (copenhaver2022:436).

unverified draft

Historiography

Beyond quoting the Latin opening of the conclusion, Howlett lists the thesis among the thirteen censured propositions without further discussion (howlett2021:2029). An unidentified German-language study on Pico's Kabbalah cites the thesis alongside 22.12 ('Conclusio secundum Porphyrium') and a thesis on the doctrine of Hermes Trismegistus, without stating an argument connecting them beyond their proximity in Pico's own text (kabbalah_paradigma:1395).
