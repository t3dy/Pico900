Latin

Beatitudo est essentialiter in actu intellectus. Correlarium: Nec fruitio, nec aliquis actus uoluntatis, est essentialiter beatitudo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11419

English

Beatitude consists, in its essence, in an act of the intellect. Corollary: neither enjoyment nor any act of the will is, in its essence, beatitude. unverified draft

First of the series on intellect versus will in beatitude (Farmer's note, farmer1998:11497). Sense checked against farmer1998:11476.
