Latin

Siquis in opere precedentis conclusionis intellectualiter operabitur, per meridiem ligabit septentrionem; si uero mundialiter per totum operabitur, iudicium sibi operabitur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25887

English

If anyone works intellectually in the operation of the preceding conclusion, he will bind the north through the south; but if he works throughout in a worldly manner, he will work judgment upon himself. unverified draft
