Orphic Hymns · thesis 10>14

Thesis 10>14

Latin

Siquis in opere precedentis conclusionis intellectualiter operabitur, per meridiem ligabit septentrionem; si uero mundialiter per totum operabitur, iudicium sibi operabitur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25887

English

Working the preceding conclusion intellectually binds the north by means of the south; working it in a wholly worldly way brings judgment down upon the operator himself.
