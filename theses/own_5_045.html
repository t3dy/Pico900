Sensus naturae quem ponunt Alchindus, Bacon, Guilielmus Parisiensis, et quidam alii, maxime autem omnes magi, nihil est aliud quam sensus uehiculi quem ponunt Platonici.

English

Al-Kindi, Bacon, William of Paris, and various others—indeed virtually every magus—posit a 'sense of nature'; this is nothing but the sense possessed by the vehicle that the Platonists describe. unverified draft
