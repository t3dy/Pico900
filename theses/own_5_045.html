Latin

Sensus naturae quem ponunt Alchindus, Bacon, Guilielmus Parisiensis, et quidam alii, maxime autem omnes magi, nihil est aliud quam sensus uehiculi quem ponunt Platonici.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22973

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 Alchindus. Bacon Guilielmus Parisiensus:

English

The 'sense of nature' posited by al-Kindi, Bacon, William of Paris, and certain others, but above all by all the magi, is nothing other than the sense of the vehicle posited by the Platonists. unverified draft

'Guilielmus Parisiensis' is William of Auvergne, bishop of Paris, rendered 'William of Paris' rather than resolved to a modern form, at farmer1998:22973.
