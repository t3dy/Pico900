Hebrew Cabalists · thesis 28.8

Thesis 28.8

Latin

Animae a tertio lumine ad quartam diem, et inde ad quintam descendunt, inde exeuntes corporis noctem subintrant.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17645

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 luminae | 1487 lumine

1486 reads 'luminae' for 'lumine' (1487 corrects to 'lumine'; farmer1998:17655).

English

From the third light souls descend to the fourth day and then to the fifth, and on leaving that fifth they pass into the body's night.

Sense checked against farmer1998:17695; reworded to avoid near-identity with the published English at that line.
