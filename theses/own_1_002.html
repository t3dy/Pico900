Latin

Qui dicunt innascibilitatem esse positiuam proprietatem constituentem patrem in esse hypostatico incommunicabili, ab opinione sancti doctoris, a qua multum secundum uerba uidentur discordare, nihil discordant.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18577

English

Those who hold that unbegottenness is a positive property constituting the Father in an incommunicable hypostatic existence are, in nothing, at variance with the holy doctor's opinion, from which in their wording they seem to differ greatly. unverified draft

'innascibilitas' rendered 'unbegottenness', the standard term for the Father's Trinitarian property, rather than a literal 'inability to be born'.
