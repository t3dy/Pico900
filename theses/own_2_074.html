Latin

Dico secundum Thommam dicendum esse in actu reflexo intellectus

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20099

English

I hold that, according to Thomas, it must be said that our beatitude consists in a reflexive act of the intellect. unverified draft

Farmer's note on this thesis paraphrases the 'reflexive act of the intellect' as the intellective soul contemplating itself (farmer1998:20136); the Latin attributes the claim explicitly to Thomas ('secundum Thommam'), so pico_stance is given as unstated (farmer1998:20099).
