Latin

Ex dicto illo Platonis in Phedro, quod nisi anima hominis ea que uere sunt intuita esset, in hoc animal non uenisset, si recte intelligatur, intelligetur quod opinio Plotini ponens transmigrationem animarum in bruta non est ad mentem Platonis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23083

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 Phedro: quod nisi ... venisset. Si recte

English

From that saying of Plato's in the Phaedrus, that unless a man's soul had beheld the things that truly are, it would not have come into this animal, if it is rightly understood, it will be understood that Plotinus's opinion, positing the transmigration of souls into brute beasts, is not in accordance with Plato's mind. unverified draft
