Doctrine

The thesis restricts the metaphysician's proper object to quiddities taken universally, not as instantiated in particular individuals; Edelheit calls this 'an obvious conclusion which can be drawn from different discussions of Thomas — following Aristotle — regarding philosophical disciplines and their proper objects' (edelheit2022:12745), and reports finding in Ockham's own presentation of Thomas's view 'a formulation which is almost identical to Pico's thesis' (edelheit2022:12745).

Historiography

Edelheit (2022) reads thesis 42 as a standard restatement of a Thomistic-Aristotelian position on the metaphysician's proper object, noting its near-identity to a formulation he finds in Ockham's presentation of Thomas (edelheit2022:12745).
