Thesis 10>10

Latin

Qui praecedentis conclusionis opus attentauerit, adhibeat opus cabalae secundum appropriata timori Isaac.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25782

English

One who undertakes the operation of the preceding conclusion must join to it a work of Cabala drawn from what is assigned to the fear of Isaac.

Sense checked against farmer1998:25837; no divergence.
