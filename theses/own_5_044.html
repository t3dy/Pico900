Plato · thesis 5>44

Thesis 5>44

Latin

Cur homo non possit non uideri si sit presens a recte disposito in potentia uisiua, demon autem tunc solum uideatur cum uult uideri, ex modo quo unusquisque eorum uidetur haberi potest.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22892

English

Consider the manner in which each is seen, and you will grasp why a man, once present before someone rightly equipped to see, cannot help being seen, whereas a demon becomes visible only when it chooses to be.
