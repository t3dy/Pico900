Latin

Cur homo non possit non uideri si sit presens a recte disposito in potentia uisiua, demon autem tunc solum uideatur cum uult uideri, ex modo quo unusquisque eorum uidetur haberi potest.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22892

English

Why a man cannot fail to be seen if he is present to one rightly disposed in the visual faculty, while a demon is seen only when it wishes to be seen, can be understood from the manner in which each of them is seen. unverified draft
