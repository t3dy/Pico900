Latin

Vnumquodque in natura intellectuali habet a sua intelligibili unitate et quod quodlibet sibi uniat, et quod immaculatam ac inpermixtam sibi suam seruet proprietatem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20542

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 habet’ (typographical error not = habetur) | 1487 habet

English

Each thing in intellectual nature has, from its intelligible unity, both that it unites everything to itself, and that it keeps its own property immaculate and unmixed. unverified draft
