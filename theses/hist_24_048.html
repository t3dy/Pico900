Latin

Sicut prima trinitas post unitatem est omnia intelligibiliter, commensurate, et finiformiter, ita secunda trinitas est omnia uitaliter, uere, et infinitiformiter. Tertia est omne secundum mixti proprietatem et pulchriformiter.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16705

English

Just as the first triad after the primal unity is all things intelligibly, commensurately, and in the mode of the finite, so the second triad is all things vitally, truly, and in the mode of the infinite. The third is all things according to the property of the mixed, and in the mode of beauty. unverified draft
