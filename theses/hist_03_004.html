Francis of Meyronnes · thesis 3.4

Thesis 3.4

Latin

Ens denominatiue dicitur de deo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11984

English

Being is said of God only denominatively.

'denominatiue': predicated accidentally or by derivation rather than substantially (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12052). Sense checked against farmer1998:12039.
