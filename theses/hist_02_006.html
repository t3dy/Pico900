Latin

Ex diuina bonitate potest sumi ratio praedestinationis aliquorum et reprobationis aliorum, et sola diuina uoluntas est ratio quod istos reprobet et illos eligat in gloriam.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11324

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486, 1487 summi

English

From divine goodness the reason for the predestination of some and the reprobation of others can be drawn, and the divine will alone is the reason he reprobates these and elects those to glory. unverified draft

Doctrine

The thesis holds that the reason for predestining some and reprobating others is drawn from divine goodness, while the divine will alone accounts for who is reprobated and who elected (farmer1998:11324). Edelheit reports that Pico states that the reason of the predestination of some and the reprobation of others can be inferred from divine goodness, and that only the divine will is the reason for condemning the latter and electing the former to glory, locating the thesis in Thomas's Summa theologiae, 1, question 23, article 5 (edelheit2022:10367). Farmer groups this thesis with 2.2-9 under the divine omnipotence/free will problem (farmer1998:11348).
