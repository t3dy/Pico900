Latin

Rationabile est secundum philosophiam omnem seriem animalem in proprio beatificari intellectu; quod tamen non assertiue sed probabiliter dictum sit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21348

English

Philosophically speaking, it makes sense that each series of souls reaches beatitude in its own intellect; let this, though, count only as a probable claim, not an assertion. unverified draft

The Latin itself hedges the claim ('non assertiue sed probabiliter'), which is why pico_stance is marked unstated rather than endorses.
