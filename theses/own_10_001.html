Latin

Sicut secretam magiam a nobis primum ex Orphei hymnis elicitam fas non est in publicum explicare, ita nutu quodam, ut in infrascriptis fiet conclusionibus, eam per amphorismorum capita demonstrasse, utile erit ad excitandas contemplatiuorum mentes.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25569

The sheet's OCR text joined 'elicitam' and 'fas' as 'elicitam_fas'; restored as two words.

English

The secret magic I have drawn for the first time out of the hymns of Orpheus cannot rightly be set out openly; hinting at it instead through the headings of aphorisms, in the conclusions that follow, will still serve to stir the minds of those given to contemplation. unverified draft
