Mathematics · thesis 7>4

Thesis 7>4

Latin

Sicut subiecta mathematicorum, si absolute accipiantur, intellectum nihil perficiunt, ita si ut imagines accipiantur superiorum, immediate nos ad intelligibilium speculationem manu ducunt.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23623

English

Just as the objects of the mathematical sciences, taken in an absolute sense, bring the intellect to no perfection, so, taken as images of higher things, they lead us directly, as though by the hand, to the contemplation of the intelligible.
