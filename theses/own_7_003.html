Mathematics · thesis 7>3

Thesis 7>3

Latin

Mathematicae scientiae non sunt propter se, sed ut uia ad alias scientias quaerendae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23620

English

The mathematical sciences are to be pursued not for their own sake, but as a pathway toward the pursuit of other sciences.

Sense checked against Farmer's English at farmer1998:23673. Recast as a gerundive ('are to be pursued... as a pathway toward') to render 'quaerendae' and vary the wording.
