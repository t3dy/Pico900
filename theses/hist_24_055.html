Latin

Sicut intellectus perfectus ab intelligibilibus quaerendus est, ita uirtus sursum ductiua ab intellectualibus; operatio absoluta et sequestrata a materiaab ultramundanis; uita alata a mundanis; expressio diuinorum uera ab angelicis choris; repletio eius, quae a diis est aspirationis, a bonis daemonibus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16822

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 sequaestrata

English

Just as perfect intellect is to be sought from the intelligibles, so the power that leads upward is to be sought from the intellectuals; activity absolute and set apart from matter, from the ultramundane beings; winged life, from the mundane beings; the true expression of things divine, from the angelic choirs; the fulfillment of the inspiration that comes from the gods, from the good daemons. unverified draft
