Aeuum est supra per se animal in secunda et media trinitate.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16714

English

The aevum lies above the animal per se, in the second, the middle triad. unverified draft

'Aeuum' is the scholastic 'aevum'; left untranslated in the body of the rendering because no English word fits without gloss. 'per se animal' as in 24.39.
