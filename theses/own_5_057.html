Latin

Dictum illud Platonis in Sophiste, de simulachris quae dicit demonica machinatione conficta, et si multis aliis modis possit uerificari, conueniens tamen est exponere per hoc: quod illa, ut gradum medium in entitate tenent, demonico ordini proportionantur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23192

Apparatus (Farmer): colon retained from 1486 edition

English

That saying of Plato's in the Sophist, concerning the images which he says are wrought by demonic contrivance, even though it can be verified in many other ways, is nonetheless fittingly explained by this: that, holding as they do a middle rank in being, they are proportioned to the demonic order. unverified draft

'demonica machinatione' is rendered 'demonic contrivance', glossing the Sophist's discussion of images (eidola), at farmer1998:23192.
