Doctrine

The thesis continues the series begun at thesis 4.16 on the nature of relation, asserting that every relation other than the creature-to-God relation just discussed is really distinguished from its foundation (farmer1998:12365). Farmer treats theses 4.16–17 together as raising 'an elaborate series on the nature of "relations" in scholastic philosophy' and cross-references the same cluster of historical theses, 5.11–13, 6.1, 7.24, 7.39 and 13.1 (farmer1998:12399). Edelheit states that thesis 17 derives, to some extent, from the same discussion as thesis 16, and locates the specific point in Scotus's Lectura, book 1, distinction 31, question unica, where only in God and among the Persons of the Trinity is there a perfect identity of relation and foundation, so that any other relation is really (realiter) distinguished from a foundation (edelheit2022:14811, 14815).

Historiography

Edelheit's identification of the Lectura passage, and his observation that thesis 17 continues the discussion behind thesis 16, are the only scholarly treatment of the thesis in the material consulted (edelheit2022:14811).
