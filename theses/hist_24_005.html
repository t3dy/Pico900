Latin

In intelligibilibus non est numerus, sed multitudo, et numerorum causa paternalis ac maternalis; in intellectualibus uero est numerus secundum essentiam et communicatiue multitudo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15958

English

Among the intelligibles there is no number, but multitude, and the paternal and maternal cause of numbers; among the intellectual beings, however, there is number according to essence, and multitude by communication. unverified draft
