Thesis 7>11

Latin

Per numeros habetur uia ad omnis scibilis inuestigationem et in/tellectionem; ad cuius conclusionis uerificationem polliceor me ad infrascriptas lxxiiii. questiones per uiam numerorum responsurum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23732; 1486 print folio <26r/26v>

The copy-text's OCR gives the numeral as 'xxiii.' (23); corrected here to 'lxxiiii.' (74), since Farmer's own English renders it 'seventy-four questions' (farmer1998:23784) and the list that follows (7a>1-7a>74) runs to seventy-four items.

English

Numbers supply a road to the investigation and understanding of everything knowable; to prove this conclusion I undertake to answer the seventy-four questions written below by the way of numbers.
