Mercury Trismegistus · thesis 27.9

Thesis 27.9

Latin

Decem intra unumquemque sunt ultores: ignorantia, tristitia, inconstantia, cupiditas, iniustitia, luxuries, inuidia, fraus, ira, malitia.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17358

Apparatus (Farmer): colon retained from 1486 edition | 1486 luxuries: deceptio: ... ira: temeritas: | Emendationes errorum, dele dictiones deceptio: temeritas: quas superflue ponuntur | 1487 text emended sic

Colon retained from the 1486 edition; 1486 reads 'luxuries: deceptio: ... ira: temeritas:', which the Emendationes errorum direct be deleted as superfluous, and the 1487 text is emended accordingly (farmer1998:17370).

English

Within each person there are ten avengers: ignorance, sorrow, inconstancy, desire, injustice, excess, envy, deceit, anger, malice.
