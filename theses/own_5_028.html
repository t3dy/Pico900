Latin

Cum dixit Plato in Timeo ex indiuidua diuiduaque substantia conflatam animam, per indiuiduam substantiam animalem intellectum significauit, per diuiduam animalem rationem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22655

English

When Plato said in the Timaeus that the soul is compounded from an indivisible and a divisible substance, by the indivisible substance he meant the animal intellect, and by the divisible, the animal reason. unverified draft

'indiuidua diuiduaque substantia' echoes Timaeus 35a on the soul's indivisible and divisible portions, at farmer1998:22655.
