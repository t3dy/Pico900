Latin

Ipseitas uniuscuiusque tunc maxime est ipsa, cum in ipsa ita sunt omnia, ut in ipsa omnia sint ipsa.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20547

English

The selfhood of each thing is then most itself, when all things exist in it in such a way that in it all things are itself. unverified draft

"ipseitas"/"ipsa" (selfhood/itself) is kept as Pico's coinage; the clause turns on the reflexive sense in which a thing's identity is most fully realized when what is other is, within it, not other (farmer1998:20547).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch13, from Latin at farmer1998:20547

Attribution: Pico's own opinion · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

The thesis holds that a thing's selfhood (ipseitas) is most fully itself precisely when all things exist within it in a manner identical to itself (farmer1998:20547). Farmer places it in a series on the principle of individuation that also includes 2.26, from Thomas Aquinas, on signate matter as the principle of individuation, and 4.6, from Scotus, on individuating difference or "haecceitas" (farmer1998:11656; farmer1998:12236; farmer1998:20580).

Context

Farmer's note directs the reader to his own discussion, pages 113-14 of this thesis within the series on individuation, and links it to the immediately preceding 3>19, which holds that each thing in the intellectual nature has its unity from its own intelligible unity and preserves its own property unmixed (farmer1998:20542; farmer1998:20580).

unverified draft

Historiography

Georgios Steiris quotes the thesis's closing clause verbatim while discussing Pico's reading of Anaxagoras, glossing it in English as "when in itself all things exist in such a way that in itself all things are itself", and aligns it with the later Neoplatonic formula that all things are in all things, each according to its own proper nature, as Proclus states it in the Institutio Theologica (steiris2014:37; steiris2014:152). The same clause also appears, without separate discussion, in a footnote of the German study Erkenntnistheorie und christliche Kabbalah (kabbalah_paradigma:1701).
