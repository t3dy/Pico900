Latin

Ipseitas uniuscuiusque tunc maxime est ipsa, cum in ipsa ita sunt omnia, ut in ipsa omnia sint ipsa.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20547

English

The selfhood of each and every thing is most fully itself precisely when all things exist within it in such a way that, within it, all things are itself. unverified draft
