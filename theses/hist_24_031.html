Proclus · thesis 24.31

Thesis 24.31

Latin

Secundum precedentem conclusionem ex Procli mente potest exponi unum ex dictis Zoroastris secundum quod apud Graecos legitur, quanquam apud Chaldeos aliter et legitur et exponitur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16470

English

In accordance with the preceding conclusion, one of the sayings of Zoroaster, as it is read among the Greeks, can be expounded from the mind of Proclus, although among the Chaldeans it is both read and expounded differently.

'ex Procli mente' rendered 'from the mind of Proclus'. Sense checked against farmer1998:16529; no further crux.
