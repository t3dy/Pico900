Latin

Qui annotauerit diligenter dicta ab aristotele in expositione definitionis de anima, uidebit cur Orpheus Palladi et Veneri uigilantiam attribuerit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 26139

English

He who diligently notes what was said by Aristotle in his exposition of the definition of the soul will see why Orpheus attributed wakefulness to Pallas and Venus. unverified draft
