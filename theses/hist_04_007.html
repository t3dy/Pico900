Latin

Ens dicitur de deo et creatura uniuoce in quid.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12240

English

Being is predicated univocally, and quidditatively, of both God and creature. unverified draft

Reflects the chief Thomist-Scotist conflict Pico planned to resolve at Rome (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12275). Sense checked against farmer1998:12300.
