Thesis 7.15

Latin

Vniuersalia sunt ex parte rei in potentia tantum, actu autem per operationem animae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13149

English

On the side of the thing, universals exist only potentially; they exist in act only through an operation of the soul.
