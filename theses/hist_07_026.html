Averroes · thesis 7.26

Thesis 7.26

Latin

Propositio necessaria quae ab Aristotele in libro Priorum contra possibilem et inuentam distinguitur, est illa quae est ex terminis necessariis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13282

English

What makes a proposition necessary, on the distinction Aristotle draws in the Prior Analytics against the possible and the contingent, is that it is built from necessary terms.
