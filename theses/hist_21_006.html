Latin

Quia sicut dixit Abdala, videre somnia est fortitudo imaginationis, intelligere ea est fortitudo intellectus, ideo qui uidet ea ut plurimum non intelligit ea.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15358

English

Because, as Abdala said, to see dreams is a strength of the imagination, and to understand them a strength of the intellect, it follows that those who see them for the most part do not understand them. unverified draft
