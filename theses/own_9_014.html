Latin

Siqua est natura immediata nobis quae sit uel simpliciter_uel saltem ut multum rationaliter rationalis, magicam habet in summo, et eius participatione potest in hominibus esse perfectior.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25283

OCR text runs 'simpliciter_uel' together for 'simpliciter vel' (farmer1998:25283).

English

If there is any nature immediate to us that is either simply rational, or at least so in a highly rational way, it possesses magic in the highest degree, and by participation in it, magic can become more perfect in men. unverified draft
