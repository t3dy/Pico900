Latin

Sub secundo ducalis trinitatis est uita et generatio, omnis anima sub generationem ueniens, erraticae spherae, aqua et infima aeris pars, quae sub antra minerae et terremotus, media pars terrae et circa centrum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16380

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 generatio. Omnis

English

Under the second member of the leader-triad fall: life and generation, every soul that comes under generation, the wandering (planetary) spheres, water and the lowest part of air, the things beneath the mineral caverns and earthquakes, the middle part of the earth, and the region around the center. unverified draft
