Latin

Licet ad receptionem speciei concurrat sensus passiue, ad iudicandum tamen de sensibili actiue concurrit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11149

English

Although sense cooperates passively in receiving a species, it cooperates actively in judging the sensible object. unverified draft
