Adeland the Arab · thesis 21.1

Thesis 21.1

Latin

Intellectus agens nihil est aliud quam pars animae quae sursum manet et non cadit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15279

English

The agent intellect is nothing other than the part of the soul that remains above and does not fall.

No English translation is given for this thesis in Farmer's edition, whose note there (farmer1998:15316) gives a close paraphrase of the same thesis; translated directly from the Latin.
