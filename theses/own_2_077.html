Latin

Quod dicitur a communi schola philosophantium omnium latinorum de prima operatione intellectus error est, quia non est alia operatio partis rationalis quam illae duae quas ipsi secundam et tertiam ponunt, compositio scilicet et discursus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20112

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486, 1487 scola

The sheet reads 'quar' for 'quam'; an obvious OCR misreading, restored to 'quam' (farmer1998:20112).

English

What the common school of all the Latin philosophers says about the first operation of the intellect is an error, since there is no operation of the rational part other than the two they themselves posit as second and third, namely composition and discursive reasoning. unverified draft
