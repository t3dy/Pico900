Latin

Intentio dici de omni secundum Aristotelem est talis quod praedicatum dicitur de subiecto, et de omni eo quod est subiectum actu uel potentia, possibilitate contingentiae, non necessitatis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13789

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 que praedicatum | Emendationes errorum, corrige: quod praedicatum | 1487 text emended sic

English

The sense of 'predicated of all' according to Aristotle is such that the predicate is predicated of the subject, and of everything that is the subject either actually or potentially, with a contingent possibility, not a necessary one. unverified draft

'dici de omni' (the dictum de omni) is the technical term from the Prior Analytics for a predicate holding of every instance of a subject term (farmer1998:13789).
