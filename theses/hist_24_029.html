Latin

Sub tertio eiusdem ordinis est specialium diuisionum conuersio, animarum purgatio, sphera actiuorum et passiuorum, terra cum terrestribus, quae circa Tartarum, et pars occidentalis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16385

English

Under the third member of that same order fall: the reversion of special divisions, the purification of souls, the sphere of active and passive things, the earth together with earthly things, the things around Tartarus, and the western region. unverified draft
