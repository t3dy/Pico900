Thesis 17.1

Latin

Cognoscere actum suum non est commune cuilibet sensui exteriori, sed humanis sensibus est speciale.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14546

English

To know its own act is not shared by every external sense, but belongs distinctively to the human senses.
