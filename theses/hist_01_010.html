Latin

Obiectum per se et proprie sensus communis est magnitudo, ut bene dixit Auicenna.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11053; 1486 print folio <1r/1v>

English

The object of common sense, taken per se and properly, is magnitude, as Avicenna rightly said. unverified draft

'sensus communis': the faculty coordinating the several senses' perception of shared objects such as magnitude (Farmer's note, farmer1998:11125). Sense checked against farmer1998:11108.
