Latin

Exemplum Aristotelis in secundo Methaphysice, de nicticorace respectu solis, non denotat impossibilitatem, sed difficultatem, alioquin natura aliquid ociose egisset.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13277

English

Aristotle's example in Metaphysics Book 2, of the night-owl in relation to the sun, denotes not impossibility but difficulty; otherwise nature would have acted in vain. unverified draft

'Nicticorax' (night-owl/night-raven) refers to Aristotle's simile at Metaphysics 993b9-11 comparing our intellect before first principles to an owl's eyes before daylight (farmer1998:13277).
