Latin

Exemplum Aristotelis in secundo Methaphysice, de nicticorace respectu solis, non denotat impossibilitatem, sed difficultatem, alioquin natura aliquid ociose egisset.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13277

English

Aristotle's example in the second book of the Metaphysics, of the night-bird's relation to the sun, points to a difficulty rather than an impossibility; otherwise nature would have acted to no purpose in something. unverified draft

'nicticorax' left as 'night-bird'; Farmer's note at farmer1998:13308 surveys the rival identifications (owl, night-raven, night-heron, bat) and settles on 'owl' for metaphorical reasons — this translation leaves that identification open.
