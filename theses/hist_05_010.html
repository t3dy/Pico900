Doctrine

Edelheit reports that the thesis simply states that friendship is a virtue, and that Henry deals with it at Quodlibet X, question 12, providing a philosophical account of friendship using mainly the authorities of Aristotle and Cicero, and maintaining that friendship is indeed a virtue (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15679).
