Non fit assimilatio ad diuina per uirtutes etiam purgati animi nisi dispositiue.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15112

English

Even a purified soul is not made like the divine by the virtues themselves; the virtues only prepare it for that likeness. unverified draft
