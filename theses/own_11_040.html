Thesis 11>40

Latin

Hoc habent ineuitabiliter concedere Cabaliste, quod uerus messias per aquam homines purgabit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27231

English

There is no escaping it for the Cabalists: the true Messiah will cleanse mankind by water.

Sense checked; no further crux. Farmer cross-references 11>44-45 for the same water/fire pairing (farmer1998:27305).
