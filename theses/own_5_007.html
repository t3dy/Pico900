Plato · thesis 5>7

Thesis 5>7

Latin

Cum dicit Plato in Timao in medio mundi positam animam, quicquid dicant caeteri Platonici, ego per medium lunam intelligo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22237

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 coeten

1486 print reads 'coeten' for 'caeteri' (farmer1998:22247).

English

When Plato says in the Timaeus that the soul is placed in the middle of the world, I—whatever the other Platonists may say—understand the moon by 'the middle.'
