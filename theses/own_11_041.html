Latin

Sciri potest in Cabalam per mysterium mem clausi cur post se Christus miserit paraclytum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27235; 1486 print folio <33r/33v>

English

Through the mystery of the closed mem, the Cabala lets us know why Christ, once gone, sent the Paraclete after him. unverified draft

The 'closed mem' is the final form of the Hebrew letter mem, which Jewish commentary noted occurs only once outside word-final position, in Isa. 9:6, a verse both Jewish and Christian readers took as messianic (farmer1998:27307).
