Latin

Sapientes Israhel post cessationem prophetiae per spiritum, prophetarunt per filiam uocis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18402

English

The wise men of Israel, after the cessation of prophecy through the spirit, prophesied through the daughter of the voice. unverified draft

'filiam uocis' is kept literal, 'daughter of the voice', calquing the rabbinic bat qol; Farmer glosses 'spirit'/'daughter of the voice' as higher and lower modes of prophecy flowing from the tenth sefirah, 'kingdom' (paraphrase, farmer1998:18429).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch12, from Latin at farmer1998:18402

Attribution: Reports Hebrew Cabalists (farmer1998:18402). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer's note, following the passage from Recanati cited by Wirszubski (1989: 51), glosses 'spirit' and 'daughter of the voice' as higher and lower modes of prophecy flowing from 'kingdom', the tenth and lowest sefirah, and states that the scheme fits a general view in Pico's historical theses of a systematic degradation of wisdom over time (paraphrase, farmer1998:18429).

Historiography
