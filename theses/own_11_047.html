Latin

Qui sciet proprietatem Aquilonis in cabala, sciet cur sathan Christo promisit regna mundi, si cadens eum adorasset.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27343

English

Whoever knows what the north signifies in the Cabala will know why Satan offered Christ the kingdoms of the world, on condition that he fall down and worship him. unverified draft

'north' is the standard symbol of the fifth sefirah, one of whose properties is power, and which was regularly associated with Satan and the origin of evil (farmer1998:27408).
