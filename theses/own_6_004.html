Latin

Quamuis dicat Abucaten quod esse, quod est primum creatum, est super intelligentiam, non credas tamen illud secundum hypostasim esse distinctum ab intelligentia.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23447

English

Although Abucaten says that being, which is the first created thing, is above intelligence, do not believe that it is on that account distinct from intelligence according to hypostasis. unverified draft

'hypostasim' (hypostasis) is kept as the technical term for a really distinct subsistent, at farmer1998:23447.
