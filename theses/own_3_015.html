Latin

Contradictoria coincidunt in natura uniali.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20452

English

Contradictories coincide in the unial nature. unverified draft

'uniali' is an adjective Pico coins from 'unum' ('the One'), distinct from 'unitas' ('unity'); rendered here as 'unial' rather than smoothed into 'unified' or 'one', to preserve the coinage that Farmer's note ties to a distinctively Neoplatonic register in this run of theses (paraphrase, farmer1998:20526).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch12, from Latin at farmer1998:20452

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (farmer1998:20452). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

Farmer's note states that this thesis is 'regularly cited (originally by Cassirer and most recently by Wirszubski) as evidence of the influence on Pico of Nicholas of Cusa's coincidentia oppositorum' (farmer1998:20526). He argues against that reading, stating that 'the idea expressed both by Pico and by Cusanus was a scholastic commonplace', whose syncretic origins he discusses elsewhere in his book, and directs the reader to thesis 2.31 for the bearing of this run of theses on traditional logic (paraphrase and quotation, farmer1998:20526).
