Orphic Hymns · thesis 10>18

Thesis 10>18

Latin

Anima aquea, ut inferiora generat, superiora contemplatur in seipsa se sistit, triplici hymno maris, Neptunni, et oceani ab orpheo decantatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25904

English

The watery soul, even as it brings the lower things into being, contemplates the higher things it holds within itself, a truth Orpheus sings in his three hymns to the sea, to Neptune, and to Ocean.
