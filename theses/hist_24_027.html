Proclus · thesis 24.27

Thesis 24.27

Latin

Sub primo ducalis trinitatis est esse substantiale, omnis anima nobilis generationi non subdita, primum mobile, ignis et suprema aeris pars, quae super terram crescunt, et pars orientalis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16375; 1486 print folio <10r/10v>

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 substantiale. Omnis

English

Beneath the first member of the leaders' trinity lie substantial being, every noble soul not subject to generation, the first mobile, fire and the uppermost part of the air, the things that grow up above the earth, and the eastern part.

Farmer's page has no English text at a separate thesis line for 24.27 in the packet ('None'); sense checked instead against the English given at farmer1998:16428 (which the packet lists among Farmer's notes). 'primum mobile' kept as 'first mobile'; 'quae super terram crescunt' taken as 'the things that grow above the earth'. The 1486 apparatus punctuates 'substantiale. Omnis' (farmer1998:16398).
