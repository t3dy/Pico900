Henry of Ghent · thesis 5.11

Thesis 5.11

Latin

Ratitudo formaliter cuiuslibet creati est respectus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12610

English

The 'ratitude' — the confirmed, settled being — of every created thing is, formally, a relation.

'ratitudo' is a rare scholastic coinage from 'ratus' (fixed, ratified, valid); Farmer renders it 'rectitude' (farmer1998:12640), which may reflect a different reading or an association with 'rectitudo'. Which term Henry intended is not established without the critical apparatus of his own edition. Sense checked against farmer1998:12640.
