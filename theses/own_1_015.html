Latin

In quaestione de contingentia ad utrumlibet in materia de casu et fortuna, re et fundamentaliter non discordant Auenrois et Auicenna, licet superficie tenus et in uerbis eorum oppositum appareat.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18797

English

On the question of contingency to either outcome in the matter of chance and fortune, Averroes and Avicenna are not, in fact and at bottom, in disagreement, although on the surface and in their wording the contrary may appear true. unverified draft
