Latin

Propositio est per se cum uel subiectum diffinit praedicatum, uel praedicatum subiectum, uel ambo diffiniuntur per idem tertium.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14898

English

A proposition holds per se whenever the subject supplies the definition of the predicate, or the predicate that of the subject, or else a single third term defines them both. unverified draft
