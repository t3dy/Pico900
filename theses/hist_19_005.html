Latin

Propositio est per se cum uel subiectum diffinit praedicatum, uel praedicatum subiectum, uel ambo diffiniuntur per idem tertium.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14898

English

A proposition is per se when either the subject defines the predicate, or the predicate the subject, or both are defined through the same third [term]. unverified draft
