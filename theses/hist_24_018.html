Latin

Sicut paternalis proprietas est solum in intelligibilibus, ita conditoria siue factiua solum in deis nouis; paterna simul et conditoriain exemplari intelligibili; conditoriaet paterna in opifici.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16171

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 paterna inopificia. | 1487 paterna inopifitia.

English

Just as the paternal property exists only in the intelligible gods, so the creative, or productive, property exists only in the new gods; the paternal and the creative together are in the intelligible exemplar; the creative and the paternal together are in the demiurge. unverified draft
