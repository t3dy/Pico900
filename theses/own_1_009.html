Latin

In materia quid prius cognoscatur magis an minus uniuersale concordant Thommas et Scotus, qui maxime in ea discordare existimantur; de qua pono infrascriptas tres conclusiones ex utriusque mente.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18693

English

On the question of whether what is more or less universal is known first, Thomas and Scotus — thought to disagree on this above all — agree; on which I set down the three conclusions written below, drawn from the thought of both. unverified draft
