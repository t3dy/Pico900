Thesis 28.14

Latin

Qui nouerit proprietatem meridionalem in dextrali coordinatione, sciet cur omnis profectio Abraam semper fit uersus austrum. <13r/13v>

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17745; 1486 print folio <13r/13v>

English

Knowing the southern property within the right-hand order explains why Abraham's every journey tended always southward.
