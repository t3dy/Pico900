Alexander of Aphrodisias · thesis 18.8

Thesis 18.8

Latin

Methaphysicus et dialecticus aeque de omnibus disputant, sed ille demonstratiue, hic probabiliter.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14833

English

Both the metaphysician and the dialectician take every subject as their province, but one argues by demonstration and the other only by probability.
