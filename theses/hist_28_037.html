Latin

Per secretum orationis antelucanae, nihil aliud debemus intelligere quam proprietatem pietatis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18201

English

By the secret of the prayer before dawn we ought to understand nothing other than the property of piety. unverified draft

'antelucanae' ('before dawn, early-morning') and 'proprietatem pietatis' ('the property of piety') are rendered literally; Farmer glosses 'piety' as naming the fourth sefirah (paraphrase, farmer1998:18275).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch12, from Latin at farmer1998:18201

Attribution: Reports Hebrew Cabalists (farmer1998:18201). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer's note glosses 'early morning prayer' as an allusion to Abraham's early rising in Genesis 19:27, and 'piety' as the fourth sefirah, elsewhere symbolized by 'Abraham' (paraphrase, farmer1998:18275). He states that Pico would have used theses of this kind to underline the affinities he saw between the sefirotic system and its scriptural correlates, though the note as excerpted does not complete the comparison (paraphrase, farmer1998:18275).
