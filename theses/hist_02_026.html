Thesis 2.26

Latin

Materia signata est principium indiuiduationis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11656

Sheet's underscore in 'signata_est' corrected to a space.

English

Matter, once determined by form, is the principle of individuation.

'materia signata': matter as marked out and limited by form (Farmer's note, farmer1998:11733). Sense checked against farmer1998:11712.
