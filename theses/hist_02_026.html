Doctrine

The thesis states Thomas's doctrine that designated matter -- matter as determined and limited by form -- is what individuates a species into distinct particulars (farmer1998:11733; edelheit2022:11830). Edelheit locates the discussion in Thomas's Summa theologiae 1, question 75, article 4, on whether the soul is the human being, where Thomas holds that a thing's definition signifies not form alone but form together with matter (edelheit2022:11830; edelheit2022:11854).

Context

Farmer's note opposes the thesis to thesis 4.6, reported from Scotus, which grounds individuation instead in a proper individual difference or 'haecceitas', and refers to 3>20 among Pico's own opinions for Pico's own view of individuation (farmer1998:11733).
