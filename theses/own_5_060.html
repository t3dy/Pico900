Latin

Per id quod in Lachete dicitur: Quorumcumque est scientia non esse aliam eorundem ut preteritorum, aliam ut presentium, aliam ut futurorum, illud potest intelligi tritum apud peripateticos, non esse scientiam nisi uniuersalium.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23209

English

The Laches teaches that, for anything we know, our knowledge of it does not change in kind depending on whether the thing is past, present, or future; from this we can grasp the Peripatetics' familiar maxim, that there is no science except of universals. unverified draft
