Latin

Nullum est quaesitum naturale aut diuinum in quo Aristoteles et Plato sensu et re non conueniant, quamuis uerbis dissentire uideantur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18502

The sheet's transcription fuses 'diuinum in' into 'diuinumin'; restored here as two words.

English

No question, whether about nature or about God, exists on which Aristotle and Plato are not at one in meaning and in fact, however much they may appear, in their wording, to be at odds. unverified draft

'sensu et re' rendered 'in meaning and in fact', kept distinct from Farmer's 'meaning and substance'.
