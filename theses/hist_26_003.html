Latin

Substantiales rerum uisibilium qualitates non a uirtute separata particulari, ut credunt aegyptii, sed a primo receptaculo fontis luminum per animalem splendorem dependenter resultant.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17134

English

The substantial qualities of visible things do not result from a particular separated power, as the Egyptians believe, but depend, by way of an animate splendor, on the first receptacle of the font of lights. unverified draft
