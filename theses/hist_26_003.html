Latin

Substantiales rerum uisibilium qualitates non a uirtute separata particulari, ut credunt aegyptii, sed a primo receptaculo fontis luminum per animalem splendorem dependenter resultant.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17134

English

The substantial qualities of visible things do not result, as the Egyptians believe, from a particular separate power, but dependently from the first receptacle of the fount of lights, through an ensouled splendor. unverified draft
