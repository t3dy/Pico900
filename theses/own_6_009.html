Latin

Ex antepenultima propositione Abucaten colligi potest quod declinare plus ad sensum quam ad intellectum non est animae ut anima est, sed ut cadens est.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23548

English

From Abucaten's third-to-last proposition it can be gathered that inclining more toward sense than toward intellect does not belong to the soul insofar as it is a soul, but insofar as it is falling. unverified draft
