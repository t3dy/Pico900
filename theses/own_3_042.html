Thesis 3>42

Latin

Infinitas dei per superexcedentiam ad esse intellectuale et uiam mysticae theologiae probari potest, et ad id probandum omnis alia uia inefficax est.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20881

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 super excedentiam | Emendationes errorum, corrige: per superexcedentiam 1487 text emended sic

The 1486 print read "super excedentiam" as two words; the errata correct it to the single word "superexcedentiam" (farmer1998:20897).

English

The infinity of God can be proved through its transcendence relative to intellectual being and through the way of mystical theology, and for proving it every other way is ineffective. unverified draft

"superexcedentiam" (transcendence/superexceeding) is kept close to the Latin coinage rather than smoothed into ordinary English (farmer1998:20881).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch13, from Latin at farmer1998:20881

Attribution: Pico's own opinion · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

The thesis holds that God's infinity can be proved only through its transcendence relative to intellectual being and through the way of mystical theology, and that every other way of proving it fails (farmer1998:20881). Farmer's note identifies "the method of the Mystical Theology" as Pseudo-Dionysius's negative way, referring especially to chapter 3 of his Mystical Theology, and directs the reader to his note on 7.18 for related theses (farmer1998:20909; farmer1998:13165).
