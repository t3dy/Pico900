Albert the Great · thesis 1.2

Thesis 1.2

Latin

Corruptis omnibus indiuiduis specie humanae haec est uera: homo est animal.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 10929

English

Should every individual of the human species perish, the proposition 'man is an animal' would still hold true.
