Latin

Eadem quae Rhea dicitur ut Saturno coexistens secundum suam summitatem, ut Iouem producit et cum Ioue totales et partiales deorum ordines, dicitur Caeres.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16161; 1486 print folio <9v/10r>

English

The same goddess who is called Rhea insofar as she coexists with Saturn according to her summit is called Ceres insofar as she produces Jupiter and, together with Jupiter, the total and partial orders of the gods. unverified draft
