Doctrine

The thesis holds that the whole substance of the rational soul is an intellectual part (farmer1998:21257).

unverified draft

Historiography

The German study Erkenntnistheorie und christliche Kabbalah lists the thesis together with 3>60 ("Nihil intelligit actu et distincte anima, nisi se ipsam") and 3>66 in a note on Pico's account of the soul's self-understanding, and separately alongside 3>52 in a note citing Roulier's Jean Pic de la Mirandole and Farmer's own discussion at pages 112-14 of his Syncretism in the West, without further comment on 3>61 itself (kabbalah_paradigma:1695; kabbalah_paradigma:1765).
