Latin

Duplex genus demonum de quibus dixit secundum Porphirium praecedens conclusio nihil credimus esse aliud quam membrorum legem et potestates harum tenebrarum, de quibus apud Paulum, quamuis de quiditate et substantia harum potestatum eos non credo conuenire.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15554

English

The twofold kind of demons of which the preceding conclusion spoke, following Porphyry, I believe to be nothing other than the law in the members and the powers of this darkness spoken of in Paul, although I do not believe that they agree about the quiddity and substance of these powers. unverified draft
