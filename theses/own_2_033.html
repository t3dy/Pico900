Latin

Possibilis est regressus a causa ad effectum negatione quam somniat Burleus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 19327

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 absque negatioe | 1486 Emendationes errorum, ubi scribitur : absque negatôe (sic) : Corrige: negatione | 1487 absque negotiatione

The 1486 text and its own Emendationes errorum disagree over this word (printing, then correcting to, 'negatione'); the 1487 reprint instead prints 'negotiatione'. Farmer's note judges the 1487 reading a mistaken emendation and argues the list of errors in fact means to delete 'absque' from the thesis; 'negatione', as given in the sheet, is followed here.

English

A regress from cause to effect is possible, even by the negation that Burley dreams of. unverified draft

'Burleus' = Walter Burley (d. c. 1343). The crux over 'negatione'/'negotiatione' is Farmer's own and is not resolved here beyond following his preferred reading; worth a human Latinist's eye.
