Thesis 3>6

Latin

Sicut cognitio per demonstrationem habita homini, pro communi statu quem hic experimur, est perfectissima cognitio, ita simpliciter inter cognitiones est imperfectissima.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20336

English

Knowledge gained by demonstration is, for the common condition we experience here, the most perfect knowledge available to man; yet considered absolutely, among all kinds of knowledge it is the least perfect.

Contrasts a relative perfection (for our present state) with knowledge's absolute rank; sense checked.
