Thesis 3>6

Latin

Sicut cognitio per demonstrationem habita bomini, pro communi statu quem hic experimur, est perfectissima cognitio, ita simpliciter inter cognitiones est imperfectissima.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20336

Farmer's transcription reads 'bomini' (farmer1998:20336); the Dougherty anthology quotes the same conclusio, citing Farmer's own edition and translation at pages 400-1, with 'habita homini' instead (dougherty2008:9970), suggesting 'bomini' is an OCR misreading of 'homini'.

English

Just as the cognition had by man through demonstration is, for the common condition we experience here, the most perfect cognition, so absolutely, among the kinds of cognition, it is the most imperfect. unverified draft

'pro communi statu quem hic experimur' is rendered 'for the common condition we experience here' (farmer1998:20336), keeping Pico's contrast between demonstrative cognition's perfection relative to the human condition and its imperfection considered absolutely among kinds of cognition.

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch12, from Latin at farmer1998:20336

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (farmer1998:20336). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

The Dougherty anthology sets this thesis beside a passage from Pico's own De ente et uno, chapter 5, citing Garin's edition at page 408 and Miller's translation at page 47, and quotes it immediately after the conclusio's Latin as a comparandum (dougherty2008:9970).
