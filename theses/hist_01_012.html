Albert the Great · thesis 1.12

Thesis 1.12

Latin

Non introducitur uegetalis anima ante sensualem, nec sensualis ante rationalem, sed tota simul.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11145

English

The vegetative soul is not introduced prior to the sensitive soul, nor the sensitive prior to the rational, but the whole soul is introduced at one and the same time.
