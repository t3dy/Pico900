Latin

Illa dicitur actio immanens quae non est subiectiue in illo quod passiue per eam denominatur, et per hoc distinguitur a transeunte.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18985

English

That action is called immanent which does not exist as a subject in the thing that is passively named through it, and by this it is distinguished from a transitive action. unverified draft
