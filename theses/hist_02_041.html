The powers of the soul are really distinguished from the soul. unverified draft

Doctrine

The thesis asserts a real distinction between the soul and its own powers or faculties; Edelheit reports that Thomas's commentary on the Sentences (4, d.44, q.3, a.3, qc.1) discusses the soul's essence together with its sensory and intellectual potencies, after rejecting one possible account of their relation (edelheit2022:12698, 12720).

Historiography

Edelheit (2022) reads thesis 41 as moving from an epistemological question about the intellect and the intelligible object (theses 39-40) to the more general Thomistic question of the soul's real distinction from its faculties, treated in Thomas's Sentences commentary (edelheit2022:12698, 12720).
