Iamblichus · thesis 23.5

Thesis 23.5

Latin

Super hunc mundum, quem uocant Theologi < >, est alius quem uocant < >, et super hunc alius quem uocant < >.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15746

English

The theologians have a name for this world, left blank here in the print, a further name for the world set above it, and yet a third name for the world above that one again.

Sense checked against farmer1998:15792; reworded for distance from Farmer's rendering. The terms Pico intended for the blanks are unrecovered — Farmer's note at farmer1998:15774 rejects Kieszkowski's supplied triad as invalid for this context.
