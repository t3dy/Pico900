Latin

Voluntas potest non frui ostenso obiecto fruibili.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11981

Farmer notes that Pico's Basel editors inserted a spurious 'non' before 'frui', reversing the thesis's sense and turning Francis into an intellectualist (farmer1998:12011); the reading given follows the text at farmer1998:11981, without that insertion.

English

The will has the power to withhold enjoyment even when an enjoyable object is presented to it. unverified draft
