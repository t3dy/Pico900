Book of Causes · thesis 6>5

Thesis 6>5

Latin

Cum dixit Abucaten causam primam superiorem esse omni narratione, non tam propter id habet ueritatem quod primo affert, quia scilicet causam ante se non habet, quam propter id quod secundario innuit, quia omne intelligibile unialiter antecedit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23452

English

Abucaten calls the first cause too high for any account of it to reach; this holds true less because of his first point—that it has no cause before it—than because of his second, unstated one: that it precedes everything intelligible in a mode of pure oneness.
