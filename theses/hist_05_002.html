Paternitas est principium generandi in patre.

English

Paternity is the principle of generating in the Father. unverified draft

'Patre' is rendered as the first Trinitarian Person, following the Trinitarian context Edelheit gives the thesis (farmer1998:12486; edelheit2022:15330).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch7, from Latin at farmer1998:12486

Attribution: Reports Henry of Ghent (farmer1998:12486; edelheit2022:15330). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Edelheit reports that the thesis comes from Henry of Ghent's Quodlibet III, question 3, where Henry argues that the Father's own characteristic feature, paternity, is the reason of production, and not the divine essence, although one can say more (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15330).
