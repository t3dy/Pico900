Simplicius · thesis 17.5

Thesis 17.5

Latin

Eadem pars rationalis, ut extra se uadens et procedens perficitur speciebus quae in ipsa sunt, ut manens est dicitur intellectus in habitu.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14562

English

The same rational part, insofar as it goes out and proceeds beyond itself and is perfected by the forms that are within it, is called, insofar as it abides, the intellect in habit.
