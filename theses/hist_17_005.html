Latin

Eadem pars rationalis, ut extra se uadens et procedens perficitur speciebus quae in ipsa sunt, ut manens est dicitur intellectus in habitu.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14562

English

The same rational part [of the soul], insofar as it goes forth and proceeds outside itself, is perfected by the species that are in it; insofar as it remains [within itself], it is called intellect in habit. unverified draft

'intellectus in habitu' is the habitual intellect, holding intelligible species in readiness short of actual consideration (farmer1998:14562).
