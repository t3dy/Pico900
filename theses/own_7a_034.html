Thesis 7a>34

Latin

Quomodo differat infinitas quam theologi attribuunt deo ab illa quam dicunt philosophi impossibile esse dari.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24167

English

How the infinity theologians ascribe to God differs from the infinity the philosophers say is impossible to grant.

Sense checked against Farmer's English at farmer1998:24225; 'ascribe'/'impossible to grant' render 'attribuunt'/'impossibile esse dari' more literally than Farmer's 'attribute'/'cannot exist'.
