Zoroaster and Chaldeans · thesis 8>14

Thesis 8>14

Latin

Per dictum illud Zoroastris, adhuc tres dies sacrificabitis et non ultra, apparuit mihi per arithmeticam superioris merchiaue illos computandi dies, esse in eo dicto expresse praedictum aduentum Christi.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25041

'merchiaue' renders Hebrew 'merkabah'; Farmer's note glosses 'superior merkabah' as the speculative branch of the Kabbalah concerning divine things (farmer1998:25053).

English

By that saying of Zoroaster, 'Three days more you shall sacrifice, and no longer', it became clear to me, when I counted those days by the arithmetic of the higher merkabah, that the coming of Christ is foretold in that saying in so many words.
