Proclus · thesis 24.12

Thesis 24.12

Latin

Inter extremales paternos deos, Saturnum et Iouem, mediat necessario Rhea per proprietatem uitae foecundae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16069

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 proprieratem

Source prints 'Saturnumet' without a word space; corrected to 'Saturnum et' (farmer1998:16069). 1486 also reads 'proprieratem' for 'proprietatem' (farmer1998:16085).

English

Rhea, by necessity, stands as mediator between Saturn and Jove, the paternal gods at the two extremes, in virtue of her property of fruitful life.
