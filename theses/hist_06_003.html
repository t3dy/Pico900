Doctrine

Edelheit reports that the thesis holds that God as glorifier is the subject of theology, and finds this in Giles's commentary on the Sentences, book 1, prologue, question 1, article 3, in a passage distinguishing theology from metaphysics by their differing consideration of God (paraphrase, edelheit2022:16121).

unverified draft

Context

Farmer's note pairs this thesis with 6.2 as starting a series on the formal object of theology, cross-referencing 1>4, 2>27, 3>8, 7a>37 and 11>3 (paraphrase, farmer1998:12710). Edelheit groups it among the first seven of the eleven Giles theses that he characterizes as theological (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15967).
