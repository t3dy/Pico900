Latin

Sicut non omne corpus, sed perfectum anima participat, ita non omnis anima sed perfecta intellectu participat; omnia tamen uno participant.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16615

English

As it is not every body but the perfected one that shares in soul, so it is not every soul but the perfected one that shares in intellect; yet all things share in the one. unverified draft

'perfectum' and 'perfecta' are taken as 'the perfected' body and soul. No further crux.
