Latin

Frustra Palemonem et Leucotheam adibit, qui Nereum non attraxerit, nec Nereum attrahet qui circa primariam animalem trinitatem operatus non fuerit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25786

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 palimonem

English

He who has not drawn Nereus to himself will approach Palaemon and Leucothea in vain, and he will not draw Nereus to himself who has not worked upon the primary trinity of the soul. unverified draft
