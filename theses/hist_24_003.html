Thesis 24.3

Latin

Dei appellatio simpliciter absolute uni competit, qui est deus deorum; simpliciter non absolute cuilibet supersubstantialium; secundum essentiam_ intellectualium cuilibet; secundum participationem anı/mis diuinis; secundum contactum et coniunctionem demonibus; secundum similitudinem animis humanis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15947; 1486 print folio <9r/9v>

English

The appellation 'god' belongs simply and absolutely to the One alone, who is the god of gods; simply but not absolutely to each of the supersubstantial beings; according to essence to each of the intellectual beings; according to participation to the divine souls; according to contact and conjunction to demons; according to likeness to human souls. unverified draft

The source (farmer1998:15947) prints 'essentiam_' with a trailing underscore and 'anı/mis' with a stray dotless-i and slash; read as 'essentiam' and 'animis' respectively (both apparent OCR artifacts).
