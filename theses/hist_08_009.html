Latin

Odor secundum esse reale et non intentionale usque ad sensum multiplicatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13672

English

Odor is propagated as far as the sense according to real being, and not according to intentional being only. unverified draft

'Esse reale' vs. 'esse intentionale' is the medieval optics/perception distinction between a quality's real physical propagation and its merely representational ('intentional') transmission (farmer1998:13672).
