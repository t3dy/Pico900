Latin

Facit opifex alias partes proportionaliter ad suarum formarum exemplaria sicut de primo est dictum; nec opportet explicare, quia quilibet sciens_ uiam analogizandi ex se poterit deducere.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16601

English

The demiurge makes the other parts proportionally to the exemplars of their own forms, just as has been said concerning the first; nor need this be explained further, since anyone who knows the way of reasoning by analogy will be able to work it out for himself. unverified draft
