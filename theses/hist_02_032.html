Latin

De eadem re nullo modo actu distincta extra animam possunt uerificari contradictoria.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11763

English

Of one and the same thing, in no way actually distinct outside the soul, contradictories can be verified. unverified draft

The transmitted Latin carries no overt negation ('possunt uerificari', not 'non possunt') (farmer1998:11763). Edelheit's paraphrase nonetheless renders the sense as a denial, matching Farmer's gloss of the thesis as an application of the law of noncontradiction (edelheit2022:12212; farmer1998:11797). The translation given here follows the transmitted Latin literally; whether a negation has dropped from the copy-text is not established by the sources consulted (farmer1998:11763).

Doctrine

Farmer glosses 'distinct outside the soul' as distinguished in a real, extramental sense, and situates the thesis against Thomas's commentary on Aristotle's Metaphysics 2.7-11 and 11.5-6, on the metaphysical form of the law of noncontradiction (farmer1998:11797). Edelheit paraphrases the thesis as holding that, of the same thing, actually distinct and outside the soul, it is impossible for contradictory claims to be established or proved, and traces its vocabulary of 'a thing outside the soul' to Thomas's Sentences commentary (edelheit2022:12212, 12242).

unverified draft

Context

Farmer records that Thomas held predicating contradictions of a single, really undivided object would erase all distinction between separate beings, and contrasts this with Pico's own later position that contradictories can coincide, developed in a run of Pico's own theses (3>13-3>20) on contradiction in the intellectual and 'unial' nature (farmer1998:11797).

unverified draft

Historiography

Farmer (1998) reads the thesis within the scholastic debate on noncontradiction and flags Pico's later, divergent theses on contradictories coinciding in the intellectual nature (farmer1998:11797); Edelheit (2022) traces its terms to Thomas's Sentences commentary on how a mental conception relates to an extramental thing (edelheit2022:12212, 12242).
