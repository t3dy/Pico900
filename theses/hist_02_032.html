Latin

De eadem re nullo modo actu distincta extra animam possunt uerificari contradictoria.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11763

English

Contradictory predicates, actually and extramentally distinct, can never both hold true of one and the same thing. unverified draft

'distincta extra animam' = distinguished in a real, extramental sense (Farmer's note, farmer1998:11797). Sense checked against farmer1998:11824.
