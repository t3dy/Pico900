Latin

Anima separata intelligit per species sibi a principio sui esse concreatas, quibus dum est in corpore aut nunquam aut raro utitur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11040

OCR 'sıbi' corrected to 'sibi'.

English

The separated soul understands by means of species created together with it from the beginning of its existence, which it uses either never or rarely while it is in the body. unverified draft
