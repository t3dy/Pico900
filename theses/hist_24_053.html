Latin

Quaecunque de supercoelesti loco in Phedro affirmatiue dicuntur, de eo dicuntur non ut simpliciter primum, sed ut habet ante se superiora genera quibus participat. Quaecunque negatiue dicuntur, dicuntur ut analogice se habet ad primum bonum, quod est absolute caput omnium, non determinati ordinis huius uel illius.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16812

English

Whatever is said affirmatively of the supercelestial place in the Phaedrus is said of it not as of something simply first, but insofar as it has, prior to itself, higher kinds in which it participates. Whatever is said of it negatively is said insofar as it stands analogically to the first good, which is absolutely the head of all things, of no determinate order, this one or that. unverified draft
