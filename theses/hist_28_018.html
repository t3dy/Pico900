Latin

Qui media nocte cum Tipheret copulabitur, prospera erit ei omnis generatio.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17858

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 Quia media nocte

English

Whoever unites with Tiferet at midnight, all his descendants will prosper. unverified draft

'Tipheret' (Tiferet, 'Beauty') is the sixth sefirah (farmer1998:17932); 'copulabitur' renders a technical term for mystical or theurgic union rather than marriage, and 'generatio' is here taken as 'offspring/descendants' rather than as a single temporal 'generation'.

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch11, from Latin at farmer1998:17858

Attribution: Reports Hebrew Cabalists (farmer1998:17858). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer glosses Tiferet as the sixth sefirah, already identified with the 'great Adam' at thesis 28.10, and reads the thesis as carrying mystical and magical overtones suggesting the soul's union with the purified man-as-microcosm (farmer1998:17932). He notes that Pico's second set of Cabalistic conclusions regularly associates Christ with the sixth sefirah, which he takes to reinforce this mystical sense here (farmer1998:17932).

unverified draft

Context

Farmer cross-references the thesis to the opening of Pico's magical conclusions, 9>10 and following theses, as bearing on the same theurgic theme (farmer1998:17932); the thesis is also linked by its subject, the sixth sefirah, to 28.10, on the 'great Adam' who is Tiferet (farmer1998:17728).

unverified draft

Historiography

Wirszubski transcribes the thesis as his 'Conclusio XVIII' and gives Recanati, fol 213rb-va, as its source (wirszubski1989:2060).
