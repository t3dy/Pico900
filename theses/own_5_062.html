Latin

Licet ratio Platonis in Phedone per uiam contrariorum absolute non concludat, expositis tamen a Cebete ad hominem aliquid concludit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23297

English

Although Plato's argument in the Phaedo by way of contraries does not conclude absolutely, it does conclude something, once Cebes's objections have been set forth, as an argument to the man. unverified draft

'ad hominem' is again rendered 'argument to the man', matching the usage at 5>61, at farmer1998:23297.
