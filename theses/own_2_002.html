Intentio secunda est ens rationis, habens se per modum formae qualitatiue, ab operatione intellectus proueniens consequutive, non effectiue.

English

A second intention is a being of reason, holding itself after the manner of a qualitative form, arising from an operation of the intellect consequently, not effectively. unverified draft
