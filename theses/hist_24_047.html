Proclus · thesis 24.47

Thesis 24.47

Latin

Omnis ordo medius manet in antecedente stabiliter, et in se firmat consequentem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16701

English

Every intermediate order stays fixed in the one before it, and establishes the one after it within itself.

'firmat' is 'establishes/makes firm'; 'antecedente' and 'consequentem' are the preceding and following orders. No further crux.
