Latin

Per arithmeticam non materialem, sed formalem, habetur optima uia ad prophetiam naturalem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23724

English

Through an arithmetic that is not material but formal, the best way is had to natural prophecy. unverified draft

Farmer's note distinguishes a 'material' arithmetic concerned with quantity in the realm of motion from a 'formal' arithmetic symbolizing cosmological and metaphysical principles; both terms are kept rather than paraphrased (farmer1998:23747).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch16, from Latin at farmer1998:23724

Attribution: Pico's own opinion · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

The conclusion claims only 'formal' arithmetic, not the arithmetic of quantities in motion, as the route to natural prophecy (farmer1998:23724, farmer1998:23747). Mandosio quotes the Latin in a discussion of the divinatory as well as speculative powers Pico credits to formal number (mandosio2012:313).

Historiography

Mandosio cites this conclusion alongside 7>11 as evidence for the range of effects Pico attributes to formal number (mandosio2012:313).
