Latin

Diffinitio generis quam dedit Porphyrius mala est, sed sic debet diffiniri: Genus est quod duorum uniuersalium est uniuersalius.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13777

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 dabit Porphyrius | Emendationes errorum, corrige: dedit | 1487 text emended sic

English

The definition of genus that Porphyry gave is bad; rather it ought to be defined thus: a genus is that which is more universal than two universals. unverified draft
