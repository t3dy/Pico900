Doctrine

The thesis explains the impassibility (incapacity for suffering) of resurrected bodies as an effect of the soul's complete dominion over the body (edelheit2022:10916). Farmer's note ties the same issue to the debate over Christ's own impassibility discussed at thesis 4.12 (from Scotus), and situates it within Dominican-Franciscan controversy (farmer1998:11512).

Historiography

Edelheit is the only scholar found discussing this thesis in the sources consulted; he treats it as continuing an examination, begun at the preceding thesis, of what happens to bodies in a non-corporeal, spiritual or miraculous condition (edelheit2022:10916).
