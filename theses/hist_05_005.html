Latin

Demones et animae peccatrices patiuntur ab igne, in quantum calidus est, afflictione eiusdem rationis cum ea qua affliguntur corpora.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12495

English

Demons and sinful souls are afflicted by fire, insofar as it is hot, in the same way and to the same degree that bodies are afflicted by it. unverified draft
