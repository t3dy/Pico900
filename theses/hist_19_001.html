Themistius · thesis 19.1

Thesis 19.1

Latin

Intellectus possibiles qui illuminantur tantum plures sunt; agentes participati illuminantes et illuminati plures quoque sunt; agens illuminans tantum unus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14881

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 illuminati.Plures quoque sunt.agentes

1486 reads 'illuminati.Plures quoque sunt.agentes', where the sense requires the clause division given here (farmer1998:14904).

English

The possible intellects that are only illuminated are many; the participated agent intellects that both illuminate and are illuminated are likewise many; the one agent intellect that illuminates only is single.
