Doctrine

Farmer connects this thesis's numerological premise to his own broader argument that the square number 900 (30×30) carried, within the numerological tradition Pico draws on, a symbolism of the soul roused to self-return through poetic frenzy — part of Farmer's own explanation for why Pico's text comprises exactly 900 theses (farmer1998:17101).

Historiography

Allen (2017) cites this thesis together with 25.14, from Pico's Opera Omnia, Basel 1572, page 79, in a discussion of Ficino's Timaeus commentary on point, line, plane and solid as stages mediating between center and circumference, associating such figures with souls and minds (allen2017:2687).
