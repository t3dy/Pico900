Ambrosia est analoga termino, et nectar infinito.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16819

English

Ambrosia is analogous to limit, and nectar to the unlimited. unverified draft

'Terminus'/'infinitum' render the Platonic-Pythagorean pair of limit and the unlimited that Farmer's note ties to thesis 24.1 (farmer1998:16870).

Doctrine

Farmer draws the thesis from Proclus's Platonic Theology book 4, glossing 'ambrosia' and 'nectar' as the food the charioteer feeds his horses in Plato's Phaedrus myth, and 'limit'/'infinite' by comparison with thesis 24.1 (farmer1998:16870).
