Thesis 9.7

Latin

Possibile quod diffinit Aristoteles in libro Priorum est commune ad possibile et ad inuentum, ut contra distinctum necessario.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13881

The 'possible' Aristotle defines in the Prior Analytics covers both the possible and the contingent, set over against the necessary.
