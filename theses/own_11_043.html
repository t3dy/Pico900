Thesis 11>43

Latin

Per mysterium duarum litterarum uau et iod scitur quomodo ipse messias ut deus fuit principium suiipsius ut homo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27327

English

The mystery of the two letters vav and yod shows how the Messiah, as God, was the origin of himself considered as man.

Vav and yod are the first and last letters of the name Jesus in Pico's letter-play, as in 11>14 (farmer1998:27360).
