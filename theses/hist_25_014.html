Thesis 25.14

Latin

Quilibet numerus linearis symbolizat deos.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17025

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 Qilibet

1486 reads 'Qilibet' (farmer1998:17034).

English

Every linear number is a symbol of the gods.

'numerus linearis', literally 'linear number', i.e. a number divisible only by itself and one; sense checked against farmer1998:17089.
