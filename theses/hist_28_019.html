Thesis 28.19

Latin

Eaedem sunt litterae nominis cacodemonis qui est princeps mundi huius et nominis dei Triagrammaton, et qui sciuerit ordinare transpositionem deducet unum ex alio.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17862

English

The letters of the name of the evil demon who is the prince of this world are the same as those of the three-letter name of God, and whoever knows how to arrange the transposition will derive the one from the other.
