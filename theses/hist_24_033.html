Thesis 24.33

Latin

Trinitatem Proserpinae comitantur a latere trinitas custoditiua et conseruatiua.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16479

English

The guardian trinity and the conserving trinity accompany, at its side, the trinity of Proserpina.

The Latin has one object (Trinitatem Proserpinae), a plural verb (comitantur) and a singular subject 'trinitas' with two adjectives; taken as two trinities, guardian and conserving, on the strength of the plural verb and of 24.35. Sense checked against farmer1998:16538.
