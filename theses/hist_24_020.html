Latin

Post intellectualem ebdomadem ordinandi sunt immediate supermundani dei a partibus uniuersi exempti, et incoordinabiles ad hunc mundum, et secundum causam eum undique circumplectentes.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16181

English

After the intellectual hebdomad, the supermundane gods must be ranked immediately -- exempt from the parts of the universe, not coordinate with this world, and embracing it on every side according to causation. unverified draft
