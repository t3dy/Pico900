Latin

Cum dicit Aristoteles omnem doctrinam et omnem disciplinam fieri ex praeexistenti cognitione, intelligende sunt per doctrinam et disciplinam cognitio diffinitiua et argumentatiua.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13784

English

Where Aristotle says that all teaching and all learning proceed from preexisting knowledge, 'teaching' and 'learning' should be taken to mean knowledge gained through definitions and through arguments. unverified draft
