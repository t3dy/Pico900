Latin

Cum dicit Aristoteles omnem doctrinam et omnem disciplinam fieri ex praeexistenti cognitione, intelligende sunt per doctrinam et disciplinam cognitio diffinitiua et argumentatiua.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13784

English

When Aristotle says that all teaching and all learning arise from pre-existing knowledge, by 'teaching' and 'learning' are to be understood definitional and argumentative knowledge. unverified draft

'doctrina' and 'disciplina' render the standard scholastic pairing of teaching (the teacher's act) and learning (the learner's act), here glossed as knowledge gained respectively by definition and by argumentation (farmer1998:13784).
