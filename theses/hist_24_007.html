Latin

Per unum, plura, totum, partes, finitum, infinitum, de quibus in Parmenide, habemus intelligere secundum ordinem trinitatis intelligibilis intellectualis, secundum illius ordinis trinariam diuisionem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15967

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 intelligibilibus intellectualibus

English

Through the one, the many, the whole, the parts, the finite, and the infinite -- discussed in the Parmenides -- we must understand these according to the order of the intelligible-and-intellectual triad, according to that order's threefold division. unverified draft
