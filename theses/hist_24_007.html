Latin

Per unum, plura, totum, partes, finitum, infinitum, de quibus in Parmenide, habemus intelligere secundum ordinem trinitatis intelligibilis intellectualis, secundum illius ordinis trinariam diuisionem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 15967

Apparatus (Farmer): 1487 intelligibilibus intellectualibus

1487 reads 'intelligibilibus intellectualibus' for 'intelligibilis intellectualis' (farmer1998:15976).

English

By the one and the many, the whole and its parts, the finite and the infinite, spoken of in the Parmenides, we must understand the second order of the intelligible-intellectual trinity, according to that order's threefold division. unverified draft
