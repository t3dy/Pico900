Latin

In omni quaestione per demonstrationem scibili, oportet praecognoscere quid subiecti et passionis, non intelligendo per quid, quid nominis, ut intelligunt expositores, sed quid rei.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 19322

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 per quid. quid nominis

1486/1487 apparatus: 1486 per quid. quid nominis

English

In every question knowable through demonstration, one must first know the what of the subject and of the property demonstrated of it — understanding by 'what' not the meaning of the name, as the commentators take it, but the nature of the thing. unverified draft

'quid nominis' versus 'quid rei' is the standard distinction between a nominal and a real definition.
