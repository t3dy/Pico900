Latin

Ex praecedentibus conclusionibus potest intelligi qui sit formalis numerus, quem dixit Pythagoras esse principium omnium rerum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20648

English

From the preceding conclusions it can be understood what the formal number is, which Pythagoras said to be the principle of all things. unverified draft

"formalis numerus" is kept as Pico's technical term rather than smoothed away (farmer1998:20648).

Attribution: Pico's own opinion · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

The thesis presents itself as a conclusion drawn from a preceding series on how ideas or numbers exist in the intellect: it claims that this series makes intelligible what "formal number" is, the term by which, on Pico's report, Pythagoras named the principle of all things (farmer1998:20648). Farmer's note assigns extended discussion of "formal" numbers to his note on 7>9, on arithmetic and natural prophecy, and lists further related theses under his note 7.34 (farmer1998:20686; farmer1998:23724).

unverified draft

Context

3>26 stands at the end of a run, 3>24-3>26, on how ideas or numbers exist in the intellect without multiplying it (farmer1998:20639; farmer1998:20643), and is followed immediately by a series, 3>27-3>33, reducing the categories to five (unity, substance, quantity, quality, and relation) against Archytas's and Aristotle's ten, Plotinus's five, and the Stoics' four (farmer1998:20652; farmer1998:20656).

unverified draft

Historiography

Giulio Busi and Raphael Ebgi, discussing Pico's Pythagoreanism, state that for Pico, who had learned the Pythagoreans' lesson well, formal numbers are nothing other than the principles of every being, cite this thesis, and direct the reader to Wirszubski's Pico della Mirandola's Encounter with Jewish Mysticism for further discussion (busi2014:10178). Jean-Marc Mandosio, tracing the afterlife of Pico's formal numbers in John Dee, quotes the thesis's central clause and reports that on Johannes Reuchlin's reading, Pythagoras's "number" in this maxim is meant allegorically (mandosio2012:247).
