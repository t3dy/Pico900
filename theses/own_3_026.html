Thesis 3>26

Latin

Ex praecedentibus conclusionibus potest intelligi qui sit formalis numerus, quem dixit Pythagoras esse principium omnium rerum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20648

English

The preceding conclusions make it possible to grasp what formal number is — the very thing Pythagoras called the principle of all things.
