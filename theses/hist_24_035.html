Dei de quibus in Phedro_in quatuor trinitates sunt distinguendi: Opificatiuam, Custoditiuam, Vitalem, et Conuersiuam.

English

The gods spoken of in the Phaedrus are to be distinguished into four triads: the creative, the guardian, the vital, and the reversive. unverified draft
