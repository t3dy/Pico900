Proclus · thesis 24.35

Thesis 24.35

Latin

Dei de quibus in Phedro in quatuor trinitates sunt distinguendi: Opificatiuam, Custoditiuam, Vitalem, et Conuersiuam.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16487

Apparatus (Farmer): colon retained from 1486 edition

Latin as in the packet with run-together or artefact spellings normalised: 'Phedro_in' to 'Phedro in'.

English

The gods of whom the Phaedrus speaks are to be divided into four trinities: the fashioning, the guardian, the vital and the converting.

Underscore in 'Phedro_in' (farmer1998:16487) treated as an OCR artefact and replaced by a space. 'Opificatiuam' rendered 'fashioning' (from opifex, maker); the colon is retained from the 1486 edition (farmer1998:16502). Sense checked against farmer1998:16546.
