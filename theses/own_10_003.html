Orphic Hymns · thesis 10>3

Thesis 10>3

Latin

Nomina deorum quos Orpheus canit non decipientium demonum, a quibus malum et non bonum prouenit. Sed naturalium uirtutum diuinarumque sunt nomina, a uero deo in utilitatem maxime hominis, si eis uti sciuerit, mundo distributarum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25580

English

When Orpheus sings the names of the gods, he is not naming deceiving demons, the source of evil rather than good; he is naming natural and divine powers that the true God has scattered through the world for man's great benefit, provided man knows how to put them to use.
