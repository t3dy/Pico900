Latin

Scitur per fundamenta cabale quam recte dixerit Iesus: Antequam nasceretur Abraam, ego sum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27323

English

The foundations of the Cabala make it known how rightly Jesus spoke when he said, Before Abraham came to be, I am. unverified draft

John 8:58. Farmer's note connects the thesis to 11>37, since 'Jesus', tied to the second sefirah, there precedes 'Abraham', tied to the fourth (farmer1998:27355).
