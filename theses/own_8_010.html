Latin

Per canem nihil aliud intelligit Zoroaster quam partem inrationalem animae et proportionalia, quod ita esse uidebit qui diligenter dicta omnia expositorum considerauerit, qui et ipsi, sicut et Zoroaster, enigmatice loquuntur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24945

English

By the dog Zoroaster understands nothing other than the irrational part of the soul and things proportionate to it, as anyone will see who carefully considers all the sayings of the expositors, who themselves, like Zoroaster, speak enigmatically. unverified draft
