Latin

Opinio quae Auicennae ascribitur, quod illud unum, quod cum ente conuertitur sit unum quod est principium numeri, et ita consequenter quod unaquaeque res sit una per intentionem additam essentiae suae, si non est necessario uera, est tamen probabilis et defendetur a me.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 19890

English

The opinion ascribed to Avicenna — that the one convertible with being is the one that is the principle of number, and consequently that each thing is one through an intention added to its essence — even if not necessarily true, is nonetheless probable, and I will defend it. unverified draft
