Latin

Motus est sensibile commune, a sensu exteriori absque actione alterius uirtutis cognoscibile.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13975

English

Motion, a common sensible, can be known by an outer sense without any other faculty coming into play. unverified draft

'sensibile commune' kept as 'common sensible', the Aristotelian term for objects (motion, number, shape, etc.) perceived by more than one sense; cf. farmer1998:14035.
