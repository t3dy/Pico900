Latin

Sicut angelus necessario componitur ex essentia et esse, ita anima necessario componitur ex substantia et accidente.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20441

English

An angel's composition is, of necessity, out of essence and existence; the soul's composition, equally of necessity, is out of substance and accident. unverified draft

'substantia'/'accidens' here denote the soul's intellectual and rational parts respectively, on Pico's usage elsewhere (cf. 3>59, 3>61); sense checked.
