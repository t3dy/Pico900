Thesis 3>8

Latin

Si theologia theologice tradatur, erit ut de primo subiecto de eo quod est unialiter unum, et de quolibet quod est secundum suum esse uniale tanquam de subiecto secundario.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20346

English

If theology were handed down in a properly theological manner, it would treat as its primary subject whatever is one in the unial mode, and as its secondary subject whatever exists according to its own unial being.

'unialiter unum' names Pico's technical mode of unial being; sense checked.
