Latin

Si theologia theologice tradatur, erit ut de primo subiecto de eo quod est unialiter unum, et de quolibet quod est secundum suum esse uniale tanquam de subiecto secundario.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20346

English

If theology is treated theologically, it will be concerned, as with its primary subject, with that which is unially one, and, as with a secondary subject, with whatever is one according to its unial being. unverified draft
