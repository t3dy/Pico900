Thesis 28.28

Latin

Per uolatile quod creatum est die quinta, debemus intelligere angelos mundanos qui hominibus apparent, non eos qui non apparent nisi in spiritu.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18078

English

By the winged creature that was created on the fifth day, we ought to understand the worldly angels who appear to human beings, not those who appear only in spirit. unverified draft

'uolatile' ('winged/flying creature') refers to Gen. 1:20, the creation of birds on the fifth day (farmer1998:18152).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch11, from Latin at farmer1998:18078

Attribution: Reports Hebrew Cabalists (farmer1998:18078). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer reads the thesis as referring to Genesis 1:20 and as implying a hierarchy of angelic powers comparable to that found in 6.7; he notes that which sefirah is meant by 'the fifth day' is unclear, cross-referencing his note on 28.8, and further cross-references 28.30 as a related thesis (farmer1998:18152).

unverified draft

Context

Farmer cross-references 28.8, on souls descending from the third light through the fourth and fifth days (farmer1998:17645), and 6.7, on the illumination of an inferior angel by a superior one (farmer1998:12693) (farmer1998:18152).
