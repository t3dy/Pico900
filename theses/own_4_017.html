Thesis 4>17

Latin

Primum peccatum angeli fuit peccatum omissionis, secundum peccatum luxuriae, tertium peccatum superbiae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21782

English

The angel's first sin was one of omission, the second one of lust, the third one of pride.

'luxuria' rendered 'lust' rather than 'voluptuousness'; sense unaffected.
