Doctrine

Farmer's note records that Wirszubski (1989: 49) cites a passage of the Sefer ha-Bahir associating 'east' with the seventh sefirah, and adds that the Sefer Yetzirah, which Pico also knew, offers a further point of comparison not completed in the excerpt consulted (paraphrase, farmer1998:18368).

unverified draft

Historiography

Wirszubski identifies the source of this thesis as the Bahir, section 104 in Scholem's edition, per a private communication from Gershom Scholem (wirszubski1989:2817), located at Vatican manuscript Ebr 191, folio 312v. Elsewhere he places the thesis's manuscript location among a block of theses drawn from Recanati, noting that it 'treats of new souls and its source is the Bahir, either directly or indirectly through the quotation in Recanati at folio 9va', and calls its position among the Recanati-derived theses 'remarkable' (wirszubski1989:3184).
