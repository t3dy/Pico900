Hebrew Cabalists · thesis 28.3

Thesis 28.3

Latin

Quamuis nomen ineffabile sit proprietas clementiae, negandum tamen non est quin contineat proprietatem iudicii.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17540

English

The ineffable name belongs properly to clemency, yet it cannot be denied that it holds the property of judgment within it as well.
