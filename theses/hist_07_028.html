Latin

Cum Aristoteles dixit ex maiori necessaria et minore inuenta/concludi conclusionem necessariam, intelligendum est de minore quae est inuenta per se, necessaria per accidens.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13290; 1486 print folio <4v/5r>

Farmer's text shows a slash ('inuenta/concludi') at the point of an apparent line break in the source; read continuously as 'inuenta concludi' (farmer1998:13290).

English

When Aristotle said that a necessary conclusion is concluded from a necessary major premise and a 'found' minor premise, this must be understood of a minor that is 'found' per se but necessary per accidens. unverified draft
