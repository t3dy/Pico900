Orphic Hymns · thesis 10>4

Thesis 10>4

Latin

Sicut hymni Dauid operi Cabalae mirabiliter deseruiunt, ita hymni Orphei operi uerae, licitae, et naturalis magiae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25671

English

As the Psalms of David marvelously answer to a work of the Cabala, the hymns of Orpheus likewise answer to a work of true, licit, natural magic.

Sense checked against farmer1998:25722; no divergence. 'hymni Dauid' rendered 'the Psalms of David' per Farmer's identification (farmer1998:25697).
