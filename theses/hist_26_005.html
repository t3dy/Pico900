Latin

Coordinatio intelligibilis non est in intellectuali coordinatione ut dixit Amosis aegyptius, sed est super omnem intellectualem hierarchiam in abysso primae unitatis, et sub caligine primarum tenebrarum, inparticipaliter abscondita.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17143

English

The intelligible order does not lie within the intellectual order, as Ahmose the Egyptian said, but is hidden, without participation, above the whole intellectual hierarchy, in the abyss of the first unity and beneath the mist of the first darkness. unverified draft
