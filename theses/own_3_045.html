Latin

Ordo librorum Methaphysicae post naturales, eo modo quo eos ordinauit Aristoteles, secundum nullum processum, siue compositiuum siue resolutiuum, potest esse rectus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20971

English

The order of the books of the Metaphysics after the natural books, in the way Aristotle arranged them, cannot be correct according to any procedure, whether compositive or resolutive. unverified draft
