Latin

Cum dicit Anstoteles non recordari nos post mortem quia passiuus intellectus corrumpitur, per passiuum intellectum, possibilem intellectum intelligit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14655

English

When Aristotle says that we do not remember after death because the passive intellect is corrupted, by 'passive intellect' he means the possible intellect. unverified draft

The source text reads 'Anstoteles' for 'Aristoteles' (farmer1998:14655).
