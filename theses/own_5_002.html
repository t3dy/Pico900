Latin

Cum quaeritur a Platone an ad exemplar genitum an ingenitum factus sit mundus, nihil aliud quaeritur nisi an ad animales rationes, an ad ideas intellectuales.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22214

English

When it is asked of Plato whether the world was made according to a generated or an ungenerated exemplar, nothing else is being asked except whether it was made according to psychic (soul-level) rational principles, or according to intellectual Ideas. unverified draft
