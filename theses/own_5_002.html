Plato · thesis 5>2

Thesis 5>2

Latin

Cum quaeritur a Platone an ad exemplar genitum an ingenitum factus sit mundus, nihil aliud quaeritur nisi an ad animales rationes, an ad ideas intellectuales.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22214

English

When Plato asks whether the world was made after a generated or an ungenerated pattern, nothing else is being asked except whether it was made after animate reasons or after intelligible ideas.
