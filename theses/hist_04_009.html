Doctrine

The thesis denies that Christ possessed 'acquired knowledge,' as opposed to knowledge that is infused or otherwise not gained through ordinary human learning (edelheit2022:14286). Farmer's note calls this 'another issue dividing the Scotists and Thomists,' cross-referencing Thomas's Summa theologiae 3a, questions 11-12, and records that in the Apology Pico charged Thomas with inconsistency on the topic (farmer1998:12331).

unverified draft

Context

Edelheit quotes the Scotist text under discussion distinguishing 'infused' from 'acquired' cognition in Christ's soul (edelheit2022:14314).

unverified draft

Historiography

Edelheit locates the doctrine in Scotus's Lectura 3, distinction 14, question 3, on whether Christ's soul knew everything within its own genus, and quotes the opening of the relevant distinction between infused and acquired cognition (edelheit2022:14286, 14314).
