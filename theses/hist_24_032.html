Latin

Tertia trinitas deorum supermundanorum dicitur Apollo, et conuersiuum ei appropriatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16475

English

The third triad of the supermundane gods is called Apollo, and the revertive property is assigned to it. unverified draft

'Conversivum' names the reversion (epistrophe) member of the Neoplatonic remaining/procession/reversion triad, per the thesis's association of Apollo with a specific triadic rank (farmer1998:16511).
