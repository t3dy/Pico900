Proclus · thesis 24.32

Thesis 24.32

Latin

Tertia trinitas deorum supermundanorum dicitur Apollo, et conuersiuum ei appropriatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16475

English

The third trinity of the supermundane gods is called Apollo, and the converting property is appropriated to it.

'conuersiuum' is used substantively (neuter); supplied 'property' after the parallel 'proprietas' of 24.22. Sense checked against farmer1998:16534.
