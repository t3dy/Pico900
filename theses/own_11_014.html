Thesis 11>14

Latin

Per litteram <Hebrew>, id est, scin, quae mediat in nomine Iesu, significatur nobis cabalistice quod tum perfecte quieuit, tanquam in sua perfectione, mundus cum Iod coniunctus est cum Vau, quod factum est in Christo, qui fuit uerus dei filius et homo.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 26697

The bracketed run '<W>' renders as OCR garble the single Hebrew letter named in apposition immediately after it ('id est, scin'); replaced here with '<Hebrew>' since the letter itself does not render (farmer1998:26697). Farmer's edition also prints 'lesu' for 'Iesu' (I/long-s confusion), corrected here (farmer1998:26697).

English

Through the Hebrew letter that stands in the middle of the name Jesus, that is, shin, we are shown Cabalistically that the world then came fully to rest, as though at its own perfection, when Yod was joined to Vav — which came about in Christ, who was true Son of God, and man.

Yod and Vav are also the first and last letters of the ineffable name Pico associates with the Messiah in 11>15; shin mediates between them in the name Jesus, as Pico reads it letter by letter rather than as a word.
