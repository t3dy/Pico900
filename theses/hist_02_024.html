Latin

Subiectum et propria passio realiter distinguuntur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11650

English

A subject is really distinct from the proper accident that belongs to it. unverified draft

'propria passio': a property belonging inseparably to a subject without being part of its essence, e.g. snubness in a nose (Farmer's note, farmer1998:11682). Sense checked against farmer1998:11706.
