Latin

Per coelum in Epinomide, quod dicit Plato esse nobis causam omnium bonorum, non ideam coeli, sed ipsum coelum, quod est coeleste animal, intelligere debemus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22762

English

By 'heaven' in the Epinomis, which Plato says is for us the cause of all good things, we must understand not the Idea of heaven, but heaven itself, which is a celestial living being. unverified draft

'coeleste animal' is kept literal, 'celestial living being', echoing the Timaeus's living cosmos, at farmer1998:22762.
