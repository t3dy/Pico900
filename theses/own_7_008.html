Latin

Medicina sanat animam per corpus, musica autem corpus per animam.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23721

English

Medicine heals the soul through the body, while music heals the body through the soul. unverified draft

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch16, from Latin at farmer1998:23721

Attribution: Pico's own opinion · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

Paired with 7>7, this conclusion states a converse relation: where 7>7 has medicine and music each moving the mediating spiritus toward body or soul, here each art is said to heal the opposite part indirectly, medicine reaching the soul by way of the body and music reaching the body by way of the soul (farmer1998:23717, farmer1998:23721). Farmer reads both conclusions against the ancient theory of the spiritus and a broadly attested belief in the curative power of music (farmer1998:23743).

Historiography
