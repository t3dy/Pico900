Latin

Ad hoc, quod aliqua scientia alteri non subalternetur, sufficit quod faciat reductionem ad per se nota in suo genere abstractionis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12786

OCR 'abstractions' corrected to 'abstractionis'.

English

A science escapes subordination to another provided it can be traced back to self-evident principles within its own genus of abstraction. unverified draft

Farmer calls this the premodern antecedent of Gödel's second theorem (farmer1998:12806). Sense checked against farmer1998:12827.
