Doctrine

The thesis draws a parallel between the divine intellect and the soul with respect to unity and multiplicity: just as God's intellect multiplies unity, so the soul quantifies and extends the multitude that belongs to intellect (farmer1998:20767).

Historiography

The German study Erkenntnistheorie und christliche Kabbalah quotes the thesis in full while discussing how the sefirot can, for Pico, be read as the communicated and thought unity of God beyond his absolute unity, a reading it attributes in turn to Massetani's La filosofia cabbalistica (kabbalah_paradigma:1710).
