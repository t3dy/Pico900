Latin

Cum dicit Abucaten intelligentiam, ut diuina, est regere res, intelligendum est de regimine statuitiuo, non ordinatiuo qui ei competit ut intelligentia

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 23553

English

When Abucaten says that intelligence, as something divine, governs things, this is to be understood of a constitutive governance, not the ordinative governance that belongs to it as intelligence. unverified draft
