Latin

Licet natura intellectualis simul omnia intelligat, non tamen hoc est per uirtualem et unitiuam continentiam, sed per mutuam penetrationem formarum, et indissociatam concatenationem totius esse participati, id est, formalis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22877

English

Although intellectual nature understands all things at once, this is not through a virtual and unifying containment, but through the mutual interpenetration of forms and the inseparable concatenation of the whole of participated being, that is, formal being. unverified draft

'unitiuam continentiam' is rendered 'unifying containment', contrasted here with a rival model of mutual interpenetration of forms, at farmer1998:22877.
