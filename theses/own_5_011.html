Latin

Primus septennarius uitae humanae est sub Mercurio, secundus sub Venere, tertius sub Marte, quartus sub Ioue, quintus sub Saturno, et reliqui septennarii secundum eum qui fuerit praedominatus in praecedentibus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22344

English

The first seven-year span of human life falls under Mercury, the second under Venus, the third under Mars, the fourth under Jove, the fifth under Saturn, and the remaining seven-year spans follow whichever planet has predominated in the ones before. unverified draft
