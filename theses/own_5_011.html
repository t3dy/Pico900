Latin

Primus septennarius uitae humanae est sub Mercurio, secundus sub Venere, tertius sub Marte, quartus sub Ioue, quintus sub Saturno, et reliqui septennarii secundum eum qui fuerit praedominatus in praecedentibus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22344

English

The first seven-year period of human life is under Mercury, the second under Venus, the third under Mars, the fourth under Jupiter, the fifth under Saturn, and the remaining seven-year periods follow whichever planet has predominated among the preceding ones. unverified draft

'septennarius' names a seven-year period of life; 'praedominatus' is rendered 'predominated,' of a planet's rule over the earlier septennia, a usage Farmer's note does not further gloss (farmer1998:22372).

Doctrine

The thesis assigns each successive seven-year period of a human life to a planet in turn, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn, and has later septennia follow whichever planet has predominated among the earlier ones (farmer1998:22344). Farmer treats it as part of the same set as 5>9-10 and 5>12, in which the sun and moon are read as figures for the intellect and its lower reflection in reason, and in which, he states, no predictive or horoscopal astrology is involved (farmer1998:22372).
