Latin

Quae opinio uerior de trinitate: Arn, Sabellii, Eucliph, aut fidei catholicae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24263

The copy-text's OCR gives 'Arn' and 'Eucliph' for the genitive forms of the names; the precise correct Latin spellings are not established by the sources consulted. Farmer's note identifies the three referents as Arius, Sabellius, and Wyclif (farmer1998:24293), and the translation follows that identification.

English

Which view of the Trinity is closer to the truth: that of Arius, that of Sabellius, that of Wyclif, or that of the catholic faith? unverified draft
