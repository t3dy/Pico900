Latin

Quae opinio uerior de trinitate: Arn, Sabellii, Eucliph, aut fidei catholicae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24263

English

Which opinion about the Trinity is truer: that of 'Arn', of Sabellius, of 'Eucliph', or that of the catholic faith. unverified draft

The first and third names are given exactly as the source text prints them at farmer1998:24263 ('Arn', 'Eucliph'); these read as corrupted or abbreviated forms of heresiarchs' names, transcribed as-is rather than silently emended.
