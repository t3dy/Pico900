Latin

Decem ultores, de quibus dixit secundum Mercurium praecedens conclusio, uidebit profundus contemplator correspondere male coordinationi denariae in cabala_et praefectis illius, de quibus ego in cabalisticis conclusionibus nihil posui, quia est secretum.

English

The ten avengers of the preceding conclusion, spoken of according to Mercury, will be seen by a discerning observer to answer to the evil tenfold ordering in the Cabala and its rulers; of these I have set down nothing among my Cabalistic conclusions, for it is a secret. unverified draft

Doctrine

The thesis completes the sequence begun at thesis 27.9, which lists ten avengers within each person: ignorance, sorrow, inconstancy, desire, injustice, luxury, envy, fraud, wrath, and malice (farmer1998:17358). It maps that decad onto the Cabala's own tenfold ordering and its governing powers, while withholding further explanation as a secret reserved for Pico's own Cabalistic conclusions (farmer1998:17362). Farmer records that the Hermetic Corpus and the editio princeps name twelve avengers, and that Pico reduced the number to ten in his Emendationes errorum in order to preserve this correspondence (farmer1998:17407).
