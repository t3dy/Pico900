Latin

Si ex concursu elementorum fiat mixtum, quocunque modo ponantur elementa manere immixto, fiet mixtum ex elementis ut ex materia digesta, a calido spirituali uaporoso eleuato ab eis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 19983

The sheet's underscores in 'elementis_ut' and 'spirituali_uaporoso' are restored to spaces.

English

If a mixture arises from the concurrence of the elements — whatever way the elements are held to remain unmixed within it — the mixture comes to be from the elements as from a digested matter, raised up from them by a spirit-like vaporous heat. unverified draft
