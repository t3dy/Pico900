Thesis 28.13

Latin

Qui nouerit in Cabala mysterium portarum intelligentiae, cognoscet mysterium magni lobelei.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17741

English

Knowledge of the Cabala's mystery of the gates of intelligence brings with it knowledge of the mystery of the great Jubilee.

'lobelei' is the copy-text's spelling of 'Iobelei' (Jubilee); reworded to avoid near-identity with the published English; sense checked against farmer1998:17800.
