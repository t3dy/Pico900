Orphic Hymns · thesis 10>6

Thesis 10>6

Latin

Quarumcunque uirtutum naturalium uel diuinarum eadem est proprietatis analogia, idem etiam nomen, idem hymnus, idem opus, seruata proportione. Et qui tentauerit exponere uidebit correspondentiam.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25680

English

Whatever the natural or divine powers may be, their analogy of property is the same, and so too the name, the hymn, and the work belonging to each, proportion being preserved; whoever attempts to expound this will perceive the correspondence. unverified draft

This rendering is prepared independently from the Latin; Howlett separately quotes Farmer's own published translation of the sentence together with the Latin in her discussion of the thesis (howlett2021:6221).

Doctrine

The thesis states a principle of universal analogical correspondence linking name, hymn, and operative work for each natural or divine power, and no interpretive note on this specific thesis, or on its relation to 10>7, was located in the sources consulted (farmer1998:13641; see not_established).
