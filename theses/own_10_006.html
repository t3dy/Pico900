Orphic Hymns · thesis 10>6

Thesis 10>6

Latin

Quarumcunque uirtutum naturalium uel diuinarum eadem est proprietatis analogia, idem etiam nomen, idem hymnus, idem opus, seruata proportione. Et qui tentauerit exponere uidebit correspondentiam.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25680

English

For every natural or divine power, the same analogy of properties holds, and with it the same name, the same hymn, the same operation, proportion being kept throughout; anyone who sets out to expound this will discover the correspondence.
