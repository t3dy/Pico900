De potentia dei absoluta possibile est culpam originalem deleri sine infusione gratiae.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12258

English

By the absolute power of God it is possible for original guilt to be erased without the infusion of grace. unverified draft

'Culpam originalem': original guilt, that is, original sin considered as culpability rather than as the resulting corruption of nature (farmer1998:12258).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch6, from Latin at farmer1998:12258

Attribution: Reports Scotus (farmer1998:12258). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

The thesis concerns the scope of God's absolute power (potentia dei absoluta) in relation to sin and grace: whether original guilt could, in principle, be removed without the accompanying infusion of sanctifying grace (farmer1998:12258). Edelheit states that according to thesis 13 it is possible, by the absolute power of God, for original sin to be eliminated without the infusion of grace, and locates the discussion in Scotus's Ordinatio, book 4, distinction 1, part 4, question 1, where Scotus first presents and rejects an opposing view of Richard of Middleton before arguing his own position (edelheit2022:14519).
