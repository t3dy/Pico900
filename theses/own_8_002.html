Thesis 8>2

Latin

Ibidem dico interpretes nihil aliud per uirtutes mysteriales intelligere quam naturalem magiam.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 24728

English

There too, I hold that when the interpreters speak of mysterious powers, they mean nothing beyond natural magic.
