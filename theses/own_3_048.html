Latin

Praeter tria demonstrationis genera, quia, propter quid, et simpliciter, quae ponunt Aristoteles et commentator, datur quartum genus demonstrationis quae dici potest demonstratio conuertibilitatis, fortius omnibus praedictis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20984

English

Beyond the three kinds of demonstration that Aristotle and the Commentator posit — of the fact, of the reasoned fact, and unqualified demonstration — there exists a fourth kind, strongest of them all, which may be called demonstration by convertibility. unverified draft

'commentator' = Averroes; the three named kinds correspond to the standard Posterior Analytics distinction (quia / propter quid / simpliciter); sense checked.
