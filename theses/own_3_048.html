Latin

Praeter tria demonstrationis genera, quia, propter quid, et simpliciter, quae ponunt Aristoteles et commentator, datur quartum genus demonstrationis quae dici potest demonstratio conuertibilitatis, fortius omnibus praedictis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20984

English

Besides the three kinds of demonstration posited by Aristotle and the Commentator -- 'that' (quia), 'on account of which' (propter quid), and simple demonstration -- there is a fourth kind of demonstration, which can be called demonstration of convertibility, stronger than all those mentioned. unverified draft

'the Commentator' is the standard scholastic epithet for Averroes; left untranslated as a title (farmer1998:20984).
