Latin

Stat speciem cuius dicimur reminisci esse totaliter deperditam et abolitam.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11141

English

It is possible for the species of which we are said to have a memory to be entirely lost and destroyed. unverified draft

'species' here bears the sense 'image', specifically the image of God in the soul (Farmer's note, farmer1998:11166). Sense checked against farmer1998:11189.
