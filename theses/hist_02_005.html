Thomas Aquinas · thesis 2.5

Thesis 2.5

Latin

Quodcunque contingens deus sciuit esse futurum, necessario sciuit illud esse futurum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11321

English

Whatever contingent thing God knew would come to be, he knew of necessity that it would come to be.
