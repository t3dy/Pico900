Mohammed of Toledo · thesis 13.3

Thesis 13.3

Latin

Sensibilia communia proprias species ad sensus multiplicant, distinctas a speciebus sensibilium propriorum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14221; 1486 print folio <6r/6v>

English

The common sensibles send their own species to the senses, species distinct from those of the proper sensibles.

'sensibilia communia'/'sensibilium propriorum' kept as 'common sensibles'/'proper sensibles', the standard Aristotelian pair; cf. 10.3 and farmer1998:14035.
