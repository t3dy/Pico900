Thesis 3>54

Latin

Ad probandum primam materiam esse, magis certificat uel uia numerorum, uel uia catholicae philosophiae, quam ulla ratio physica in qua sensus habeat introitum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21090

English

Either the way of numbers or the way of universal philosophy gives a surer proof that prime matter exists than any physical argument in which the senses play a part.

'uia catholicae philosophiae' = the 'universal' philosophy, i.e. reasoning from cosmic correspondences, not a confessional sense; sense checked.
