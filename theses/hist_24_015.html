Latin

Per deorum cedes tragice a theologis septima unitas discretiua intellectualis ebdomadae designatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16157

The German-language secondary work cited here as 'kabbalah_paradigma' quotes the thesis with the verb 'designantur' (plural) rather than Farmer's printed 'designatur' (singular) (kabbalah_paradigma:1868); this variant is not otherwise explained in the material consulted.

English

Through the slaughters of the gods, the theologians tragically signify the seventh, distinguishing unity of the intellectual hebdomad. unverified draft

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch9, from Latin at farmer1998:16157

Attribution: Reports Proclus (farmer1998:16157). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer's note identifies the 'theologians' as the Orphic theologians, and glosses the 'seventh distinguishing unity' as the last of the seven principal henads in what Proclus calls the intellectual hebdomad (farmer1998:16194).
