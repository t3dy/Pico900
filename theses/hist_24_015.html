Proclus · thesis 24.15

Thesis 24.15

Latin

Per deorum cedes tragice a theologis septima unitas discretiua intellectualis ebdomadae designatur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16157

English

Through the tragic slaying of the gods, the theologians indicate the seventh unity of the intellectual hebdomad, the one that discriminates.
