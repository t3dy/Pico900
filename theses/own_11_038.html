Latin

Effectus qui sunt sequuti post mortem Christi debent conuincere quemlibet Cabalistam quod Iesus Nazarenus fuit uerus Messias.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27222

English

Every Cabalist ought to be convinced, by what followed upon Christ's death, that Jesus of Nazareth was the true Messiah. unverified draft

'The effects' picks out the eclipse and the torn temple veil that Farmer's note lists from Luke 23:44-46 and Matthew 27:45-54 (farmer1998:27256).
