Latin

Effectus qui sunt sequuti post mortem Christi debent conuincere quemlibet Cabalistam quod Iesus Nazarenus fuit uerus Messias.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27222

English

The effects that followed after Christ's death ought to convince any Cabalist that Jesus of Nazareth was the true Messiah. unverified draft

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch19, from Latin at farmer1998:27222

Attribution: Pico's own opinion (farmer1998:27222). · Pico's stance: endorses

Doctrine

Farmer's note identifies 'the effects that followed the death of Christ' as the eclipse of the sun and the tearing of the Temple veil (Luke 23:44-46, Mark 15:33-39), adding that Matthew 27:45-54 further reports an earthquake and the rising of the dead, and that Pico returns to the theme in the Heptaplus's second preface (farmer1998:27256).

Historiography

The Erkenntnistheorie study groups this thesis with 8>9 and 11>46 under a discussion of the Temple veil, quoting the Latin of 11>46 in full and citing Pompeo Faracovi's study of 'il tema dell'eclissi' (the theme of the eclipse) for further background (kabbalah_paradigma:2122).
