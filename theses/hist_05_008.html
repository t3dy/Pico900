Latin

Irascibilis et concupiscibilis ita distinguuntur in appetitu superiori sicut in

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12505

The transcription at farmer1998:12505 breaks off after 'sicut in', omitting the final word. Edelheit's quotation of the printed text gives the complete reading: 'Irascibilis et concupiscibilis ita distinguuntur in appetitu superi- ori sicut in inferiori.' (edelheit2022:15657).

English

The irascible and the concupiscible are distinguished in the higher appetite in the same way as in the lower. unverified draft

Doctrine

Edelheit reports that the thesis holds that the irascible and concupiscent powers are divided in the same way in both the superior and the inferior appetite, and that the question is discussed at Henry's Quodlibet VIII, question 15, on whether the rational appetite distinguishes irascible from concupiscent power (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15631). Farmer's note cross-references his note to thesis 2.11 on the 'irascible' and 'sensual' passions as two broad classes of emotion (paraphrase, farmer1998:12588).
