Latin

Irascibilis et concupiscibilis ita distinguuntur in appetitu superiori sicut in inferiori.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12505

English

The irascible and concupiscible powers are as distinct within the higher appetite as they are within the lower. unverified draft

'irascible'/'concupiscible': the two broad classes of passions, the first covering fear, hope, anger and the like, the second sensual desire (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12588). Sense checked against farmer1998:12571.
