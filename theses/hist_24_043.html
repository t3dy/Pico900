English

By way of intellect, love and faith we climb to the Lord who is wise, beautiful and good.

The Latin (farmer1998:16606) reads 'Ad sapiens, pulchrum, bonumque dominum', with the participle 'sapiens' in an unexpected form; rendered as three adjectives on 'dominum'. Farmer's note ties 24.43-44 to Theologia Platonica 4 and to the series starting at 2.12 (farmer1998:16679).
