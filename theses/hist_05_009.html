Latin

Habere aliquiditatiuam et diffinibilem realitatem commune est figmentis et non figmentis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12603

English

To have a quidditative and definable reality is common to both figments and non-figments. unverified draft

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch7, from Latin at farmer1998:12603

Attribution: Reports Henry of Ghent (farmer1998:12603; edelheit2022:15675). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer's note directs the reader to theses 4.7-8 for an overview of the conflict between Scotus and Henry on this point, and records a strong link to thesis 3.5 from Francis of Meyronnes (paraphrase, farmer1998:12623). Edelheit reports that the thesis holds that having some specific and definable reality is common to both the imaginary and the non-imaginary, and connects it forward to his discussion of thesis 11 on relation (paraphrase, edelheit2022:15675).
