Ammonius · thesis 16.3

Thesis 16.3

Latin

Cum dicit Aristoteles quod oportet prima principia semper permanere, nihil aliud intendit nisi quod in qualibet transmutatione reperiuntur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14482

English

When Aristotle says that the first principles must always persist, he means nothing more than what is found present in every change.

Sense checked against farmer1998:14517; Farmer's note (farmer1998:14528) glosses 'what is found in every transmutation' as prime matter.
