Latin

Cum dicit Aristoteles quod oportet prima principia semper permanere, nihil aliud intendit nisi quod in qualibet transmutatione reperiuntur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14482

English

When Aristotle says that first principles must always remain, he means nothing other than that they are found present in every change. unverified draft
