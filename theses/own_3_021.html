Per praedictas conclusiones intelligi potest quae sit omiomeria Anaxagorae, quam opifex intellectus distinguit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20551

English

The foregoing conclusions make it possible to grasp what Anaxagoras's homoiomereia is, the mixture that the intellect, as craftsman, sorts into distinct things. unverified draft
