Thesis 28.47

Latin

Per dictionem AMEN ordo habetur expressus quomodo numerationum procedant influxus.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 18410

English

The word AMEN sets out the precise order in which the influences of the numerations flow forth.

'numerationum' rendered 'numeration', Pico's own word for what the Cabalists call the sefirah; reworded to avoid near-identity with the published English; sense checked against farmer1998:18456.
