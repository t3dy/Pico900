Mohammed of Toledo · thesis 13.2

Thesis 13.2

Latin

Species rerum representatiue sunt reductiue in praedicamento in quo sunt res representate.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 14217

English

The representative species of things belong, by reduction, to the same category as the things they represent.

'species representatiue' kept as 'representative species', the scholastic term for the cognitive likeness of a thing; cf. farmer1998:14279.
