Thesis 5.12

Latin

Ad hoc, ut sit mutuitas realis relationis, requiritur quod fundamentum ex sua natura ordinetur ad aliud, tanquam ad suam perfectionem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12613

English

For there to be a real mutuality of relation, it is required that the foundation, by its own nature, be ordered toward another thing, as toward its own perfection. unverified draft

'Fundamentum': ground of relation. 'Mutuitas realis': real reciprocity.

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-swarm-batch5, from Latin at farmer1998:12613

Attribution: Reports Henry of Ghent (farmer1998:12613). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer's note places the thesis within a series on relation that begins at 4.16-17 and continues through 5.11-13 (farmer1998:12655). Edelheit states that 'theses 11, 12 and 13 deal with Henry's notion of relation, a crucial point in Henry's thought which can be found in many different parts of his works' (edelheit2022:15721). He traces thesis 12 to Henry's Quodlibet XIV, q. 2, where Henry holds that real relations require a real diversity in their foundations, so that distinct relations are not similar according to the same measure and thing measured (edelheit2022:15877), and to Quodlibet III, q. 4, where Henry identifies a relation taken 'per se and according to existence' with 'order' (ordo) — the power to found or constitute which thesis 12 attributes to the foundation (edelheit2022:15883).
