Henry of Ghent · thesis 5.12

Thesis 5.12

Latin

Ad hoc, ut sit mutuitas realis relationis, requiritur quod fundamentum ex sua natura ordinetur ad aliud, tanquam ad suam perfectionem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12613

English

For a relation's mutuality to be real, its ground must by its own nature be ordered toward something else, as toward its own perfection.
