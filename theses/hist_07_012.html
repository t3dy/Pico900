Averroes · thesis 7.12

Thesis 7.12

Latin

Grauia et leuia mouent se per accidens mouendo medium per se.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 13055

English

Only per accidens do heavy and light bodies move themselves, since it is the medium that they move per se.
