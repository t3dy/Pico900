Latin

Tenendo communem modum doctorum, quod scilicet de deo aliquid formaliter praedicetur, dico duas conclusiones proximas, quarum haec est prima: quod solus deus ita est substantia, quod nullo modo est non substantia.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 19652

English

Holding to the common way of the doctors — namely, that something can be formally predicated of God — I state the next two conclusions, of which this is the first: that God alone is substance in such a way that in no respect is he not substance. unverified draft

Farmer's note reads this and 2>48 as adopting the doctors' premise only provisionally, to draw a conclusion Pico would otherwise deny; pico_stance is accordingly given as unstated rather than endorses.
