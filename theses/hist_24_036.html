Latin

Quatuor sunt exercitus iuniorum deorum. Primus habitat a primo coelo usque ad principium aeris; secundus inde usque ad dimidium aeris. Tertius inde usque ad terram.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16491

English

There are four hosts of the younger gods. The first dwells from the first heaven down to the beginning of the air; the second from there down to the middle of the air; the third from there down to the earth. unverified draft
