Latin

Rectius ad quinque suprascripta reducitur entium diuersitas quam ad decem quae Architas primum, deinde Aristoteles posuit, uel quinque quae ponit Plotinus, uel ad quatuor quae ponunt stoici.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20656

English

The diversity of beings is more correctly reduced to the five categories written above than to the ten which Archytas first, then Aristotle, posited, or the five which Plotinus posits, or the four which the Stoics posit. unverified draft
