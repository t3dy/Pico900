Latin

Quodlibet abstractum dependet a primo abstracto in triplici genere causae, formalis, finalis, et efficientis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12951

English

Any abstract substance depends on the first abstract substance under a threefold genus of cause: formal, final, and efficient. unverified draft

'Abstractum' renders the Averroist term for a separate, immaterial substance (an intelligence), not 'abstraction' in the modern sense (farmer1998:12951).
