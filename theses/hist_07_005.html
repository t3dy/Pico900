Averroes · thesis 7.5

Thesis 7.5

Latin

Quodlibet abstractum dependet a primo abstracto in triplici genere causae, formalis, finalis, et efficientis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12951

English

Every abstract substance depends on the first abstract substance under a threefold kind of cause: formal, final, and efficient.

'abstractum' rendered 'abstract substance', following the sense given at farmer1998:13016 (for Averroes, God as the first abstract substance).
