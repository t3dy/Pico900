Latin

Sicut secundum causalitatis ambitum, ita secundum nature et gradus sublimitatem haec per ordinem se inuicem excedunt: Anima, Intellectus, Vita, Ens, et Vnum.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16695

The packet ends the text at 'Vita,'; 'Ens, et Vnum.' supplied from the next line of the OCR (farmer1998:16697).

English

As in the range of causality, so in the loftiness of nature and rank, these surpass one another in sequence: Soul, Intellect, Life, Being, and the One. unverified draft
