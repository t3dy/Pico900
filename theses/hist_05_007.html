Latin

Angeli intelligunt per habitum scientialem sibi connaturalem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12502

English

Angels understand by means of a habit of knowledge that is connatural to them. unverified draft

'habitus connaturalis': a habit created together with a nature but not part of its formal definition (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12580). Sense checked against farmer1998:12568.
