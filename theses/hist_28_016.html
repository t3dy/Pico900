Doctrine

The thesis holds that everyone before Moses prophesied 'through the hind-unicorn', a phrase Farmer traces to Flavius Mithridates' rendering of the Hebrew ayalah, 'hind', a symbol of the tenth sefirah, by the doublet 'cerva vel unicornis' (farmer1998:17885). On Farmer's reading, the thesis's point is that Moses' own prophecy drew on higher levels of the divine nature than the prophecy of his predecessors, a point Pico cross-references at thesis 28.45 (farmer1998:17885).

Historiography

Wirszubski calls the thesis 'a commonplace expressed in unusual language' and reads its point as setting Moses' directly inspired prophecy apart from what came before, in the passage consulted (wirszubski1989:1959).
