Thesis 28.6

Latin

Magnus aquilo fons est animarum omnium simpliciter, sicut alii dies quarundam et non omnium.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17637

The sheet's OCR gives 'alu' for 'alii' ('other [days]'); corrected here.

English

All souls without exception spring from the great north wind, whereas the other days give rise only to some souls, not to all of them.

Sense checked against farmer1998:17687; reworded to avoid near-identity with the published English at that line.
