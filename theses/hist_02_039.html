Latin

Phantasma est agens secundarium et instrumentale in productione speciei intelligibilis.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11878

English

The phantasm is a secondary and instrumental agent in the production of the intelligible species. unverified draft

'phantasma', per Farmer's note, denotes a sensory image (farmer1998:11904); 'species intelligibilis' is the scholastic term for the intelligible form abstracted by the agent intellect.

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch4, from Latin at farmer1998:11878

Attribution: Reports Thomas Aquinas (farmer1998:11878). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

Farmer glosses phantasma as a sensual image, placing the thesis within a series beginning at 1.1 on Thomistic epistemology of cognition (farmer1998:11904). Edelheit reads the thesis as holding that the phantasm plays only a secondary, instrumental role in producing the intelligible species, and observes that Pico here is 'more cautious than Thomas', preferring the probabilistic formula potius-quam, also used in the neighboring thesis 38, over a full commitment (edelheit2022:12566).

Historiography

Edelheit (2022) reads thesis 39 as carrying the immediately preceding theses on modes of motion into a parallel epistemological register, noting Pico's characteristically cautious, comparative phrasing (edelheit2022:12566).
