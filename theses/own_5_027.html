Cum dicit Plato Omne quod fit a causa fieri, referendum est per se ad per se, et per accidens ad per accidens.

Doctrine

The thesis states that, on Plato's view that everything that comes to be does so from a cause, the causal relation must be referred per se to per se and per accidens to per accidens (farmer1998:22651). Farmer reads it as a 'modal' interpretation of Timaeus 28a, in which 'what exists per se' names Pico's intellectual nature and 'what exists per accidens' names the lower levels of reality informed by soul and by reason (farmer1998:22685).
