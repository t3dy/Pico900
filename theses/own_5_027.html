Latin

Cum dicit Plato Omne quod fit a causa fieri, referendum est per se ad per se, et per accidens ad per accidens.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 22651

English

When Plato says that everything that comes to be, comes to be from a cause, this must be referred: what exists per se to per se causes, and what exists by accident to accidental causes. unverified draft
