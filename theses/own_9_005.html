Thesis 9>5

Latin

Nulla est uirtus in caelo aut in terra seminaliter et separata quam et actuare et unire magus non possit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 25190

The sheet's OCR text joined 'caelo' and 'aut' as 'caelo_aut'; restored as two words.

English

Whatever power lies in heaven or on earth in seed and in separation, the magician is able to bring into act and to join together.
