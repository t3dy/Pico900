Doctrine

Farmer's note explains that 'intension and remission of forms' in scholastic usage concerns alteration in the Aristotelian sense, an increase or decrease of accidental qualities over time (farmer1998:11066). The note reports that Pico's views on such questions were conservative, and cross-refers the thesis to his complaint, among his own theses, about the mathematical treatment of such matters by the moderni (farmer1998:11066).

Historiography

Edelheit's chapter reports that thesis 5 holds that form does not change in intensity and remission according to essence, but according to existence, and sets this beside Albert's definition of form in his commentary on the Physics (edelheit2022:9304).
