English

Wherever Scripture mentions the love of male and female, the conjunction of Tiferet and Keneset Israel, or of Beth and Tiferet, is mystically signified for us. unverified draft

Doctrine

The thesis treats every scriptural mention of the love between male and female as a mystical sign of the conjunction of two sefirotic pairs, Tiferet and Keneset Israel, or Beit and Tiferet, applying the standard kabbalistic association of male and female with superior and inferior sefirot, whose mating symbolizes mystical or eschatological union, or the original creation of the world through the sefirot (farmer1998:17923). Farmer's further note groups this thesis with theses 28.42-43 among those he traces to Recanati's commentary, and states that no evidence supports Wirszubski's claim that Pico instead used Mithridates' translation of that work, since it was not completed until after Pico's Conclusiones were published (farmer1998:18383).

Historiography

A study of Pico's Kabbalistic epistemology quotes the Latin of this conclusion together with thesis 28.28 and thesis 11>53, in a discussion of Wirszubski's account of Pico's sources (kabbalah_paradigma:1826). Wirszubski quotes the same Latin as the seventeenth conclusion of the Cabalistic set and compares it to Recanati's commentary (wirszubski1989:2005, 2008).
