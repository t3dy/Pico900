Latin

Per eandem conclusionem sciri potest quod idem filius, qui est sapientia patris, est qui omnia unit in patre, et per quem omnia facta sunt, et a quo omnia conuertuntur, et in quo demum sabbatizant omnia.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 27736

English

The same conclusion lets us know that this Son, the Wisdom of the Father, is the one who joins all things in the Father, through whom all things were made, by whom all things are turned back, and in whom, finally, all things come to rest. unverified draft

'Wisdom of the Father' echoes 1 Cor. 1:25; Farmer's note reads the four clauses as matched to the four terms generated in 11>59, and links 'sabbatize' to Christ as in 11>16 and 11>18 (farmer1998:27764).
