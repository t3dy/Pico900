Latin

Intuitus diuinae cognitionis ad creaturas tanquam obiecta primaria uel secundaria formaliter non terminatur, ut dicit communis schola theologorum, sed se tantum et nihil aliud a se intuens primarie nec secundarie, unitiue et eminenter, et plus quam aequipollenter, omnia cognoscit.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 21559

Apparatus (Farmer): 1486 tantum.& nihil | 1486 primariae nec secundariae | 1487 primarie nec secundane

English

The gaze of divine knowledge is not formally terminated upon creatures as primary or secondary objects, as the common school of theologians says; rather, beholding only itself and nothing other than itself, neither primarily nor secondarily, it knows all things unitively and eminently, and more than equivalently. unverified draft
