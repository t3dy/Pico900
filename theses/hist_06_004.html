Latin

Pater et filius non solum duo spirantes, sed etiam duo spiratores dici possunt.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 12682

English

The Father and the Son may be called not only two spirating Persons, but two spirators as such. unverified draft

'spirantes'/'spiratores': the participial and substantive forms of 'to spirate' (breathe forth) the Holy Spirit; Thomas accepted the former but not the latter, fearing it implied too sharp a distinction of Persons (Farmer's note, farmer1998:12761). Sense checked against farmer1998:12741.
