Proclus · thesis 24.13

Thesis 24.13

Latin

Secunda trinitas ebdomadis intellectualis est trinitas curetum, quos uocat theologia intemeratos deos.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16073

Source prints 'intellectualisest' without a word space; corrected to 'intellectualis est' (farmer1998:16073).

English

The Curetes form the second triad of the intellectual hebdomad; theology names them the unpolluted gods.

Sense checked against farmer1998:16128; 'curetum' kept as the proper name 'Curetes' rather than Farmer's descriptive 'guardians'. 'trinitas' rendered 'triad'.
