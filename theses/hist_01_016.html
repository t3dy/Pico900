Albert the Great · thesis 1.16

Thesis 1.16

Latin

Potentia respectiua materiae non addit supra materiam rem, sed rationem.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11159

English

A relative potency in matter adds to matter not a further thing, but only a further concept.

'res'/'ratio' (thing/concept) mark the standard scholastic distinction between a real addition and a merely conceptual one (Farmer's note, farmer1998:11224). Sense checked against farmer1998:11207.
