Proclus · thesis 24.52

Thesis 24.52

Latin

Per supercoelestem locum habemus intelligere quod de secunda trinitate plus est intelligibile quam intellectuale; per subcoelestem concauitatem quod magis intellectuale quam intelligibile. Per coelum id quod aeque utroque participat.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 16722; 1486 print folio <11r/11v>

OCR line-break 'secun/da' (farmer1998:16722) joined to 'secunda'; the folio mark '<11r/11v>' dropped.

English

'Supercelestial place' is for us to take as what, within the second triad, is intelligible more than intellectual; 'subcelestial hollow' as what is intellectual more than intelligible; 'heaven' as what takes part in the two alike.

The Latin 'quod de secunda trinitate plus est intelligibile' is elliptical; 'that part of' is supplied. The apparatus marker '<11r/11v>' is omitted (farmer1998:16725).
