Thesis 3>1

Latin

Sicut esse proprietatum praeceditur ab esse quiditatiuo, ita esse quiditatiuum praeceditur ab esse uniali.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20265

English

The existence of properties is preceded by quidditative existence, in the same way that quidditative existence is itself preceded by unial existence.

'esse uniale' ('unial existence') is Pico's own coinage for the mode of being proper to primordial unity; sense checked, no further crux.
