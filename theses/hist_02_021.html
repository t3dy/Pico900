Latin

Non est sub eadem specie angelorum plurificatio.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 11543

English

There is no plurification of angels under one and the same species. unverified draft

'Plurificatio' names the multiplication of individuals within a species; Farmer's note explains that Thomas's commitment to angels' total immateriality, together with matter as the principle of individuation (thesis 2.26), made each angel necessarily its own species (farmer1998:11623).

Translation: original translation, drafted 2026-09-26 by WRITER-full-batch3, from Latin at farmer1998:11543

Attribution: Reports Thomas Aquinas (farmer1998:11543). · Pico's stance: reports

Doctrine

The thesis denies that angels can be multiplied as individuals within one species (edelheit2022:11340). Edelheit locates the discussion in Thomas's commentary on the Sentences, book 2, distinction 3, question 1, article 4, which reasons that, angels being immaterial and no form or nature multiplying number except through diversity of matter, an immaterial form not received in matter must be unique (edelheit2022:11340; edelheit2022:11370). Farmer's note adds that Pico rejected the Thomistic premise on grounds of cosmic correspondence, holding that matter existed in the angelic nature no less than in lower natures, and that he planned to settle the underlying question of matter's uniformity across levels of reality at thesis 2>68 (farmer1998:11623).

unverified draft

Context

Farmer records that Pico had already postponed the question of matter's uniformity in the Commento (Garin, Scritti vari, 472), and planned to argue the same question 'proportionally' at a further passage the note does not fully specify in the excerpt consulted (farmer1998:11623).

Reception

Edelheit states that this thesis is among those criticized by the Florentine physician Bernardo Torni, and that he discusses it again, with further detail, in a later chapter of his book (edelheit2022:11340).

Historiography

Edelheit is the only scholar found discussing this thesis in the sources consulted; he situates it within Torni's criticisms of Pico and, on the doctrinal point itself, traces its reasoning to Thomas's Sentences commentary (edelheit2022:11340).
