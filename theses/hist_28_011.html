Hebrew Cabalists · thesis 28.11

Thesis 28.11

Latin

Dictum est ex Heden exire fluuium qui diuiditur in quatuor capita, ad significandum quod ex secunda numeratione procedit tertia, quae in quartam, quintam, sextam, et decimam diuiditur.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 17732

English

Scripture says a river leaves Eden and splits into four heads; this signifies that the third numeration proceeds from the second, and itself divides into the fourth, fifth, sixth, and tenth.

'numeratio' rendered 'numeration' throughout this edition, Pico's own word for what the Cabalists call the sefirah (cf. farmer1998:17811); reworded to avoid near-identity with the published English; sense checked against farmer1998:17791.
