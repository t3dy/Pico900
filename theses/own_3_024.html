Latin

Non potest dici quod in intellectu ideae, uerbi gratia, ignis, aquae, et aeris sint tres ideae, sed oportet dicere quod sunt ternarius.

Copy-text: Farmer 1998, line 20639

English

It cannot be said that in the intellect the ideas, of fire, water, and air for example, are three ideas; rather, it must be said that they are a ternary. unverified draft
