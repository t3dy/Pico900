## Key Avicennist Philosophical Themes

The 12 conclusions cluster around four major themes:

### 1. **Logic & Reasoning** (I.8.1–I.8.2, I.8.12)
- Expansion of Aristotelian logic to composite syllogisms
- Flexibility in modal logic enabling prophecy and future knowledge
- Rigorous but non-standard reasoning for occult phenomena

### 2. **Natural Philosophy & Cosmology** (I.8.3, I.8.5, I.8.9–I.8.11)
- Universal hylomorphism (all things = matter + form)
- Celestial matter identical to terrestrial (legitimizes astral influence)
- Spontaneous generation (supports alchemical transformation)
- Perception physiology grounded in real causal influence

### 3. **Metaphysics** (I.8.6–I.8.8)
- Essence/existence distinction (central to Pico's Q1, Q4 on divine embodiment)
- First Cause and causal hierarchy
- Emanation principle (One produces One)

### 4. **Epistemology & Soul** (I.8.4)
- Knowledge as dynamic actualization (not innate)
- Soul's progressive conjunction with Active Intellect
- Foundation for mystical ascent and prophetic illumination
