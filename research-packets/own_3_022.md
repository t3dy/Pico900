**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:20605

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:20622: 3221-22. “homoeomeria” = the original homogeneous state of things in Anaxagoras, the Western prototype of the principle that “all things exist in all things in their own mode.” Pico’s reference to the demiurge in 3>21 and his phrasing in 3>22 suggests that he was drawing from some Neo-Platonizing commentary on Aristotle Physics 3.4 (203220ff.).
