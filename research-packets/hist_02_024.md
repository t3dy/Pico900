## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:11680: 2.24. On the “being” and “one” issue in general, see above, pp. 25-29.
- farmer1998:11682: 2.24. Starts a complex series on the substance/accident distinction. A propria passio = a property that is not part of the essence or formal definition of a subject or substance; it nevertheless belongs “inseparably” to that subject since it is in that subject alone that the property exists (a standard example was “snubness” in noses).
