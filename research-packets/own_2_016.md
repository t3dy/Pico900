## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:19122: 2>16. Treatise on Supposition = the first of the seven treatises in De proprietatis terminorum, the last of the seven tracts in the standard medieval textbook in logic, Peter of Spain’s (Pope John XXI’s) Summulae logicales (mid-thirteenth century). Pico himself occasionally used the language of “supposition theory,” illustrated in 4>10 and note, but on the whole he viewed breaks with traditional Anstotelian logic (other than his own) with disdain. C£, e.g., 2>60. 2>17-18.
