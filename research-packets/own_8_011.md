**1486 apparatus (Farmer):** 1486 ne ex eas | 1486 trasit | 1487 omits blank space (farmer1998:24964)

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:25001

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:25017: 8>11. Cf. Exod. 12:1ff. A space exists here in the editio princeps—it is omitted in all later editions—which was apparently left for insertion of an Aramaic word (see Plate 5). It is not likely that the space arose from sloppy typesetting, since no similar end-of-page breaks exist anywhere else in the 1486 edition. Assuming that this space was left intentionally, as seems to be the case, we have prima facie evidence that Pico did have some kind of Aramaic text in hand when he compiled this section—for reasons discussed earlier, most likely a forgery by Mithri- dates.
