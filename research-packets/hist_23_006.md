## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:15750):** Cum excellenter ad intellectum assimilatur anima, fit in uehiculo motus perfecte circularis.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:15796

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:15816: 23.6. Drawn from Proclus In Timaeum (Diehl 2:72). On the “vehicle” or Neo-Platonic “body” of the soul, see note 5>45. The thesis is evidently meant to clash with 20.11 from Plotinus.

## Farmer's cross-references (his 'Cf.' network; Latin is public domain)

- **22.4** (Porphyry, farmer1998:15448): Omnis anima participans uulcanio intellectu seminatur in lunam.
