## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:18952: 2>3. “exists anywhere subjectively” = possesses existence (esse) anywhere in a metaphysical subject. The idea is that both concrete and abstract concepts of entities are “beings of reason.” The implied nominalism here is clarified in 3>2-7. Cf. also 2>5-6.
