**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:16012

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:15984: 24.4. Drawn from Theologia Platonica 4 (p. 209). "second trinity" = the “intelligible-intellectual order." Pico here associates the second henad or “unity” of that order with a phrase from the charioteer myth, Phaedrus 246a ff. For Ficino's later reading of that myth, see Allen (1981).
