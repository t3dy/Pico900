## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:27102: 11>32. Cf. Gen. 49:10 (spoken by Jacob), Prov. 8:22, Gen. 1:2. Pico here is correlating three unconnected passages from the Old Testament to read out their collective Christian message. On this syncretic technique, see above, pp. 67-68. As Wirszubski (1989: 177) puts it, each alef in these texts “is no more than a kind of code number or punched card by means of which three different verses are linked, each carrying latent Messiological connotations.”
