## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:15643):** Opifex sensibilis mundi septimus est hierarchiae intellectualis.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:15702

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:15720: 23.2. Cf. Proclus In Timaeum (Diehl 1:308). Some related theses are listed in note 22.1-3. “intellectual hierarchy” = one of the henadic orders in Proclus (see opening note to next sec- tion). Saffrey and Westerink (1978: 3:ix ff.) claim that the henads originated with Proclus's mas- ter Syrianus, but cf. Dodds (1963: 346) and Dillon (1973: 412), who trace them back much earlier. Dillon, like Pico, finds evidence of the henads in Iamblichus.
