## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:23354):** Cum dixit Abucaten Auenan animam esse supra tempus, intelligendum est de anima absoluta secundum substantiam, praescindendo ab omni operatione quae competit ei inquantum est anima.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:23395

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:23424: 6>1. “soul detached in substance” = presumably the unparticipated or world soul. Cf. 23.9 from Iamblichus. This thesis attempts to reconcile the language of the Book of Causes with the views Pico supports in 5>37-40. Other theses on duration are listed in note 2.18.
