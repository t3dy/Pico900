## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:23167: 5253. On linguistic realism. Cf, e.g., Cratylus 383a—b, 387d. In the Apology (Opera, 175), Pico tells us that in the Cratylus Plato claimed that names had magical powers if they were "rightly imposed.” In general, for Pico, that meant in Hebrew or languages “closely derived” from it; cf. 28.33, 28.47, 2>80, 3>55, 9222, etc.
