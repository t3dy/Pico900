## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:13044):** Coelum est corpus simplex, non compositum ex materia et forma.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:13098

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:13116: 7.9. Cf. in this series 7.13, 7.40, 8.3, 14.1, 15.1, 23.4, 7a>10, and 11>67. The point of 7.9, drawn from Averroes De substantia orbis 1-2 (or from Elia del Medigo’s commentary on that work, which was written for Pico), is not to deny a material or formal element to the caelum but to claim that their fusion is too close for us to speak of them as separate entities. Cf. 2>55.
