## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:27858: 11765. On "AMEN," cf. 28.47. Pico evidently planned to demonstrate the interconvertibility of AMEN, Tiferet (the sixth sefirah), and kingdom (the tenth sefirah) using some variation of gematna, as suggested by his reference to his “way of number.” Since Pico also correlated Tiferet, the “great Adam,” with Christ, his association of it here with AMEN would undoubtedly lead to secondary symbolic equations.
