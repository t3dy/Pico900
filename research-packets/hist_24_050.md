## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:16714):** Aeuum est supra per se animal in secunda et media trinitate.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:16772 (cumulative thesis number 320)

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:16792: 24.50. Pico’s interpretation of Theologia Platonica 3 (p. 148). “per se animal” = symbol of the third order of the “intelligible trinity” (cf. 24.38-42 and note). Aevum or “aeviturnity” = symbol in Proclus of the second main trinity of the “intelligible gods” as well as a durational state. Cf. the series beginning at 2.18.
