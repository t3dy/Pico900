## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:12220: 4.4. Cf. Scotus Ord. 3, d. 6, q. 1 (Wadding edition), and thesis 2.16 from Thomas and note. Since Chnst united two natures or distinct essences in one Person, did he possess two esse or existences as well? Pico planned to reconcile Scotists and Thomists on this problem in 1>5. Some clues to his approach can be gathered from the Apology, in Opera, 187.
