## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:13397):** Vnum methaphysicum est fundamentum unius arithmetici.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:13453

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:13471: 7.33. The first of a large number of theses involving the theory of numbers and proportions. Pico again included no theses on these topics from the Latins, whom he felt paid too little attention to the proportional structure of reality. Cf. especially, besides the previous thesis and the next, 2>64 from Pico’s own opinions.
