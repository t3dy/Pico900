## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:11214: 1.13. For Albert every sense had both an active and passive element, an idea that Pico found appealing due to the symmetry it suggested with the structure of higher cognitive powers. Re- lated historical theses, some in conflict, include 8.10, 10.3, 17.1, 17.7. Thesis 2>1 suggests that Pico believed that inferior universals were abstracted directly by the senses.
