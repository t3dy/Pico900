**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:12039

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:12052: 3.4. To predicate denominative (“through naming”) in this context = to predicate something accidentally or derivatively, as opposed to substantially; see Pico’s use of this term in Opera, 282. The thesis is opposed to 4.7 from Scotus.
