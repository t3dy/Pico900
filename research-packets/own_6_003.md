## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:23477: 6>3. In the series starting at 2.17. Presumably “every primary cause” = both God and the intellectual nature. For the latter, cf. the wording here with 3>25. The apparent goal of this thesis was to prepare the way for a reconciliation of emanationism with creationism by distinguishing different “modes” of causation. On this strategy, see above, pp. 20-21.
