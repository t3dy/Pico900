## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:15109):** Ciuiles uirtutes uirtutes simpliciter non sunt appellandae.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:15163

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:15180: 20.8. For other theses on the virtues, see note 2.11. Drawn from Enneads 1.2.1ff. The idea is that different "modes" of the virtues exist, but only those that link us to God are virtues simpliater—1.e., virtues in an absolute sense.
