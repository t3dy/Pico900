## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:18780: 1>13. Involves the question of whether or not dimensions exist in prime matter conceived independently of form. Cf. 7.16 from Averroes. In the Apology (above, p. 48) Pico charged Thomas with inconsistency on this issue.
