## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:11859: 2.36. Series starts at 2.24. Except in special cases like the Eucharist, Thomas argued that accidents drew their existence and unity from the substances in which they inhered. Hence to speak of two identical accidents in a single unifying subject implied a contradiction.
