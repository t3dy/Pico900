**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:20151

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:20128: 2272. P1co's series on metaphysical unity begins at 2.23. Pico ascribes the view affirmed here to Averroes in Heptaplus 3.2 (Opera, 25; Garin, Scritti vari, 254). Anyone who doubts the claim made in Pico's thesis may not be a philosopher, but he may be a theologian, since theologians posit a mulutude of angels, henads, etc., in the intellectual nature. See my discussion of the double-truth referred to in the previous note.
