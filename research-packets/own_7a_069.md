## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:24686: 7a>69. Re commentary on Peter Lombard Sentences 2, d. 15. In the Apology, in Opera, 174, Pico sings a paean to the number seven, one “worthy of great attention because the Lord rested on the seventh day, because of the seven gifts of the Holy Spint, the seven seals in Revelation, the seven trumpets, seven eyes, seven angels, seven churches, seven stars, seven evil spints, seven vices in Solomon, and seven days that the prophet David sang to God.” The number seven as a symbol of the final “Sabbath” of the soul or of history also crops up regularly in the theses and in the Heptaplus. Cf. 5>58, 11>16, etc.
