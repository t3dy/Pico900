## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:25981: 10>18. The “aquatic soul” = the world soul, which “informs” the lower world with the reflec- tions of ideas it receives from the intellectual nature or angelic mind. For the style of this thesis, cf. 24.39X. from Proclus, which involve similar emanational principles.
