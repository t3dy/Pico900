## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:17985: 28.20. “mirror not shining" /"shining mirror" = common symbols of the tenth and sixth sefirot. "Night will be just like day" = Psalm 139:12. The eschatological sense of this thesis is reinforced in 11254, especially when we recall Pico's regular association of Christ with the sixth sefirah. The passages from R ecanati cited by Wirszubski help us interpret Pico’s text, but they cannot be claimed as Pico's direct sources.
