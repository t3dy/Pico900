**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:25330

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:25337: 9>15. Given the fact that Pico distinguished a number of different types of magic and of Cabala (see above, pp. 126-28), it is not possible to know for certain his meaning here. In general, I take it that Pico’s primary sense is that since the rational part of the soul (which is pertinent to “natural magic”) derives from the intellectual part of the soul (which is pertinent to “pure Cabala”), Cabala is implicitin any magical act. Pico's view that certain parts of Cabala drew on powers higher than those of natural magic is also affirmed in the next three theses. For still another link between magia and Cabala, see 9>25.
