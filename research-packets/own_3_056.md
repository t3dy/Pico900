## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:21199: 3>56. On distinctions in general, see note 3.7. Pico’s distinctio inadequationis can be interpreted via theses 3>2-7, which suggest that distinctions that are deemed "adequate" objects of know- ledge for lower cognitive faculties might be "inadequate" for higher ones. In 2>66 and 3>58, Pico reinterprets the Scotist formal distinction in this framework.
