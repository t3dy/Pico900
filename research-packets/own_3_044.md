## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:20957: 3244. Reference to Metaphysics 12.10, meant to "save" Aristotle from problematic passages in Metaphysics 12.8-9 that refer in the plural to “unmoved movers” (implying polytheism) or that deal with the limits of divine thought. Pico would typically have claimed that the real subjects of these passages were “intelligences” or “celestial souls” and not God. Cf. 18.4-5 and notes.
