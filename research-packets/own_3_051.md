## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:21110: 3750-51. See 16.3 and note. Privation is rejected as a natural principle since it implies a sharp break in the proportions of the universe (see the next thesis). Pico hence posits that matter exists in some “mode” on every level of reality. Pico’s rejection (on the grounds of cosmic correspondence) of the idea of total immatenality constitutes one of his main breaks with the Platonic tradition and with Latin scholastics like Thomas Aquinas.
