## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:17923: 28.17. The association of male and female with superior and inferior sefirot was a kabbalistic commonplace; their mating was viewed as a symbol of mystical and/or eschatological union or the original creation of the world through the sefirot. “ Tiferet" /' Keneset Israel” = name/symbol
- farmer1998:18383: 28.17. On Binah as the "great" or "superior" jubilee, see further Scholem (1974: 120). Like other theses ın this section, theses 28.42-43 derive from Recanati’s commentary, but no evi- dence exists for Wirszubski’s claim (1989: 50) that Pico was using Mithndates’ translation of the work, which was not completed until after Pico’s text was published.
