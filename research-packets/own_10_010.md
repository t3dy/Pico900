**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:25837

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:25853: 10>10. “fear of Isaac” (Gen. 31:42) = standard kabbalistic symbol of the fifth sefirah, one of whose alternate names was Gevurah or “power.” Combining this with the previous thesis, we find that “guardians” in Orpheus, “powers” in Dionysius, and Gevurah in the Kabbalah are the same. It 1s not clear what “the work of the preceding conclusion” refers to in 10>10. Was mystical exegesis the only thing at stake here? Since the fifth sefirah is also associated with magic in 28.40 and 11>47 (see notes), it appears as if Pico believed that these powers could be called down in some fashion. This was very dangerous material to be debating at Rome.
