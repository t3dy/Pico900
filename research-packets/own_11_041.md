## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:27307: 11>41. “mystery of the closed mem” = reference to the fact that mem has both open (N) and closed (D) forms, the latter used at the end of a word. Pico apparently intended to read out the letter symbolism of the closed mem through the means suggested in 28.33. It was a common- place in Hebrew commentary traditions that the closed mem showed up only once in the Hebrew bible in a nonfinal position (Isa. 9:6); the verse has a strong Messianic character and was much discussed by both Jewish and Christian commentators. “‘Paraclete” = the Holy Spirit.
