## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:26153: 10227. “third Jove as living, not as vivifying” = cf. the phrasing in 24.24. The idea is that in its mystic state the soul reaches the “first form" (the total intellect) viewed transcendentally and not as it functions as the source of forms in the lower world. On the foundations of this distinction, cf., e.g., 3719 and note.
