**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:14775

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:14753: 18.1. Cf. especially 7.4, 17.9. Revolves again around De anima 3.5. Pico reverses the usual reading of Alexander's views as reported by Averroes. Pico was possibly drawing from the spurious second book of Alexander's commentary on De anima, including the section entitled De intellectu. For an analysis of a later scholastic conflict on this topic at the University of Padua involving Nicoletto Vernia and Agostino Nifo, both of whom were linked to Pico, see Maho- ney (1969).
