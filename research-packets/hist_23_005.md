**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:15792

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:15774: 23.5. Kieszkowski, followed by Biondi, fills in the blanks from Proclus’s Elements of Theology with the impossible triad Sv, ζωή, νοῦς (“being,” “life,” “intellect”)— valid concepts in later Greek Neo-Platonism, as we see in 20.6 and 24.46, but clearly not the three worlds intended here. Pico was again probably drawing from book 1 of De mystenis—the “theologians” are pre- sumably the Egyptian, Chaldean, and Assyrian priests discussed in that work— where much space is spent distinguishing the higher realms of the gods from the middle world of demons
