## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:16622: 24.38—42. Systematizing material from Theologia Platonica 3 (pp. 171-72). “that which lives per se” = the “per se animal” = Proclus’s designation, interpreting the Timaeus, for the third order of the “intelligible trinity” (the “intelligible exemplar" in 24.18). As in 24.18, the "demiurge"
