## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:27603: 11>55. “seventh” = the seventh sefirah, corresponding to the seven ages of history, seven stages of the mystical ascent, etc. Thus in 11>66 we find that the function of the seventh sefirah as it is reflected in the soul is the conversion to “superior things.” The miraculous correspondence with Pythagorean arithmetic is clarified in 25.5, where we find that the number seven is a Pythagorean symbol for the “‘beatifying power of the intellect.”
