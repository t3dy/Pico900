## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:18101: 28.26. Onkelos = Onkelos the Proselyte, traditional author of the Aramaic version of the Torah known as the Targum Onkelos (early common era). He is mentioned often by Moses Maimonides and many other medieval sources known to Pico. "thirty-two paths of wisdom" = idea found in the proto-kabbalistic Sefer Yetzirah (Book of Creation) and later texts that God created the world using the combined magical power of the twenty-two letters of the Hebrew alphabet and ten sefirot. Cf. also 9>19ff. from Pico’s magical conclusions, 11258.
