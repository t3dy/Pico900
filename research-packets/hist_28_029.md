**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:18135

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:18155: 28.29. "kingdom of David" = the tenth sefirah. "mem tzade pe tzade" is generated when each letter in the "ineffable name” YHVH (normally associated with the sixth sefirah) is replaced by the corresponding letters when the Hebrew alphabet is reversed; this was one form of Pico's so- called revolutio alphabetariae. See Anagnine (1937: 160), Wirszubski (1989: 43—44).
