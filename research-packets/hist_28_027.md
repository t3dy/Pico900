## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:18145: 28.27. "eathenng of waters" (Prov. 10:25)/"the just” = symbol/alternate name of the ninth sefirah. “sea to which all rivers run" (Eccl. 1:7)/“divinity” = Shekhinah, “divine presence" = symbol/alternate name of the tenth sefirah. See further the text from Recanati quoted by Wir- szubski (1989: 42). The thesis symbolically correlates isolated texts from Hebrew Scripture just as Pico’s theses from Proclus (e.g., 24.8) correlate isolated texts from the Platonic corpus. The original object in both cases was to demonstrate the secret harmonies in those sacred texts.
