## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:11653):** Forma generatur per accidens.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:11709

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:11691: 2.25. Related to the previous thesis. Cf. Thomas’s Commentary on the Metaphysics 7.7, where we find that existence per se does not belong to forms, since forms are only found in composition with matter. Forms can thus only be said to be generated “accidentally.” C£ also 2.29, 2.43.
