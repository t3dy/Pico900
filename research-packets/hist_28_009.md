## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:17649):** Per sex dies geneseos habemus intelligere sex extremitates aedificii procedentes a Bresith, sicut procedunt cedri a Libano.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:17699

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:17712: 28.9. "edifice" = the lower seven sefirot (cf. 11248). In medieval sources, the “six extremities" normally refer to the fourth through ninth sefirot. “ Bereshit" = “in the beginning” (Gen. 1:1), a decontextualized biblical term normally associated with the second sefirah, Hokhmah, the wisdom by which God created the world. In his second set of Cabalistic theses, Pico predict- ably reads Hokhmah as a symbol for Christ, the second Person of the Trinity in Christianity and the “Wisdom of the Father." Cf. also 28.25.
- farmer1998:17760: 9. Din (judgment) 4. Hesed (love or piety in Pico)
- farmer1998:17766: 9. Yesod (foundation)
