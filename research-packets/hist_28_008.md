## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:17706: 28.8. “light”/“day” when found together in medieval Hebrew texts = symbols of the superior three and inferior seven sefirot. Thus the "third light" = Binah or "intelligence," which Pico most commonly associated with his intellectual nature. Wirszubski (1989: 26—29) gives a long argument based on Mithridates’ translations that Pico identified the “fifth day" here idiosyncrat- ically with the tenth sefirah. Further on Pico and the emanation of souls, cf. 28.6, 28.41.
