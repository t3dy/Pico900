## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:24972: 829. Cf. Gen. 3:21, Exod. 36:8ff., which were heavily glossed in the Middle Ages. The skins (Exod. 36:19) made up part of the veil in the tabernacle and were red rams’ skins—for Pico, as we find in his Cabalistic theses, a symbol of Christ. In Christian mythology, the veil was torn asunder on Christ’s death; the "skins" here are hence symbols of the Fall and Redemption. Cf.
