## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:13972):** Intentio prima est quiditas rei obiectiue relucens in intellectu.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:14014

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:14031: 10.2. “first intention” = concept referring a concrete object, as opposed to a "second intention” (like genus or species), usually said to arise from reflection on a “first intention.” Tied to the series On universals starting at 1.1. Pico rejects the realist views implied here in 2>2-3.
