**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:10985

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:11003: 1.2. Standard scholastic formula for affirming the independence of species and genera from individuals (explaining the reading of “destroyed” rather than “corrupted”). Biondi translates: "Essendo corrotti tutti gli individui della specie umana, é vera la proposizione: l'uomo € un essere animato," which destroys Pico's sense. The origin of the discussion goes back to Aristotle Metaphysics 7.14-15, which attack the Platonic theory of ideas. Conflicting histoncal theses include 2.45 and 7.19 from Thomas and Averroes. See also 2235 from Pico's theses. Pico be- lieved that from the limited standpoint of human knowledge it was legitimate to view species and genera as having independent existences, but viewed from a higher perspective, these were mere “beings of reason.” Cf. 2223, 322-7 and notes.
