## Text

**Latin (Farmer 1998, farmer1998:14562):** Eadem pars rationalis, ut extra se uadens et procedens perficitur speciebus quae in ipsa sunt, ut manens est dicitur intellectus in habitu.

**Farmer's English:** farmer1998:14619

## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:14633: 3.5. Tied to the series on mystical beatitude or happiness starting at 2.12. Pico presumably intended to resolve the apparent conflict between 7.3 (from Averroes) and 17.3 (from Simpli- cius) by pointing to the distinctions in 17.4—6.
