## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:11564: 2.17. The thesis pertains to emanationism, which Thomas viewed as heretical. Pico’s opposing view is discussed in the Apology (Opera, 189) and above, pp. 20-21. Related historical theses include 7.5, 8.7-8, 11.1, 13.5, 26.3. In 7a>7 and 7a>26 Pico planned to discuss different aspects of emanationism through his “way of numbers.”
