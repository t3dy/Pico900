## Farmer's notes (excerpts to paraphrase, not reproduce)

- farmer1998:18463: 28.46. Both texts that Wirszubski (1989: 51) proposes as potential sources emphasize the favorite Pichean theme that proportion exists between higher and lower realms, expressed here in an eschatological context. The misreading of ponitur for punitur in the 1487 reprint was followed by all later Renaissance editors of Pico’s text.
